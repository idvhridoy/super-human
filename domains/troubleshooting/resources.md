# Troubleshooting Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Problem-definition worksheets: expected vs observed, timeline of change
- Intro explainers on symptom vs cause and the reproduction principle
- One-variable-at-a-time experiment templates and control-test guides
- Household fault-log templates for tracking real breakages

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Bisection-method guides with worked examples (sequences, timelines, config chains)
- Root-cause analysis primers: 5-whys and fishbone/cause-mapping templates
- Fix-verify-document report templates with filled exemplars
- Basic device-troubleshooting checklists (power → connection → component order)

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Supervised repair resources: safe device-opening practices, anti-static basics, when to escalate to a technician
- Software debugging primers: reading stack traces, minimal reproducers, logging and breakpoint methods (pairs with `coding/`)
- Process/systems debugging case studies: handoff failures, broken workflows, plan post-mortems
- Intermittent-fault methods: observation windows, evidence logging, hypothesis trees
- Incident-review and blameless-post-mortem guides from engineering practice
- Capstone dossier templates: multi-domain fault reports plus a novice-facing "method card" format
