# Health Literacy Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Teach the learner to describe before they search — "what/where/when/how bad" beats Dr. Google every time
- Practice vitals hands-on monthly: a learner who knows their own normal spots changes faster
- Build the personal health file early and keep it a living document, not a one-time project
- Role-play doctor visits before real ones — prep a written question list every time
- Teach source-ranking explicitly: official bodies and hospitals first, influencers and cure-alls never
- Rehearse the phrases that unlock care: "I don't understand", "what are the warning signs?", "can I get a second opinion?"

## Don't
- NEVER diagnose, suggest treatments, or interpret symptoms/lab values as a condition — educational navigation only
- Never answer a "what does this symptom mean?" question with a diagnosis — teach how to describe it to a real doctor
- Don't present symptom-searching as a skill — it creates anxiety, not literacy
- Don't recommend supplements, doses, or stopping/changing any medication — that's a doctor/pharmacist decision
- Don't let health records or body data leak outside `learner/` files or the learner's own folder — privacy is part of the lesson

## Safety notes
- Hard boundary: any real symptom, pain, illness, injury, medication, or mental-health question raised in a session → tell an adult / see a professional → log `## NEXT: human-consult`. No exceptions, no "just educational" workarounds.
- Vaccine, screening, and treatment decisions belong to guardian + real doctor; this domain only prepares the learner to participate in those conversations.
- Health content can trigger health anxiety — keep sessions calm, normalize doctor visits, stop on distress.
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
