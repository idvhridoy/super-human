# Health Literacy Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — `@knowledge-librarian` selects specific items and files them in `library/`.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Illustrated human-body atlases and body-map worksheets for the learner's age band
- Age-appropriate "how my body works" book series and videos (organ systems tour)
- Simple home tools: thermometer, watch/phone timer for pulse counting
- Printable vital-signs baseline log sheet and normal-range chart by age
- "Meet the healthcare team" explainers: doctor, nurse, pharmacist, dentist roles

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Symptom-description worksheets and OPQRST-style question cards
- Sample/blank medicine labels and patient-information leaflets for reading practice
- Red-flag symptom checklists from recognized pediatric/health bodies
- Local healthcare-system explainers: clinic vs. hospital, public vs. private, referral flow (Bangladesh context)
- Appointment-booking role-play scripts and mock-visit checklists
- Prevention calendars and age-based check-up/screening guides from official health organizations

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Media-literacy guides for health misinformation from official health bodies
- Source-ranking exercises: real vs. misleading health content examples (curated, not live browsing)
- Personal health-record organizer templates (folder/divider or digital doc)
- Sample lab reports (anonymized/dummy) for structure-reading practice — reference range, units, fields
- Patient-advocacy guides: preparing questions, understanding consent, second opinions
- Family health-history tree worksheet (completed with guardian)
