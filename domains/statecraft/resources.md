# Statecraft Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Five-functions observation guide: decide / allocate / enforce / represent / plan — who does what in family, school, club, town
- Simple real budgets for analysis: school club accounts, municipal summaries, game economies
- Incentive-autopsy worksheet: stated goal → actual reward → observed behavior → unintended consequence
- Age-appropriate civics primers: how local government works, what a mayor/council/principal actually does
- `domains/finance/` money-basics material — collective-scale application lives here

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- *The Art of War* — annotated young-reader or standard translation with discussion guide
- *The Prince* — key chapters (XV–XVIII especially) with critical-reading guide and ethics discussion prompts
- Negotiation primers: interests vs positions, BATNA explained simply (*Getting to Yes* adapted exercises)
- Alliance/alliance-failure case pack: historical examples where commitments held or broke
- Simulation options: strategy/board games with real resource and alliance mechanics (Risk, Diplomacy-simplified, Civilization-style games — screen-time gated per `learner/schedule.md`)

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Institutional comparison case sets: succession, corruption control, dispute resolution across systems
- Legitimacy-crisis casebook: historical breakdowns analyzed by failed legitimacy source
- *The Dictator's Handbook* (select chapters, mentor-guided) — selectorate theory as power analysis
- Long-game planning template: 20-year horizon, compounding investments, succession branch, contingency branch
- Crisis simulation scenarios: escalating multi-round scripts with information asymmetry and time pressure
- Capstone governance scenario kit: simulated domain with budget, factions, external actors, shock events, scoring rubric (prosperity + legitimacy + ethical consistency)
- `domains/strategy/` decision frameworks and `domains/history/` case depth — referenced throughout
