# Team Sports Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Warm up every single session — 5–10 min light movement + dynamic stretches; no cold starts, no exceptions
- Hydrate before, during, and after; schedule water breaks into every session plan
- Build skill drills around team context: a pass always has a target and a reason
- Coach the calls early — communication habits form fast and stick
- Normalize errors with the next-play mindset: breath, clap, refocus — praise resets, not just goals
- Rotate positions so the learner experiences every role; empathy for teammates comes from having done their job
- Debrief every match briefly: one thing that worked, one thing to try next time

## Don't
- Don't play through pain, dizziness, or a head knock — stop-signal means stop, then adult → `@safety-guardian`
- Don't tolerate aggression disguised as competitiveness: no dangerous tackles, throws at bodies, or contact beyond age rules
- Don't over-drill skills without game play — team sports are learned in context, keep scrimmage time in every session
- Don't let sessions become lecture-only; talking happens between reps, not instead of them
- Don't assign a permanent position too early or bench-style sideline anyone — everyone plays, everyone touches the ball
- Don't frame losing as failure — review it as information; blame-culture ends sessions and teammates

## Safety notes (physical domain)
- Warm-up is mandatory and logged; skipping it voids the session. Cool-down + water after.
- Injury stop-signals: sharp pain, dizziness, head contact, twisted joint, or learner says "something feels wrong" → play stops immediately, tell an adult, flag `@safety-guardian`; session resumes only after clearance.
- Contact scaled to age group per `learner/profile.md`; no heading drills or hard-ball work below age-appropriate thresholds.
- Environment checks before play: safe surface, boundaries clear of roads/water/glass hazards, weather (heat/ lightning) acceptable.
- This is cooperative play — aggressive behavior from any participant pauses the session for a reset talk.
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes and can veto sessions on sleep-debt or injury flags.
