# Team Sports Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — `@knowledge-librarian` selects specific items and files them in `library/`.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Illustrated rules guides for kids/beginners: football and cricket (objective, positions, scoring)
- Short explainer videos: "how a football match works" and "how cricket works" for young learners
- Printable field/pitch diagrams for position-labeling practice
- Basic gear list: football, cricket bat + tennis/soft ball, cones/markers, water bottle
- Skill-isolation drill cards: wall passes, trap-and-pass pairs, catching grids, underarm throwing targets
- Dynamic warm-up routine card (5–10 min, age-appropriate)

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Passing/space drill libraries: keep-away formats, triangle passing, give-and-go patterns
- Field-communication cheat sheet: the standard calls and when to use them
- Cricket calling drills: run-call decision scenarios, fielder calling practice
- Small-sided game formats (3v3, 5v5, pairs cricket) that maximize touches and communication
- Teammate-behavior reflection sheets: what encouragement looks/sounds like; reset ritual cards
- Age-appropriate video examples of good spacing and field talk in real matches

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Tactics board / diagram templates for set-pieces and field placements
- Game-reading exercises: annotated match clips with pause-and-predict prompts
- Beginner tactical guides: formations basics, pressing vs. dropping, batting partnerships, bowling to a field
- Leadership rubrics: organizing a warm-up, captaining a segment, post-match debrief structure
- Match-organization checklist for the capstone: format, rules agreement, safety setup, fair officiating
- Local directories for school teams, academies, and community games (Bangladesh)
- Coaching-basics mini-guides for the teach-back requirement
