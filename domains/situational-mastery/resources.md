# Situational Mastery Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Illustrated awareness/noticing books for young learners (observation games, scene memory)
- Observe → orient loop explainers (simple diagrams, short videos)
- Room-read journal template: exits, people, mood, one surprise
- Baseline-vs-anomaly explainer cards
- Kim's game / spot-the-difference drill kits

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Group-dynamics primers (roles, subgroups, mood shifts) — age-appropriate
- Improv game collections: yes-and, constraint scenes, missing-prop challenges
- Composure drill cards: pause-breathe-label, body reset, time-buying phrases
- Surprise-scenario decks for supervised reps (spoiled plans, sudden questions)
- Escalation-sign checklists and ranking quizzes

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- De-escalation guides for young people: calm voice, distance, acknowledge-first, face-saving exits
- Crisis size-up checklist: scene safety, help vs hazard, who-to-call scripts
- Real-world case studies of calm handling under surprise (stories, mentor-reviewed news analysis)
- Cross-reference packs: `domains/first-aid/` basics, `domains/survival/` priorities — routing, not re-teaching
- Mentor-led scenario kits and debrief templates for live reps
- Debrief template: noticed → decided → worked → change next time
