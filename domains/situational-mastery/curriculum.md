# Situational Mastery Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. Scope: the EXTERNAL skill of reading live situations and handling them smoothly — awareness, room-reading, composure under surprise, improvisation, de-escalation, and crisis triage. Etiquette and emotional intelligence live in `domains/behavior/`; speaking/negotiation mechanics in `domains/communication/`; internal cognitive tactics (reframes, scripts, focus drills) in `domains/mind-hacks/` and `domains/mindfulness/`; wilderness emergencies in `domains/survival/`; medical skills in `domains/first-aid/`. This domain references them — never repeats them. All content filtered by learner age (`learner/profile.md`).

## L0 — Foundations
- Notice-on-purpose: the observe → orient loop (look around, make sense, then act)
- Baseline awareness: what a normal room/street/group looks and sounds like
- Describe-3-things habit: after leaving a place, name three details you noticed
- **Unlock:** runs the describe-3-things habit daily for a week; can explain observe → orient in own words

## L1
- Observation drills: remember a scene after leaving it (exits, count of people, weather, mood)
- Kim's game variants: spot-the-difference, memory tray, license-plate/room-detail recall
- Listening layer: overheard tone and rhythm as information, not eavesdropping on private matters
- **Unlock:** scores 80%+ on a supervised scene-recall drill; writes 5 room-reads in the journal

## L2 — Reading Rooms
- Group anatomy: who leads, who follows, who's left out, who's watching whom
- Mood reading: energy level, tension vs ease, a room's "temperature" before and after someone enters
- Flow of attention: where the group's focus is and why
- **Unlock:** after a real gathering, maps the group (leader, mood, subgroups) and a familiar adult verifies the read

## L3 — Social Dynamics in Motion
- Baseline vs anomaly: spotting what's unusual for THIS place and time (odd behavior, mood shift, sudden silence)
- Momentum: how situations build — small signs that predict a mood change, conflict, or excitement
- Pattern over panic: most anomalies are benign — verify before reacting
- **Unlock:** logs 5 anomaly-spotting entries (what was off, how verified, outcome) — at least 3 confirmed benign

## L4 — Composure Under Surprise
- The pause: 2-second stop + one breath before responding to anything unexpected
- Body reset: unclench, lower shoulders, slow the voice — look calm to think calm
- Buying time honestly: "give me a second", "say that again", asking a question back
- Internal tactics (reframes, self-talk scripts) delegated to `domains/mind-hacks/` — applied here, built there
- **Unlock:** 3 supervised surprise reps (spoiled plans, sudden questions, minor scares) handled without freezing or snapping; debrief logged

## L5 — Improvisation Under Constraints — Intermediate milestone
- Yes-and thinking: accept the changed situation, build on what's actually available
- Resourcefulness reps: substitute, repurpose, simplify — the plan broke, the goal stands
- Time-boxed choices: pick a workable option in 60 seconds, commit, adapt as you go
- **Unlock:** 3 constraint drills passed (missing item, broken plan, surprise rule) — completed the goal anyway, debriefed

## L6 — Conflict Awareness
- Escalation signs: rising volume, invading space, repetitive accusations, gathering audience
- Positioning: exits, distance, not getting cornered — awareness, never confrontation
- Your own trigger map: what makes YOU escalate; knowing it defuses it
- **Unlock:** passes a scenario quiz ranking 10 situations by escalation risk; names own top-3 triggers

## L7 — De-Escalation
- Calm projection: slow low voice, open posture, unhurried movements — the thermostat, not the thermometer
- Acknowledge first: let the other person feel heard before any problem-solving
- Face-saving exits: give people a way to back down without losing dignity
- Verbal technique depth belongs to `domains/communication/`; conduct rules to `domains/behavior/`
- **Unlock:** 3 mentor-led de-escalation role-plays passed (angry friend, unfair accusation, group argument) — role-play only, never practiced on real conflicts

## L8 — Crisis Triage Thinking
- Scene size-up: is it safe for me? what's the actual problem? who needs help first?
- Help vs hazard: what a kid/learner CAN do (call, guide, comfort, fetch) vs what needs adults/professionals
- Calling for help: who, how, what to say — the correct play IS getting help, never playing hero physically
- Domain routing: injuries → `domains/first-aid/` skills · wilderness → `domains/survival/` · emotional crisis → trusted adult + `## NEXT: human-consult`
- **Unlock:** crisis size-up theory exam passed; drill — describes correct response to 5 scenario cards including "call adults first" judgment calls

## L9 — Live Reps
- Planned disruption drills: mentor changes a plan mid-activity; learner recovers smoothly
- Real-world reps: handle checkout hiccups, wrong orders, changed schedules, lost items — logged and debriefed
- Multiple moving parts: stay oriented when two things go wrong at once
- **Unlock:** 5 real unexpected situations handled smoothly with logged debriefs (self-rated composure ≥4/5 on at least 3)

## L10 — Mastery
- Integrates the full arc: reads a room, senses the shift, stays composed, improvises, routes specialists when needed
- Anticipates: spots the surprise before it lands — positions, prepares, prevents
- Teaches the observe → orient loop and the pause to a peer or younger learner
- **Unlock:** capstone — a real unexpected situation (not staged) handled smoothly end-to-end, full written debrief (what was noticed, decided, what worked, what changes) + one taught session, verified by a mentor
