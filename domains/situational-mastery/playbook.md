# Situational Mastery Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Observe first, act second — the orient step is where smooth handling is born
- Learn the baseline before hunting anomalies — calm rooms have a look and a sound
- Pause-breathe-label when surprised; a 2-second pause reads as composure, not weakness
- Buy time honestly: "give me a second", ask a question back, take a sip of water
- Practice improvisation in low stakes daily — games, substitutions, plan changes
- Know exits and distances everywhere you sit; make it a quiet habit, not a show
- Debrief every real situation: what you noticed, what you decided, what you'd change
- Route to specialists: injuries → `domains/first-aid/`, wilderness → `domains/survival/`, etiquette → `domains/behavior/`, inner scripts → `domains/mind-hacks/`
- Rehearse de-escalation in role-play BEFORE needing it live — calm is a trained reflex

## Don't
- Never play hero physically — if there's danger or violence: distance, adults, call help. That IS mastery, not failure
- Don't practice de-escalation on real angry people — role-play and supervised settings only
- Don't escalate to win; the win condition is everyone calmer than you found them
- Don't confuse composure with silence — saying "I need a minute" is a correct move
- Don't treat observation as snooping — awareness of a room, not inventory of private matters
- Don't diagnose or treat — mental-health or medical situations → trusted adult + `## NEXT: human-consult`
- Don't narrate your reads aloud to impress people — quiet awareness, smooth action
- Don't fake readiness — being out of depth and calling for help is always the right play

## Safety notes
- Physical danger hard rule: learner's job is distance + adults + emergency contact — never intervene physically. `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
- All live reps supervised; role-play partners are mentors/trusted people only
- Any real emergency encountered → `## NEXT: human-consult` immediately
- Content age-gated per `learner/profile.md`; crisis scenarios introduced gently, theory-first
