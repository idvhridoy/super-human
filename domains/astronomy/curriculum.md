# Astronomy Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. Observing unlocks depend on clear skies — a written/oral alternative is always acceptable when weather blocks the sky.

## L0 — Foundations: Night-Sky Orientation
- Concepts: celestial sphere basics; why stars appear to move (Earth's rotation); cardinal directions at night; light pollution and why dark skies matter; what a constellation is
- Skills: find Polaris/north (or local equivalent) at the learner's latitude; identify 5 naked-eye constellations/asterisms (e.g., Orion, Big Dipper, Cassiopeia, Scorpius, Southern Cross region as season allows); use a star chart/planetarium app matched to date+time
- Unlock: on one clear night, point out 5 constellations and 3 bright stars unaided and name them — or sketch the sky and label it from a simulated planetarium session

## L1 — The Moon & Planets
- Concepts: lunar phases and the 29.5-day cycle; why planets wander (retrograde idea); which planets are naked-eye visible (Mercury–Saturn); conjunctions and elongation
- Skills: log moon phases for 2+ weeks; distinguish planets from stars (steady light, position vs ecliptic); predict tonight's visible planets from an app before checking
- Unlock: moon-phase journal (14+ entries) + correctly identify 2 planets naked-eye on different nights

## L2 — The Solar System
- Concepts: structure of the solar system; terrestrial vs gas/ice giants; moons, asteroids, comets, Kuiper belt; why seasons happen; eclipses (solar vs lunar) mechanics
- Skills: build a scale model (distance or size) of the solar system; explain an eclipse geometry with a diagram; order and describe all planets
- Unlock: written solar-system quiz at 80%+ + a drawn scale-model with correct relative sizes/distances

## L3 — Stars & Galaxies
- Concepts: stellar life cycle (birth → main sequence → giant/remnant); star colors and temperature; light-years; the Milky Way; galaxy types; star clusters and nebulae
- Skills: read color/brightness differences among bright stars (Betelgeuse vs Rigel); locate 2 deep-sky objects naked-eye/binocular (e.g., Orion Nebula, Pleiades, Andromeda Galaxy if dark enough)
- Unlock: explain a star's life cycle in own words + locate and log 2 deep-sky objects

## L4 — Cosmology & Scale
- Concepts: Big Bang outline; expanding universe; cosmic distance ladder; age and size of the universe; dark matter/dark energy as open questions; humanity's place in scale
- Skills: order objects by distance (Moon → Sun → nearest star → galactic center → Andromeda → edge of observable universe); use powers of ten to compare scales
- Unlock: scale-ladder test at 80%+ + short written answer: "why the night sky is dark" (Olbers' paradox)

## L5 — Telescopes & Binoculars (intermediate milestone)
- Concepts: aperture vs magnification; reflector vs refractor vs compound; mounts (alt-az vs equatorial); field of view; why binoculars are a first instrument
- Skills: set up and align a finder; focus on a distant daytime object (never the sun); star-hop to a target; collimation/eyepiece basics
- Unlock: successfully acquire and observe 5 telescope/binocular targets (moon craters, Jupiter's moons, Saturn's rings, a cluster, a double star) across 3 sessions

## L6 — Deep-Sky Observing
- Concepts: Messier/NGC catalogs; averted vision; dark adaptation (20–30 min); seeing vs transparency; observing logs and sketching
- Skills: star-hop to 5+ deep-sky objects; keep a structured observing log (date, time, instrument, conditions, sketch); use red-light-only discipline
- Unlock: 10 logged deep-sky observations with sketches/notes + explain seeing vs transparency from one real night

## L7 — Astrophotography Basics
- Concepts: exposure triangle applied to the sky; wide-field vs planetary imaging; tracking and star trails; stacking idea; phone-on-tripod methods first
- Skills: capture a constellation and the Milky Way region (or moon) with tripod + manual settings; basic edit (levels/noise) on one image
- Unlock: portfolio of 5 processed sky photos with settings recorded + caption each with what's shown

## L8 — Tracking Real Sky Events
- Concepts: meteor showers (radiants, peaks); comets; eclipses; ISS and satellite passes; planetary oppositions and conjunctions; aurora (latitude-dependent)
- Skills: read an event calendar/almanac; plan an observing session around a peak; photograph or log one real event
- Unlock: catch and log 3 real sky events (e.g., a meteor shower peak, a conjunction, a lunar eclipse) with date, conditions, and notes

## L9 — Advanced Observing & Seasonal Tours
- Concepts: full seasonal sky cycles; coordinate systems (RA/Dec, alt-az); filters (lunar, narrowband intro); citizen-science projects (variable stars, meteor counts)
- Skills: give a full seasonal "sky tour" from memory; use equatorial coordinates to find a target; contribute one observation to a citizen-science count
- Unlock: complete a 4-season constellation tour + submit one citizen-science observation

## L10 — Mastery
- Concepts: synthesis — sky mechanics, observing technique, imaging, event planning
- Skills: plan and lead a complete guided stargazing session for others: site selection, timing, tour narration, telescope/binocular demonstration, safety and etiquette briefing
- Unlock: capstone — host a guided stargazing session for 2+ people with written plan + guest feedback, and pass a comprehensive oral sky-mechanics review at 85%+
