## Domain-specific KPIs
- Objects identified (count) — constellations, stars, planets, deep-sky objects correctly named unaided
- Observation sessions (count) — nights actually spent under the sky (clear or partial)
- Sky events caught (count) — meteor showers, eclipses, conjunctions, ISS passes observed and logged
- Moon-journal entries (count) — consecutive-phase logging streak
- Deep-sky objects logged (count) — with instrument noted and sketch/photo evidence
- Photos captured (count) — astrophotography keepers with settings recorded
- Clear-night hit rate (%) — planned sessions vs sessions actually run (weather reality check)
