# Astronomy Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Printed star charts / planisphere matched to the learner's latitude
- Free planetarium apps (Stellarium-style) for real-time sky simulation on phone/desktop
- Beginner "night sky" book or monthly "what's up tonight" guides from a local astronomy club
- Moon-phase calendar + printable moon-journal sheets
- Solar-system scale-model printouts and classroom-style explainer videos
- Yearly sky-events almanac (meteor showers, eclipses, planetary highlights) for the learner's hemisphere

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Introductory astronomy textbook chapters on stars, galaxies, and cosmology
- Binocular-observing guides: "touring the sky with 10x50s" style target lists
- Telescope-buying guides explaining aperture, mounts, and eyepieces (read before any purchase)
- Deep-sky object lists sorted by season and difficulty (Messier beginner subsets)
- Light-pollution map of the learner's region to pick observing sites

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Detailed observing guides: full Messier/NGC tours, double-star lists
- Astrophotography starter tutorials: tripod wide-field, phone adapters, stacking software guides
- Citizen-science platforms for variable-star and meteor-count submissions
- Star-party and astronomy-club directories for guided group sessions
- Advanced texts on cosmology and astrophysics for theory deepening
