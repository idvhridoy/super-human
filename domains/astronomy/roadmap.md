# Astronomy Roadmap

> Maintained by `@roadmap-planner`. Dates adjusted from evidence, not wishes. Assumes ~2 night/observing sessions per week plus short daytime theory reads. NOTE: astronomy is weather- and season-dependent — clouds, moon phase, and what's actually up tonight drive scheduling. Sky events (meteor showers, eclipses, conjunctions) are timing-dependent and cannot be rescheduled; treat them as calendar anchors and plan sessions around them.

## Next 30 days
- [ ] First clear-night session: find north, orient with a star chart/app, log conditions
- [ ] Learn 5 naked-eye constellations/asterisms visible this season
- [ ] Start moon-phase journal (aim 14 consecutive entries)
- [ ] Read solar safety rule + dark-sky etiquette (playbook.md) before first session
- [ ] Check upcoming sky-event calendar; bookmark the next observable event

## 60 days
- [ ] Complete L0 unlock: identify 5 constellations + 3 bright stars unaided
- [ ] Complete L1: finish moon journal, identify 2 planets on different nights
- [ ] Begin L2 solar-system study; draft scale-model diagram
- [ ] 10+ observation sessions logged in sessions.md (weather permitting)

## 90 days
- [ ] Complete L2 assessment (solar-system quiz 80%+ + scale model)
- [ ] Begin L3 stars & galaxies; attempt first deep-sky target (Orion Nebula or Pleiades, season permitting)
- [ ] Catch and log at least 1 real sky event from the calendar
- [ ] Decide on first instrument (binoculars vs telescope) with human approval — no purchase without sign-off

## This year
- Target: L4–L5 (cosmology concepts + first real instrument practice), pace gated by season and sky access
- Stretch: complete L5 unlock (5 telescope/binocular targets) and photograph the moon or a constellation (L7 preview)
- Every quarter: re-check the sky-event calendar and lock in the best 2–3 events per season
