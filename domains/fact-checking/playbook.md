# Fact-Checking Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence. HARD RULES marked — they are not negotiable.

## Do
- **HARD RULE: verify before believing AND before sharing** — the share button is a publishing decision; you are the last editor every claim passes
- **HARD RULE: stay non-partisan and evidence-first** — run the identical method on claims from your own side; check hardest the claims you want to be true
- Pause on emotional spikes — strong outrage, fear, or vindication is the tell that a check is needed most
- Find the primary source — closest to the event or data wins; reporting layers add distortion
- Triangulate with genuinely independent sources — different reporters, different evidence, same conclusion
- Read laterally — leave the page and check what trusted outsiders say about the source before trusting what it says about itself
- Trace media to the earliest version — wrong-caption-on-real-footage is the dominant visual deception
- State a verdict WITH confidence and method — "misleading, ~80%, here's the evidence trail" beats a bare true/false
- Treat "unverifiable" as a legitimate terminal answer — absence of evidence is a finding, not a failure
- Log corrections openly — a revised verdict handled transparently is craft, not embarrassment

## Don't
- **HARD RULE: never repeat `digital-literacy/` media-literacy basics** — feeds, URLs, ads-vs-content live there; reference them, don't re-teach
- Don't check only claims you dislike — asymmetric skepticism is how manipulation wins
- Don't confuse "many outlets reporting" with "independent confirmation" — find the origin count, not the echo count
- Don't treat a trusted outlet as infallible or a hated one as always wrong — grade evidence per claim
- Don't debunk people — debunk claims; humiliation hardens believers, it never corrects them
- Don't publish a verdict without the evidence trail — an unexplained verdict is just another claim
- Don't chase every claim — pick checkable, consequential ones; some things don't deserve your attention
- Don't pattern-match to a desired verdict — the method decides, not the tribe
- Don't perform cynicism — "everything is fake" is not verification, it's surrender wearing a smart costume
