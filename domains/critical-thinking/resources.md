# Critical Thinking Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Intro logic workbooks and argument-mapping exercises for beginners
- Claim/reason/evidence sorting worksheets and classroom-style logic card games
- "Introduction to arguments" explainer videos: premises, conclusions, hidden assumptions
- Simple syllogism practice sets with answers and explanations

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Illustrated fallacy field guides (name, tell, example per fallacy) — print and video formats
- Rhetoric-analysis exemplars: annotated ads, speeches, and opinion columns
- Debate-analysis worksheets: map both sides, name techniques, render verdict
- Curated "spot the fallacy" clip collections from ads, interviews, and comment sections

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Source-evaluation frameworks (CRAAP-style checklists) and primary-vs-secondary guides
- Statistics-literacy primers: correlation vs causation, base rates, misleading graphs
- Calibration-game tools and prediction-tracking journals (Brier-style scoring)
- Steelmanning/principle-of-charity essays and worked examples from philosophy intro texts
- Propaganda-analysis resources: repetition, manufactured consensus, emotional flooding case studies
- Classic critical-thinking texts for self-learners and claim-audit portfolio templates
