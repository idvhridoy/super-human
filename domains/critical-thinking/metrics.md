## Domain-specific KPIs
- Sorting/structure accuracy (%) — claim/reason/feeling and valid/sound exercises
- Argument maps completed (count) — real arguments mapped with hidden assumptions
- Fallacy log entries (count) — real-world instances named with the tell explained
- Evidence audits (count) — sources graded strong/medium/weak; verdicts-changed count
- Calibration score (%) — predicted confidence vs outcome over rolling predictions
- Beliefs updated (count) — documented cases where evidence changed the call
- Steelman exercises (count) — opposing cases stated to holder's satisfaction
- Claim audits in capstone portfolio (count) — target 8+ across ≥4 domains
