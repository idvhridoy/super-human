# Drawing & Visual Arts Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — specific picks approved by human for age and cost.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Starter kit: sketchbook, graphite pencil set, kneaded + vinyl erasers, sharpener
- Line and shape exercise sheets/drill printouts
- Beginner "how to draw" fundamentals book or video course
- Perspective primer: horizon line and vanishing point explainers
- Simple household objects for observation practice (fruit, cups, toys)

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Value and shading tutorials (hatching, cross-hatching, blending)
- Still-life arrangement guides: lighting, object selection, composition
- Gesture-drawing timer tools and pose reference libraries
- Figure proportion charts (head-units system)
- Grayscale markers or value pencils for tonal studies
- Intro anatomy-for-artists overview material

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Anatomy atlases written for artists (skeleton, musculature, surface forms)
- Masterwork studies: museum collections, public-domain art archives
- Color theory guides and color-wheel exercises
- Second-medium supplies: ink/pen set, watercolor basics, or entry digital tablet
- Portrait and figure reference collections (photos, life drawing groups where age-appropriate)
- Portfolio platforms and community critique spaces (human-approved)
