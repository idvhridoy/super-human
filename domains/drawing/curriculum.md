# Drawing & Visual Arts Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path from mark-making to an original portfolio; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.

## L0 — Foundations
- Materials setup: pencils, erasers, sketchbook, sharpener; grip and posture
- Line control: straight, curved, confident strokes; line-weight variation
- Basic shapes: circle, square, triangle, oval — clean and repeatable
- **Unlock:** fill a page of controlled lines + 20 clean basic shapes

## L1
- Shape construction: see objects as combinations of basic shapes
- Light & shadow intro: where light hits, where shadow falls
- Simple still life: single object (fruit, cup, toy) from observation
- **Unlock:** 5 still-life sketches built from underlying shapes

## L2
- Perspective: horizon line, vanishing points, eye level
- 1-point perspective: boxes, room interior, road/railway scene
- 2-point perspective: corner views of simple forms
- **Unlock:** correct 1-point room scene + 2-point box study

## L3
- Value: full 5–9 step value scale; shading techniques (hatching, blending)
- Form rendering: shaded sphere, cube, cylinder under one light source
- Texture basics: rough vs smooth, implied texture
- **Unlock:** shaded form sheet (3 forms) with full value range

## L4
- Composition: rule of thirds, focal point, balance, negative space
- Proportion measuring: sighting, comparative measurement with pencil
- Complex still life: multi-object arranged scene, full shading
- **Unlock:** multi-object still life with accurate proportions and composition

## L5 — Intermediate milestone
- Figure basics: gesture drawing (30s–2min poses), line of action
- Proportion: head-units system, mannequin/block figure construction
- Simple figures in motion from photo reference
- **Unlock:** 20 timed gesture sheets + a constructed figure with sound proportions

## L6
- Anatomy study: major skeleton landmarks, basic muscle groups
- Hands and feet: simplified structure then detailed studies
- Facial features: eyes, nose, mouth, ear — individually then together
- **Unlock:** anatomy study sheets + feature study sheet per feature

## L7
- Portraiture: head construction (loomis-style or equivalent), likeness
- Expressive figure: weight, balance, foreshortening basics
- Light on form: cast shadows, reflected light on figures
- **Unlock:** portrait with recognizable likeness + foreshortened figure study

## L8
- Color theory: wheel, harmonies, warm/cool, value in color
- Media expansion: ink/pen, watercolor, charcoal, or digital — pick one, add others
- Landscape/environment sketching outdoors or from reference
- **Unlock:** 3 finished pieces in a second medium + color harmony study

## L9
- Original work: invent subjects, not just copy references
- Style development: consistent series exploring one theme/technique
- Finish work: clean presentation, cropping, signature
- **Unlock:** series of 5 finished original pieces sharing a theme

## L10 — Mastery
- Coherent portfolio: 10+ strong pieces showing range and voice
- Consistent personal style recognizable across pieces
- Share/exhibit work: portfolio review, community show, or online presence (human-approved)
- **Unlock:** capstone portfolio review passed; mentor a beginner through fundamentals
