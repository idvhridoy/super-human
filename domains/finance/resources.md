# Finance Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Play-money sets and coin-counting worksheets/games
- Kid-friendly money explainer videos: earning, spending, saving (Khan-Academy-style kids courses)
- Printable money-log and envelope/jar budget templates
- Needs-vs-wants card-sort exercises and picture books on money basics

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Allowance/savings-goal tracker templates (paper or simple spreadsheet)
- Compound-interest calculators and doubling-time worksheets
- Sample bank statements for reconciliation practice
- Credit-card offer breakdowns: annotated real fine print for study
- Debt-danger case studies: minimum-payment math walkthroughs

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Unit-pricing and comparison-shopping worksheet packs; ad-deconstruction exercises
- Scam-spotting quizzes and consumer-protection explainers
- Stock-market simulators / paper-trading platforms (simulation only, human-approved)
- Investing-literacy primers: index funds, diversification, fees (intro-investing-course style)
- One-page business-model templates and unit-economics exercises
- Personal-finance system checklists: account structure, automation, review rhythm
