# Finance Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## ⚠️ HUMAN GATE — read first
**NO real-money action without explicit human/guardian approval.** This includes: purchases, bank/account creation or access, transfers, investments, credit/debit applications, selling anything, paid work or contracts, donating. All curriculum exercises default to paper, play money, allowance-scale, or supervised simulation. Real-world application is a feature — but always routed through a human.

## Do
- Record every in/out the same day — the money log is the foundation habit
- Budget before the month starts, not after it ends; review actual vs planned weekly
- Pay yourself first: move savings the moment income arrives, not "what's left"
- Apply the 24–48h rule to every want above a set threshold — most impulses die overnight
- Compute the real total cost: fees, interest, subscriptions, per-unit prices
- Read the fine print on every financial offer; if a claim seems too good, find the catch
- Compare at least 3 options before any significant (approved) purchase
- Teach what you learn — explaining compound interest to someone else cements it

## Don't
- Don't touch real accounts, cards, or apps without the human gate — simulation only
- Don't share financial details (balances, card numbers, PINs) with anyone — including in chat/AI tools
- Don't normalize debt: credit cards and loans are danger tools until fully understood (L5+)
- Don't fall for "guaranteed returns," urgency pressure, or free-money offers — all scams
- Don't skip tracking because the amount is small — small leaks sink budgets
- Don't present investing as safe or quick money; paper-trade first, respect risk
- Don't compare spending with peers — compare against the learner's own plan
