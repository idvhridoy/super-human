## Domain-specific KPIs
- Money-log streak (days) — every transaction recorded, zero gaps
- Budgets kept (count) — monthly budgets finished within ±10% of plan
- Savings goals hit (count) — on or before deadline
- Savings rate (%) — saved ÷ income (allowance/simulated)
- Impulse buys avoided (count) — wants resisted per the rule of the week
- Interest/debt problem accuracy (%) — L5 assessments, target 85%+
- Simulated portfolio return vs benchmark (%) — L7 paper trading only
- Scam-spotting test score (%) — target 90%+
