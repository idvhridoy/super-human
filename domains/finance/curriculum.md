# Finance Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.
> **HUMAN GATE:** every real-world money action — purchases, accounts, transfers, investments, contracts, paid work — requires explicit human/guardian approval. All exercises use simulation, play money, or supervised observation until approved.

## L0 — Money Basics
- Concepts: what money is, coins/notes and their values, earning vs spending, needs vs wants, price tags and change
- Skills: count mixed coins/notes, make change, sort needs/wants for a sample shopping list
- Unlock: counting/making-change test at 90%+ + correctly classify 20 items as need vs want with reasons

## L1 — Earning & Record-Keeping
- Concepts: where money comes from (work, gifts, business), allowance/chores link, keeping records
- Skills: maintain a simple income/expense log on paper for 2 weeks, compute weekly totals
- Unlock: accurate 2-week written money log + explain where the household's money comes from

## L2 — Budgeting
- Concepts: budget as a plan, income vs expenses, categories, envelopes/jars method, 50/30/20 adapted to learner scale
- Skills: build a written monthly budget; track actual vs planned; adjust
- Unlock: live 30-day tracked budget (real allowance or simulated income) within ±10% of plan

## L3 — Saving
- Concepts: saving goals (short/medium/long), emergency-fund concept, delayed gratification, sinking funds for planned purchases
- Skills: set a savings goal with timeline and weekly target; resist one impulse purchase deliberately
- Unlock: hit a real or simulated savings goal on schedule + written explanation of emergency funds

## L4 — Banking (human-gated: supervised observation only)
- Concepts: checking vs savings accounts, deposits/withdrawals, statements, debit vs cash, bank fees, what an ATM does
- Skills: read a bank statement line-by-line; reconcile statement vs own log
- Unlock: reconcile a sample statement 100% accurately + explain every fee on it. Real account access only with guardian co-management.

## L5 — Interest & Debt (intermediate milestone)
- Concepts: simple vs compound interest, loans, credit cards and APR, minimum-payment traps, good debt vs bad debt, the math of paying late
- Skills: compute compound growth by hand/formula; compare loan offers; calculate real cost of minimum-only payments
- Unlock: compound-interest problem set at 85%+ + written warning-grade analysis of one real credit-card offer's fine print

## L6 — Consumer Smarts
- Concepts: unit pricing, comparison shopping, ads and persuasion tactics, subscriptions and recurring charges, scams/fraud patterns, warranties
- Skills: compute unit prices, find hidden recurring costs, spot persuasion tactics in real ads
- Unlock: comparison-shop analysis beating sticker price on a real planned purchase (human-approved) + scam-spotting test at 90%+

## L7 — Investing Literacy (simulation only — no real trades)
- Concepts: stocks, bonds, index funds, risk vs return, diversification, time horizon, fees/expense ratios, "time in market"
- Skills: paper-trade a small simulated portfolio for 60+ days; explain why diversification works
- Unlock: written portfolio thesis + simulated portfolio tracked vs benchmark + quiz at 80%+. Real investing requires guardian decision at legal age.

## L8 — Income & Work
- Concepts: wages vs salary, payslips and deductions, taxes basics (what they fund, why withheld), freelance/business income, negotiating pay
- Skills: read a payslip, estimate take-home pay, compare job offers on total compensation
- Unlock: analyze a sample payslip and one freelance rate-card, computing real take-home correctly

## L9 — Entrepreneurship Basics
- Concepts: profit = revenue − costs, unit economics, pricing, customer value, break-even, cash flow vs profit, micro-business ideation
- Skills: build a one-page business model for a realistic micro-business (tutoring, crafts, digital service); compute break-even
- Unlock: written business plan with unit economics + pitched to human. Any launch/sales/accounts require explicit human approval.

## L10 — Mastery
- Concepts: personal finance system design (accounts, automated saving, review rhythm), long-term planning, insurance concepts, estate/goals basics
- Skills: design a full personal financial system on paper; teach one finance lesson to a peer
- Unlock: capstone — documented personal finance system + long-term plan + teach-back + comprehensive exam at 85%+
