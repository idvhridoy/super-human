# Brain Engineering Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.
> The flagship mind domain. Arc: **ASSESS → ENGINEER → EXCEED** — audit the brain, engineer the substrate and the stress response, then exceed under combined load. Vision: train the brain for every situation's best reaction.
> Boundaries — cross-reference, never duplicate: raw-capacity drills (memory palaces, pegs, speed mechanics) → `brain-training/`; in-the-moment tactics (calm protocol, bias defense) → `mind-hacks/`; breath/regulation practice → `mindfulness/`; logic quality → `critical-thinking/`; decision quality → `judgment/`; perception → `observation/`; external scenario handling → `situational-mastery/`. This domain measures and hardens the machine all of those run on.
> Evidence honesty: cognitive-training effects are real but modest (~g=0.45–0.5); working-memory gains are largely task-specific unless transfer is built deliberately; consistency > intensity for plasticity. Logged numbers beat confident claims.

## L0 — Foundations: The Cognitive Audit
- Skills: run the full baseline battery — simple + choice reaction time (ms), digit span forward/backward, adaptive dual-n-back entry level, sustained-attention task (10-min vigilance), processing-speed task (timed symbol/number work).
- Theory: neuroplasticity — the brain rewires under the right dose of challenge + recovery; you cannot engineer what you have not measured; every future claim of progress anchors to this audit.
- Assessment: complete the cognitive audit; results logged to `learner/assessments.md` (via `@assessment-engine`) and `metrics.md` as the permanent L0 reference.

## L1 — Stress-Response Profile
- Skills: stress probe — re-run a battery subset under mild pressure (countdown + observer) and log the delta vs calm baseline; map personal degraders (fatigue, hunger, noise, evaluation, time pressure); name early-warning signs (rushing, tunnel vision, blanking).
- Theory: the inverted-U arousal curve — too flat and too wired both degrade performance; stress chemistry narrows attention and shrinks working memory; degradation is personal and predictable — find YOUR signature.
- Assessment: written stress-response profile (top-3 degraders, warning signs, current counters) + calm-vs-pressure deltas logged in `metrics.md`.

## L2 — Biological Levers: Build the Hardware
- Skills: adopt the "learn after training" rule — hardest cognitive work scheduled within ~1–2h after physical training, coordinated with `@training-coordinator`; protect sleep for consolidation (sleep pillar + `@sleep-guardian`); follow a spaced-practice calendar (with `@learning-coach`).
- Theory: physical exercise raises BDNF — intensity-dependent, vigorous/HIIT producing the strongest acute effect — and exercise BEFORE learning improves encoding; sleep consolidates what training encoded; spacing beats massing for durable change.
- Assessment: 3-week adherence run — ≥4 days/week exercise→learning pairing, sleep targets met ≥80%, spacing calendar followed; all logged.

## L3 — Reaction Engineering
- Skills: simple-reaction drills → choice-reaction ladders (2 → 4 choices); speeded tasks with an accuracy floor (≥95%); reaction ms logged every session.
- Theory: processing speed is among the most trainable faculties — speed-of-processing training showed the strongest real-world transfer in large cognitive-training trials; speed without accuracy is noise; automaticity frees working memory for harder thinking.
- Assessment: ≥15% faster vs baseline on both simple and choice reaction at ≥95% accuracy, sustained across 3 sessions at least a week apart.

## L4 — Working-Memory Expansion
- Skills: adaptive dual-n-back progression (difficulty rises as you succeed); varied-practice transfer set — mental-math chains, multi-step instruction holds, dual-task drills.
- Theory: adaptive load keeps training at the edge of capacity where plasticity lives; honest limits — n-back gains are largely task-specific, so transfer is built deliberately through varied contexts, never assumed.
- Assessment: sustain +2 n-back levels vs baseline AND demonstrate one transfer rep (e.g., hold a 5-step instruction through a distraction, or a mental-math chain under a time limit).

## L5 — Stress Inoculation I: Education + Skills
- Skills: SIT phase 1 — learn the body-brain stress model (alarm → arousal → attentional narrowing) and map it to the L1 profile; SIT phase 2 — acquire the skill set: arousal/energy control (downshift + upshift on demand), process-goal setting under pressure, mental agility/flexibility drills (task-switching, plan-B generation).
- Theory: Stress Inoculation Training — military-validated 3-phase framework that works like a vaccine: education → skills → graded exposure; proven to reduce performance anxiety and enhance performance under stress; skills must be automated before exposure begins or they won't fire; regulation techniques themselves live in `mindfulness/`/`mind-hacks/` — here they are drilled for reliability.
- Assessment: demonstrate a 60-second on-demand downshift; written process-goal templates; mental-agility drill set passed; mild-pressure retest shows smaller degradation than the L1 deltas.

## L6 — Stress Inoculation II: Graded Exposure
- Skills: SIT phase 3 — climb a designed exposure ladder: distraction → time pressure → evaluation, one rung at a time; mental skills under load — decisions while physically fatigued (paired with `body-engineering` / `@training-coordinator`), sustained attention under distraction, emotional control while being watched/scored.
- Theory: graded dosing — each rung challenging but survivable, never flooding; every rep debriefed: what degraded, what held, what changes; exposure converts fragile skills into load-proof ones.
- Assessment: complete a 3-rung exposure ladder with logged performance; mid-exposure accuracy ≥85% of calm baseline; one debriefed "bad rep" with an extracted lesson.

## L7 — Combined Stressors (EXCEED begins)
- Skills: stack stressors deliberately — fatigue + time pressure, then fatigue + time + evaluation; a pre-load routine before stacked sessions; a post-load recovery protocol.
- Theory: stressors interact nonlinearly — combined load exceeds the sum of its parts; the method is to find the collapse edge, train just inside it, and expand it gradually; capacity margin is the real metric.
- Assessment: pass a designed dual-stressor assessment at ≥80% of calm baseline + a written map of own current breaking points.

## L8 — Best-Reaction Library
- Skills: build the reaction library — for each common scenario class (sudden question, rule change, physical surprise, social pressure, own error/failure) define and drill the trained best FIRST reaction; rapid-classification reps — recognize the scenario type → fire the matching response; scenario variety supplied by `@scenario-coach`.
- Theory: recognition-primed decision-making — under stress you don't rise to the occasion, you fall to trained responses; if-then plans ("if X happens → I do Y") fire faster than fresh deliberation; the best reaction is pre-decided, never improvised from zero.
- Assessment: mixed-scenario battery — 10 randomized scenario reps, correct first-reaction on ≥8, reaction latency logged.

## L9 — Teaching-Ready Mastery
- Skills: run the full ASSESS→ENGINEER→EXCEED protocol unsupervised; coach a peer or younger learner through one drill; explain the science in plain language (BDNF timing, spacing, SIT phases, task-specificity limits); self-coach — read own metrics and adjust the dose.
- Theory: teaching is the mastery test — explaining exposes gaps; self-coaching closes the loop so the system keeps working without the mentor.
- Assessment: one successful taught session (verified by mentor or `@assessment-engine`) + written self-coaching review showing at least one data-driven program adjustment.

## L10 — Mastery: The Multi-Stressor Capstone
- Skills: maintain an autonomous, evidence-driven brain-engineering practice; hold elite performance under fully combined load.
- Theory: synthesis — the learner owns their cognitive profile, their stress signature, their engineered routines, and their honest limits (fatigue and overwhelm → recover/escalate, don't push).
- Assessment: capstone — the Multi-Stressor Battery (fatigue + time pressure + evaluation + distraction in one graded assessment): score ≥90% of calm baseline (elite threshold) + written personal brain-engineering playbook + durable gains vs the L0 audit on spaced re-test.

## Safety frame (all levels)
- Gradual stress exposure only — never distress flooding; one new stressor at a time.
- Distress signals (panic, shutdown, overwhelm) → stop the rep, downshift, log, and drop one rung; `@safety-guardian` notified.
- This is performance training, never therapy — persistent anxiety, panic, or mood flags → `## NEXT: human-consult`.
- All difficulty filtered by `learner/profile.md` age; ward mode → guardian approves any new exposure intensity.
