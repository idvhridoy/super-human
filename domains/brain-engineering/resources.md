# Brain Engineering Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — specific titles/URLs added after librarian review (never invent links).

## L0–L2 (foundations — audit + biological levers)
- A baseline-test kit: reaction-time test tools, digit-span sheets, a free dual-n-back program, symbol-search/speed grids, a 10-min vigilance task, timers.
- A plain-language explainer of neuroplasticity and the brain's stress response.
- A reputable review-level summary of the exercise→BDNF→learning literature (not hype).
- Sleep-and-memory consolidation overview — coordinate with the sleep pillar.
- Stress-profile journal template: degraders, warning signs, counters.

## L3–L5 (intermediate — speed, working memory, SIT skills)
- Choice-reaction training tools: 2→4 choice ladders with latency logging.
- Adaptive dual-n-back programs with difficulty progression.
- Cognitive-training evidence summaries — honest effect sizes (~g=0.45–0.5), task-specificity caveats, and which training types transfer best (speed-of-processing findings in large trials).
- A Stress Inoculation Training program guide — the 3-phase structure (education → skills → graded exposure), military and sport mental-skills versions.
- Mental-skills workbooks: arousal/energy control, process-goal setting, mental agility and flexibility drills.

## L6–L10 (advanced — exposure, combined stressors, teaching)
- Graded-exposure design guides — building a safe stressor ladder (distraction → time → evaluation → combined).
- Under-load drill sets: decision tasks under physical fatigue, attention-under-distraction protocols, evaluation-pressure simulations.
- Scenario libraries for best-reaction drills — coordinate with `@scenario-coach`.
- Reading on recognition-primed decision-making and if-then implementation intentions.
- A teach-back lesson-plan template for the L9 teaching requirement.
- Capstone design notes: assembling the Multi-Stressor Battery and scoring it against calm baseline.
