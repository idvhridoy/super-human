# Brain Engineering Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Core method
- ASSESS → ENGINEER → EXCEED: measure first, engineer the substrate and the stress response, then exceed under combined load.
- Consistency > intensity — plasticity responds to frequent moderate doses, not heroic sessions. Default dose: 15–20 min, 4–5 days/week.
- Honest expectations: cognitive-training effect sizes are real but modest (~g=0.45–0.5) — engineering is the multiplier, not a magic pill.

## Do
- Schedule the hardest thinking within ~1–2h after physical training — exercise→BDNF→encoding; the "learn after training" rule, coordinated with `@training-coordinator`.
- Protect sleep — consolidation happens in sleep; cognitive loading on sleep debt is wasted effort (sleep pillar + `@learning-coach` for spacing).
- Dose stress gradually: one new stressor at a time, always logged and debriefed.
- Benchmark everything against the L0 audit — load-tolerance and transfer are the claims; drill scores are only the means.
- Log perceived stress (1–5) every session; track the calm-vs-pressure delta, not just absolute scores.
- Keep adaptive tasks adaptive — difficulty must track ability or the plasticity signal fades.
- Cross-train the best-reaction library across scenario types with `@scenario-coach`.
- Debrief every failed rep — a debriefed failure at the edge teaches more than an easy pass.
- Pair fatigue-loaded reps with `body-engineering` / `@training-coordinator` so physical load is planned, not accidental.

## Don't
- Don't flood: never jump to maximum distress — graded exposure only, one rung at a time.
- Don't train through exhaustion, illness, or dread — fatigued reps teach the brain the wrong lesson; reschedule.
- Don't claim transfer without evidence — n-back gains are largely task-specific; only varied-practice results count.
- Don't stack stressors before each single stressor is passed — dual before single is flooding.
- Don't trust same-day re-tests — they flatter; real gains show on spaced re-tests.
- Don't chase drill scores as the goal — the metric that matters is performance under load vs calm baseline.
- Don't treat this as therapy — persistent anxiety, panic, or low mood → `## NEXT: human-consult`.

## Red lines
- Distress signals during exposure (panic, shutdown, overwhelm) → STOP the rep immediately, downshift, log; `@safety-guardian` notified; next session drops one rung.
- Any mental-health flag → `## NEXT: human-consult` — this domain is performance training, never therapy.
- Never hide failed reps or inflate scores — the audit is only useful if honest.
- Age-appropriate difficulty always (`learner/profile.md`); ward mode → guardian approves any new exposure intensity.
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
