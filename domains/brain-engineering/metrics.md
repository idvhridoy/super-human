## Domain-specific KPIs (L0 audit = permanent reference)
- Simple reaction time: median ms (baseline TBD → target −15% by L3).
- Choice reaction time: median ms at ≥95% accuracy (2-choice and 4-choice tracked separately).
- Digit span: longest string recalled forward / backward.
- Working memory: highest sustained dual-n-back level (target +2 vs baseline by L4).
- Sustained attention: vigilance-task score (accuracy + lapses over 10 min); longest clean focus block (min).
- Processing speed: correct items per minute at ≥95% accuracy.
- Performance-under-stress rating: loaded score as % of calm baseline — per stressor and combined (target ≥90% at L10).
- Perceived stress: session average 1–5; rising baseline at the same dose = overreach flag.
- Exercise→learning adherence: pairings per week (target ≥4 by L2).
- Streaks / adherence: sessions completed vs planned each week.
