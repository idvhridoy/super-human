# Survival Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Theory first, practice second: every skill starts as diagrams/drills before any outdoor rep
- Always apply the tell-someone rule — route, return time, who to call
- Pack the ten essentials even for short outings; habits form on easy days
- Leave No Trace on every trip; review the site before leaving
- Rehearse the family emergency plan twice a year — plans rot if never drilled
- Debrief every outdoor session: what worked, what to change

## Don't
- Never practice fire, knives, axes, or any tool alone — adult-supervised, always
- Never drink untreated found water, even if it looks clear
- Don't wander to "find help" when lost — stay put, stay visible, signal
- Don't build shelters under dead branches, in dry streambeds, or on ant nests
- No unsupervised overnights or remote trips — supervision is a hard rule, not a level reward

## Safety notes (physical)
- All practical elements are adult-supervised; fire practice only where legal with water/extinguisher ready and full extinguishment verified
- Cutting tools: introduced supervised only, age-gated per `learner/profile.md`; gloves and safe-circle rules
- Weather: sessions cancelled on storm/heat-risk forecasts; check `learner/health.md` for allergies before outdoor work
- Any real emergency, injury, or getting lost → stop, call for help, flag `## NEXT: human-consult`
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
