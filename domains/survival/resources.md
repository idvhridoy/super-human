# Survival Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Illustrated survival-basics books for young learners (priorities, stay-put, signaling)
- Beginner map-reading and compass tutorials (charts + videos)
- Local-area maps for practice routes
- Ten-essentials packing checklist

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Water-finding and purification guides (boiling, filters, tablets — comparisons)
- Shelter site-selection and tarp-build diagrams
- Fire-lay theory charts (triangle, stages, safety checklist)
- Weather-signs and lightning-safety guides

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Leave No Trace principle cards and camp-hygiene guides
- Knot-tying step diagrams and practice-cord kit lists
- Family emergency-plan and go-bag templates
- Signaling reference (whistle/mirror/ground-to-air codes)
- Supervised-camping checklists and local outdoor-skills course directories (human-led)
