# Survival Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. All practical/outdoor elements (fire, tools, camping, water collection) are adult-supervised only — theory first, practice second, never alone.

## L0 — Foundations
- Survival priorities: rule of threes (3 min air, 3 hr shelter, 3 days water, 3 weeks food)
- The stay-put principle; tell-someone-where-you're-going rule
- Assessment: recite priorities + explain why staying put beats wandering

## L1 — Navigation Basics
- Cardinal directions from sun/shadows, landmarks, and simple mental mapping
- Basic trail awareness: looking back, noting junctions
- Assessment: navigate a short familiar route naming directions correctly

## L2 — Map & Compass
- Reading a map: scale, symbols, contour lines
- Compass basics: parts, orienting a map, following a bearing
- Assessment: plot a 3-point route on a local map and walk it supervised

## L3 — Water
- Finding water (signs, terrain); why all found water is treated
- Purification theory: boiling, filters, purification tablets; dehydration signs
- Assessment: explain the 3 purification methods + spot 5 water sources on a map

## L4 — Shelter
- Site selection: dry, flat, away from hazards (dead branches, flood lines, ant nests)
- Insulation and wind protection concepts; tarp/lean-to builds supervised
- Assessment: choose a site with reasons + help build a tarp shelter supervised

## L5 — Fire Theory — Intermediate milestone
- Fire triangle (heat/fuel/oxygen), tinder→kindling→fuel stages
- Fire safety: clearing, water nearby, full extinguishing — supervised practice only, where legal
- Assessment: lay a fire correctly (unlit) + pass the safety Q&A before any supervised lighting

## L6 — Outdoor Safety & Weather
- Reading weather signs, lightning position, heat/cold illness signs
- The ten essentials: what goes in a day pack and why
- Assessment: pack a correct day bag + weather-hazard scenario quiz

## L7 — Camping Skills
- Pitch a tent, camp hygiene, safe food storage, gray-water disposal
- Leave No Trace principles
- Assessment: one supervised campout with clean LNT review

## L8 — Knots & Cordage
- Core knots: reef, figure-8, bowline, clove hitch, taut-line
- Practical uses: ridgelines, securing loads, repairs
- Assessment: tie 5 knots blindfolded/by feel, name each use

## L9 — Emergency Preparedness
- Family emergency plan: meeting points, contacts, roles
- Go-bag build; signaling basics (whistle, mirror, ground signals, three-of-anything)
- Assessment: go-bag to checklist + family plan written and drilled once

## L10 — Mastery
- Plan and lead a supervised overnight camp: route, site, water, shelter, LNT
- Teach survival priorities + one skill to a beginner
- Assessment: led camp review report + taught session + emergency drill run
