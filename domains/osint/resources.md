# OSINT Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs. All resources vetted for ethical/legal framing; anything teaching stalking, evasion, or intrusion is excluded on sight.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Ethics-of-OSINT primers and responsible-research codes of conduct
- Intro explainers on public records: what exists, where, and why it's public
- Search-operator cheat sheets and query-building practice sets
- Public-records directories and official registry guides (company, land, court, regulator databases)

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Source-verification frameworks: corroboration, independence tests, circular-reporting detection
- Reverse-image-search tutorials and image-provenance exercise sets (public images only)
- Image-metadata primers: what EXIF shows, why it's unreliable, how to treat it as a lead
- Geolocation-clue method guides (signage, terrain, language, architecture) — public places only
- Org-profiling templates: registrations, leadership, publications, official channels

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Provenance and documentation standards: research logs, confidence labeling (admiralty-style scales)
- Structured OSINT workflow guides — collection plans, note systems, audit trails
- Defensive-OSINT self-audit checklists and exposure-reduction guides
- Report-writing exemplars: executive summary, findings, confidence labels, limitations, appendix
- Professional OSINT methodology texts and case studies from journalism/security research
- Legal-compliance references: computer-misuse and privacy-law overviews written for researchers
