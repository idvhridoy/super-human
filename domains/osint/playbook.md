# OSINT Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## LEGAL & ETHICAL RED LINES — hard rules; a violation ends the domain, not just the lesson
- **Public sources only** — no bypassing logins, paywalls, or access controls; no private or leaked data. Ever.
- **Never stalk, monitor, or harvest private individuals** — no profiling, tracking, following, or collecting on real private people. Profiling exercises are restricted to organizations, public entities, and public figures acting in their public capacity.
- **No fake accounts, sock puppets, or deception** — research is conducted under honest identity or passive public browsing only.
- **No doxxing** — never compile or publish identifying data on a person; if a task would produce it, stop.
- **Purposes: defensive security, research, verification** — anything else → `## NEXT: human-consult` before acting.
- **Comply with law** — computer-misuse, privacy, and data-protection laws apply; terms of service are respected.
- **Consent for people-audits** — auditing another person's footprint requires their explicit informed consent; default is self-audit only.

## Do
- Recite the red lines before each session — ethics first, technique second
- Prefer primary official sources — registrations, filings, official publications beat clever tricks
- Corroborate across independent sources and label confidence on every finding
- Document provenance as you collect — the audit trail IS the product
- State limitations honestly in every report — it builds trust, not weakness
- When a lead points at a private individual's personal life — stop, log the boundary, redirect
- Keep collection notes so a reviewer can re-verify every claim
- Save and cite what you find — pages change; your citation is the record

## Don't
- Don't touch private or semi-private spaces — closed groups, logged-in areas, leaked or dumped data
- Don't download or browse breach data even when "publicly available" — possession can be a crime
- Don't interact with research subjects — passive public-source reading only; no contact, no probing, no tests
- Don't geolocate private homes or individuals' whereabouts — geolocation skills are for public places and events
- Don't conflate "technically accessible" with "ethical" — the red lines decide, not capability
- Don't present inference as fact — confidence labels are mandatory on every claim
- Don't run exercises on classmates, neighbors, family members, or any private person — self and public orgs only
- Don't publish or share raw findings about real entities without human review

## Safety notes
- Illegal/abusive content encountered during research (dox dumps, leaked credentials, abuse material): do not open or copy — close, report to a human, `## NEXT: human-consult`
- Ward mode: guardian co-signs scope before L5+ exercises; all reports human-reviewed before any use
- Content age-gated per `learner/profile.md`; `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes
