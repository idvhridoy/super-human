# Music Roadmap

> Maintained by `@roadmap-planner`. Dates adjusted from evidence, not wishes.

## Next 30 days
- [ ] Complete L0: steady beat on 2 songs; 5 echo-rhythms accurate; listening log running.
- [ ] Confirm chosen instrument + obtain/arrange it (guardian decision if purchase needed).
- [ ] Establish the practice habit: 15–20 min daily at a fixed time — consistency is the milestone.
- [ ] Finish L1: 5 clean notes, correct posture, 3 sessions in a row.
- [ ] Reach L2: first simple song learned end-to-end at slow tempo.

## 60 days
- [ ] Complete L2: 3 short pieces playable start-to-finish.
- [ ] Complete L3: sight-read 5 four-bar exercises; note names secure.
- [ ] Start L4: first major scale clean at slow metronome tempo.
- [ ] Build the practice split: warm-up / slow work / fun piece — logged each session.

## 90 days
- [ ] Complete L4: 2 scales + first chord/pattern work solid.
- [ ] Complete L5: first full piece performed for an audience; unlock "First Performance".
- [ ] Repertoire count at 4+ pieces; self-recording habit started.
- [ ] Milestone check with `@orchestrator`: adjust pace for practice days/week actually achieved.

## This year
- Reach L7–L8: real repertoire (8+ pieces), song analysis skills, regular mini-performances.
- First improvisation experiments; draft the L9 composition idea.
