# Music Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — specific titles/URLs added after librarian review (never invent links).

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Rhythm clapping/echo game collections and call-and-response activities.
- Beginner instrument-method book for the chosen instrument (first-notes graded series).
- Active-listening playlists spanning genres and eras, with a simple log template.
- Intro explainer videos on beat, rhythm, melody, and dynamics.
- Simple first-songs books matched to the instrument (large notation, few notes).

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Graded sight-reading exercise books for the instrument (4–8 bar studies).
- Flashcards/apps for note names, staff positions, and rhythm values.
- A physical or app metronome — used in every technique session.
- Beginner theory workbook: staff, clefs, note values, time signatures, first scales.
- Graded repertoire collections for the first-performance milestone.
- Recording method: any phone/basic recorder + a reflection sheet.

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Intermediate repertoire across 2–3 styles the learner enjoys.
- Music-theory workbook covering intervals, chords, keys, and song-form analysis.
- Books/guides on practice methods and performance psychology at student level.
- Improvisation starter materials: backing tracks and simple chord-progression charts.
- Notation/charting tools for composition work (paper staff or beginner notation app).
- Live performance opportunities list: family recitals, school events, community stages — guardian approved.
