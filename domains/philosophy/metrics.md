## Domain-specific KPIs
- Questions logged: question-journal entries with at least two possible answers.
- Arguments dissected: count of claim/premise/conclusion breakdowns completed.
- Fallacies identified: correctly named real-world examples (target 10+ for L2).
- Thinker cards: profiles completed of 8 target thinkers (Western + Eastern mix required).
- Essays written: count + mentor review score (1–5) — must include steel-manned objection to count for L5+.
- Belief audits: own beliefs examined for source, confidence, and what would change them.
- Socratic dialogues held: live sessions + concessions made (tracked as a strength metric).
