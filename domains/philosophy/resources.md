# Philosophy Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — specific titles/URLs added after librarian review (never invent links).

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- "Big questions" illustrated books that introduce philosophy to young readers.
- Story-based logic puzzles and brainteasers that smuggle in reasoning skills.
- Beginner explainer videos on arguments, premises, and common fallacies.
- Fallacy reference sheets/flashcards with everyday examples (ads, rules, debates).
- A blank question-journal template for the L0 habit.

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Age-appropriate ethics case collections: moral dilemmas and thought experiments.
- Introductory survey texts covering major Western and Eastern thinkers.
- Thinker-profile worksheet template (idea / best argument / criticism).
- Guided retellings of classics — Plato's cave, Socratic dialogues in adapted form.
- Position-essay scaffold: claim → reasons → objection → reply template.
- Puzzle and paradox collections (Zeno, Theseus, trolley variants adapted for age).

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Accessible introductions to epistemology, philosophy of mind, and political philosophy.
- Short primary-text selections with reading guides (Descartes' Meditations excerpt, Mill, Kant groundwork excerpt — per age fit).
- Essay collections showing philosophers disagreeing on the same question.
- Debate/discussion protocols for the live Socratic-dialogue assessments.
- Writing guides for philosophical essays: structuring objections and replies.
- Recorded university-style intro lectures used selectively under mentor guidance.
