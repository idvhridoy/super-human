## Domain-specific KPIs
- Scenario-quiz accuracy: % correct on rights/duties and civil-vs-criminal items (target 80%+).
- Glossary size: legal terms defined in own words (target +3/session).
- Legal texts read & summarized: statutes, ToS clauses, constitution articles (count).
- Contracts diagrammed: count with correct element labels.
- Case briefs written: count + completeness score (facts/issue/rule/holding/reasoning present).
- Opinion memos: count + mentor review score (1–5).
- "When to ask a real lawyer" judgment: correctly flags professional-level questions in drills.
