# General Law Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Hard disclaimer (repeat as needed)
- This domain teaches legal **literacy**, not legal advice. Nothing produced here is advice for a real dispute. Any real legal question routes to a qualified, licensed professional — log `## NEXT: human-consult`.
- Laws differ by country and change over time; always state jurisdiction and check dates.

## Do
- Anchor every concept to the learner's real life: school rules, purchases, accounts, family agreements.
- Build the personal glossary every session — law is vocabulary-heavy.
- Prefer the learner's own jurisdiction for examples (default Bangladesh); note explicitly when a concept is generic.
- Teach the two-column habit: every right ↔ corresponding duty; every claim ↔ the counter-argument.
- Use simplified-but-real materials: actual short clauses, real public judgments with hard parts trimmed.
- Praise precision: "what exactly does the text say" beats "what do you think is fair".

## Don't
- Don't give or imply advice on real-life disputes the learner or family may have — flag them out.
- Don't use graphic criminal cases; pick age-appropriate, hypothetical or civil examples.
- Don't memorize for memorization's sake — every term must survive an "explain it to a friend" test.
- Don't present one country's rules as universal; label jurisdiction on everything.
- Don't skip the ethics strand: laws can be unjust; discuss rule-of-law vs rule-by-law at level-appropriate depth.
- Don't practice "gotcha" law — no teaching loophole-hunting as the goal.

## Safety notes
- If any session surfaces a real legal issue (family, school, online harm), stop instruction and log `## NEXT: human-consult` for the guardian.
