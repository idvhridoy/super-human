# General Law Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — specific titles/URLs added after librarian review (never invent links).

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Children's/young-reader civics books explaining rules, rights, and government.
- Animated explainer series on how laws are made and what courts do.
- The learner's own constitution — a child-friendly summary edition plus the real text for reference.
- Printable scenario cards: everyday situations to sort by right/duty and civil/criminal.
- A simple "bill becomes law" flowchart template for the learner's jurisdiction.

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Beginner legal-literacy guides written for non-lawyers (consumer rights, contracts, everyday law).
- Simplified case summaries adapted for students.
- Real short documents for L7 warm-up: a terms-of-service clause, a rental-agreement excerpt, a statute section.
- Mock-trial or debate kits for practicing both sides of a dispute.
- Vocabulary flashcard sets for core legal terms.

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Introductory "law for everyone" or first-year-law-student overview texts (selected chapters only).
- Public judgment databases / landmark-case summaries with guided reading notes.
- Case-briefing templates (facts / issue / rule / holding / reasoning).
- Plain-language guides to digital rights: privacy, data protection, intellectual property basics.
- A curated glossary-building workbook; spaced-review schedule for legal vocabulary.
