# Leadership Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Character story collections: service-first leaders, honest accounts of courage and responsibility (age-appropriate biographies)
- Leader-vs-boss discussion cards and scenario prompts
- Reliability tracker: promise/commitment log templates
- Initiative journal: "spot the need" entry format (need → action → result)
- Observation guide: what to watch for in adults/older kids who lead well

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Helping-skills primers: guiding questions, listen-first frameworks, teach-the-method approach
- Small-team task planner templates: goal sentence, roles, check-in rhythm
- Cooperative group games that require real coordination
- Leading-by-example case studies (youth teams, school projects)
- Feedback phrase guides: kind, specific, one-strength-one-improvement

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Group decision templates: input gathering, decide-and-why, outcome review
- Peer-mentoring guides: demonstrate → practice → feedback → independence
- Service-project planning pack: need-finding, plan, roles, timeline, review templates
- Youth volunteering/service program directories (human-supervised, local)
- Leadership debrief template: planned → happened → needed → change
- Books/talks on servant leadership adapted for young learners
