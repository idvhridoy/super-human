# Leadership Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. Scope: "hero-driven problem solving" — the learner becomes the person who steps up. Character → reliability → initiative → helping → organizing → mentoring → leading real projects that benefit others. Persuasion and speaking mechanics live in `domains/communication/`; fairness and conduct in `domains/behavior/`; decision frameworks in `domains/strategy/`; staying calm when plans break in `domains/situational-mastery/`. This domain references them — never repeats them. All content filtered by learner age (`learner/profile.md`).

## L0 — Foundations
- What leaders actually do: serve the group, carry responsibility, go first — never boss
- Character roots: courage (act despite fear), responsibility (own your part), honesty (truth even when costly)
- Leadership is observable: watch trusted adults/older kids who lead well and name what they do
- **Unlock:** explains leader-vs-boss difference; names 3 observed leadership behaviors in real people

## L1 — Reliability
- Keep small promises: chores, commitments, showing up on time
- Finish what you start: closing loops, reporting back when done
- Own mistakes fast: "that was mine, here's my fix" — no excuses, no blaming
- **Unlock:** 14-day reliability streak — every logged promise/commitment kept; one mistake owned openly

## L2 — Initiative
- Spot the need: notice what needs doing before being asked (mess, confusion, someone struggling)
- Act without permission on small things: pick it up, organize it, start it
- Volunteer: raise a hand first for unglamorous tasks
- **Unlock:** 3 logged acts of unsought initiative — need spotted, action taken, result noted

## L3 — Helping Others Solve Problems
- Listen first: understand their problem fully before any advice (depth in `domains/behavior/`)
- Guide, don't hijack: ask questions that lead them to their own solution
- Help that teaches: show the method, not just the answer — they should need you less next time
- **Unlock:** 2 logged help sessions where the other person reached the solution themselves

## L4 — Organizing a Small Team Task
- Define the goal in one sentence everyone can repeat
- Match people to tasks: ask what each wants to try, play to strengths
- Check-in rhythm: quick "where are we, what's blocking you" mid-task
- **Unlock:** organizes one real small team task (family project, group game, clean-up) to completion with all members contributing

## L5 — Leading by Example — Intermediate milestone
- Work alongside: same rules, same effort, hardest task taken first by you
- Credit publicly, correct privately: wins shared with names, feedback given one-on-one and kindly
- Friction handling: when team members clash, focus them back on the goal — fairness rules from `domains/behavior/`
- **Unlock:** leads a multi-session team effort to completion; every member can name what they contributed and felt valued

## L6 — Group Decision-Making
- Gather input first: every voice heard before deciding
- Decide and own it: pick a path, explain the why, take responsibility for the outcome
- Decision frameworks depth lives in `domains/strategy/` — applied here to real groups
- **Unlock:** leads a group through a real decision with written why + outcome review

## L7 — Mentoring a Peer
- Teach one skill to someone younger/less experienced: demonstrate, let them try, feedback kindly
- Feedback that builds: specific, honest, one strength + one improvement
- Celebrate their progress publicly — mentoring is their stage, not yours
- **Unlock:** mentors a peer through one skill to completion; mentee performs it independently

## L8 — Leading Under Pressure
- Calm is contagious: when plans break, your composure sets the team's temperature (depth in `domains/situational-mastery/`)
- Adapt openly: "plan changed, here's the new one" — deciding under uncertainty without hiding it
- Ask for help upward: knowing when a problem needs an adult/expert is leadership, not weakness
- **Unlock:** navigates a real disruption during a led activity — team stays functional, debrief logged

## L9 — Service Leadership
- Find a real need: school, neighborhood, family, club — something that benefits others, not your résumé
- Plan it properly: goal, people, tasks, timeline, review — written with mentor feedback
- Recruit and coordinate volunteers: invite, don't order; thank every contributor
- **Unlock:** service project plan approved by a mentor and kicked off with a real team

## L10 — Mastery
- Leads a real project end-to-end that benefits others: need → plan → team → delivery → review
- Hero-driven problem solving embodied: the person who steps up when something needs doing
- Produces leaders: someone the learner mentored now leads or helps others
- **Unlock:** capstone — completed service project with evidence (before/after, team feedback, beneficiary impact) + a mentee who now leads independently
