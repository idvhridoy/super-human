# Cooking Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Read the whole recipe before touching anything; mise en place first
- Wash hands on entry, after raw protein, after phone/face touching
- Clean as you cook — the meal ends when the kitchen is reset
- Taste and adjust seasoning near the end, not the start
- Log every dish in the personal recipe file with what to change next time
- Tie meals to `pillars/nutrition/meal-plan.md` — cooking serves the Eat pillar

## Don't
- No knives or heat without an adult present until explicitly cleared per `learner/profile.md` age
- Never leave a lit stove or running oven unattended — even "for a second"
- Don't mix raw and cooked utensils/boards — cross-contamination is invisible
- Don't taste raw dough/batter or undercooked meat/egg
- Don't improvise with unfamiliar appliances or gas fittings — ask the adult

## Safety notes (physical)
- Knives: claw grip mandatory, stable board, age-gated introduction, supervision until L7+
- Heat: pot handles in, dry hands near electricity, oven mitts always, know where the lid/extinguisher is for grease flare-ups — never water on oil fires
- Burns/cuts during sessions → stop, first-aid response, log, flag `## NEXT: human-consult` if beyond minor
- Allergies/dietary rules: check `learner/health.md` before every recipe — allergy changes need human approval
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
