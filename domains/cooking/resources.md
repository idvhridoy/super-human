# Cooking Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Beginner cookbooks for young learners (picture-based, no-cook and simple recipes)
- Kitchen hygiene and food-safety intro charts/videos
- Measuring and recipe-reading practice sheets
- Local staples glossary (rice, dal, spices) with photos

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Knife-skills video tutorials (claw grip, supervised practice sets)
- Egg/rice/pasta/dal technique guides and step-by-step videos
- Two-component timing worksheets for simple meals
- Beginner curry-base and fried-rice recipe cards

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Balanced-plate and label-reading guides (aligned with `pillars/nutrition/`)
- Weekly meal-planning and budget-shopping templates
- Food storage/leftover-safety charts (cooling times, fridge guide)
- Beginner baking guides (flatbread, muffins, bread)
- Regional and world cuisine recipe collections for signature-dish exploration
