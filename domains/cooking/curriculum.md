# Cooking Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. Knives, heat, and appliances are age-gated per `learner/profile.md` and always introduced under adult supervision.

## L0 — Foundations
- Kitchen layout: where tools, staples, and cleaning supplies live
- Hygiene: handwashing, clean surfaces, hair back, apron; cross-contamination basics (raw vs. cooked)
- Assessment: hygiene checklist recited + kitchen tour passed

## L1 — Kitchen Safety
- Heat awareness: stove zones, steam, "hot" reflexes, pot handles turned in
- Safe appliance rules, spill cleanup, what never goes near water/electricity
- Assessment: name 6 kitchen hazards and the rule for each

## L2 — No-Cook Prep
- Washing/peeling produce, measuring cups/spoons, reading a simple recipe
- Make 3 no-cook items: salad, sandwich/wrap, fruit plate
- Assessment: follow a written recipe start-to-finish with correct measuring

## L3 — Knife Basics (age-gated)
- Claw grip, stable board (damp cloth under), cut away from body
- Start soft: banana, cucumber, boiled potato with a safe knife — supervised only
- Assessment: 10 supervised cuts with correct claw grip, zero unsafe moves

## L4 — Heat Basics (supervised)
- Boiling, steaming, simmering; eggs: boiled and scrambled; plain rice
- Stove-on/stove-off checklist every session
- Assessment: cook rice + one egg dish supervised, checklist followed

## L5 — Staples & Simple Meals — Intermediate milestone
- Pasta, dal, fried rice, simple curry base (onion–garlic–spice)
- Timing two components at once
- Assessment: produce a complete simple meal for the family supervised

## L6 — Nutrition-Aware Cooking
- Balanced plate (protein/veg/starch/healthy fat) tied to `pillars/nutrition/meal-plan.md`
- Reading labels, sugar/salt awareness, smart swaps
- Assessment: design and cook a meal hitting the day's macro targets

## L7 — Meal Independence
- Cook full breakfast, lunch, and dinner across the week — supervision fading to check-in
- Cleanup-as-you-cook discipline
- Assessment: 3 full solo-cooked meals (adult present, not assisting)

## L8 — Meal Prep & Planning
- Write a weekly meal plan + shopping list on a budget
- Batch cooking, safe cooling/storage/reheating of leftovers
- Assessment: one week planned, shopped (with adult), and executed

## L9 — Flavor & Baking Basics
- Spice profiles (local + world), seasoning to taste, acid/fat/heat balance
- One simple bake: bread, muffins, or flatbread
- Assessment: dish adjusted by taste + one successful bake

## L10 — Mastery
- Three signature dishes cooked independently start-to-finish
- Host a family meal: plan, cook, plate, serve, clean
- Assessment: hosted meal + dishes logged in learner's personal recipe file
