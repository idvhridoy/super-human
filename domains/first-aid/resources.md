# First Aid Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Children's/young-learner first-aid picture books (scene safety, calling for help)
- National emergency-service guidance and helpline info for Bangladesh
- Printable emergency-number card and mock-call script templates
- Short age-appropriate videos on "check, call, care" sequence

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Beginner wound-care and bleeding-control guides (illustrated, non-graphic)
- CPR-awareness videos from recognized first-aid organizations (manikin demos)
- Recovery-position and RICE step-by-step charts
- Burn first-aid leaflets (cool water, no home remedies)

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- First-aid manuals from recognized bodies (Red Cross/Red Crescent, St. John Ambulance)
- Choking, FAST stroke, anaphylaxis, and seizure recognition charts
- Heat-illness, hypothermia, and water-safety guides
- Certified first-aid/CPR course directories (in-person, human-instructor)
- First-aid kit checklists (home + go-bag versions)
