# First Aid Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. This domain teaches awareness and theory — real CPR/first-aid certification requires a certified human instructor; hands-on practice is manikin-only under supervision. This domain never replaces real medical care.

## L0 — Foundations
- What first aid is and what it can/cannot do; when to act vs. when to get an adult
- Local emergency numbers (Bangladesh: 999 national helpline; ambulance/household contacts)
- Assessment: recite emergency numbers + explain "stay safe, call for help, then help"

## L1 — Scene Safety
- Scene survey: check for danger before approaching (traffic, electricity, fire, water)
- Personal protection: gloves/barriers, hand hygiene after helping
- Assessment: walk through 3 staged scenarios and correctly name the dangers first

## L2 — Calling for Help
- What to say on an emergency call: location, what happened, how many hurt, phone number
- Staying on the line, sending a bystander, unlocking the door
- Assessment: role-play a mock call scoring location + situation + calm voice

## L3 — Wounds & Bleeding
- Minor cuts/scrapes: rinse, clean, dress; recognizing wounds needing stitches
- Direct pressure for serious bleeding; nosebleed technique (lean forward, pinch soft part)
- Assessment: demonstrate pressure dressing on a manikin/prop + nosebleed steps

## L4 — CPR & Unresponsiveness Awareness
- Check responsiveness and breathing; shout for help first
- Compression-only CPR theory (rate/depth, hand position) — manikin-only practice
- Recovery position for breathing casualties
- Flag: theory only — real certification needs a certified instructor (Red Crescent, St. John, etc.)
- Assessment: describe the CPR sequence + demonstrate recovery position on a volunteer

## L5 — Burns, Scalds & Fractures — Intermediate milestone
- Burns: cool running water 20 min, never butter/toothpaste/ice; when to seek care
- Fractures/sprains: immobilize, don't straighten, RICE for sprains
- Assessment: burn-response walkthrough + splint explanation on a prop limb

## L6 — Head, Choking & Sudden Illness
- Head-injury warning signs; when "just a bump" needs a doctor
- Choking response awareness (encourage cough → back blows/abdominal thrusts theory)
- Recognizing stroke (FAST), heart attack, seizure — protect from harm, time it, call help
- Assessment: score 6 flash-scenarios with the correct first action

## L7 — Medical Emergencies Awareness
- Asthma attack, allergic reaction/anaphylaxis, diabetic emergency, poisoning
- Reading medicine labels, never giving meds without adult direction
- Assessment: match 8 symptom cards to correct responses

## L8 — Environment & Water Safety
- Heat exhaustion vs. heatstroke; hypothermia signs
- Drowning awareness: reach/throw, don't go; bites and stings basics
- Assessment: heat-casualty scenario walkthrough + water-safety rules list

## L9 — Kit Management & Drills
- Build and maintain a home/go-bag first-aid kit; expiry checks
- Timed scenario drills combining scene safety → call → care
- Assessment: kit assembled to checklist + 3 timed drills passed

## L10 — Mastery — Full Responder Readiness
- Multi-casualty triage basics (help the quiet ones first)
- Teach one full first-aid session to a beginner
- Enroll in a certified human-instructor course (guardian approval)
- Assessment: full scenario test + taught session + certification plan on file
