# First Aid Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Drill the order every time: scene safety → call for help → then care
- Practice mock calls monthly — real emergencies reward rehearsed voices
- Keep a stocked, dated first-aid kit at home and in the go-bag; check expiries quarterly
- Teach-back often: explaining the recovery position cements it faster than reading
- Pair theory with supervised manikin/prop reps — muscle memory matters

## Don't
- Never practice compression CPR on a real person — manikin or prop only
- Don't teach improvised treatments (butter on burns, tourniquets for minor bleeding, tilting head back on nosebleeds)
- Don't present this domain as medical training — it is awareness and preparedness
- Don't skip scene safety to reach the "exciting" skills — danger-check is the first skill, always

## Safety notes
- This domain never replaces real medical care. Any real injury, symptom, or emergency = get an adult and call for help immediately, then flag `## NEXT: human-consult`
- Real emergencies the learner witnesses are logged, not replayed as practice without debrief
- CPR/first-aid certification requires a certified human instructor — this curriculum only prepares for it
- Scenario drills stay low-stress; stop on distress. `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
