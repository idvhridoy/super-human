# Behavior & Etiquette Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path from core manners to principled leadership; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. All content filtered by learner age (`learner/profile.md`).

## L0 — Foundations
- Core etiquette: greetings, please/thank you, excuse me, sorry
- Table manners: utensils, chewing, asking to be excused
- Personal space and hygiene basics: knocking, covering sneezes
- **Unlock:** guardian-observed week of consistent core manners

## L1
- Polite communication: listen without interrupting, take turns
- Asking vs demanding: request forms, accepting "no" gracefully
- Eye contact (culturally appropriate) and attention signals
- **Unlock:** demonstrates turn-taking + accepts a "no" without meltdown

## L2
- Respect and empathy basics: name feelings in self and others
- Kindness practices: sharing, helping, including others
- Recognize how actions affect other people
- **Unlock:** correctly names 6+ emotions; unprompted kind act logged

## L3
- Communication skills: express needs clearly and calmly
- Sincere apology: name the harm, say sorry, make it right
- Phone/digital manners: volume, timing, putting devices away for people
- **Unlock:** one sincere self-initiated apology + calm request under frustration

## L4
- Emotional intelligence: emotion vocabulary beyond happy/sad/angry
- Self-regulation: breathing, pause, walk-away strategies that work for the learner
- Distinguish feelings from actions: all feelings OK, not all actions
- **Unlock:** uses a regulation strategy in a real heated moment (observed)

## L5 — Intermediate milestone
- Social dynamics: read a room — when to speak, joke, stay quiet
- Joining groups: entering games/conversations politely
- Making and keeping friends: reliability, reciprocity, boundaries
- **Unlock:** successfully joins a group activity + sustains a friendship practice

## L6
- Handling disagreement: disagree with the idea, respect the person
- Compromise and fair negotiation; winning/losing gracefully
- Standing up for self and others without aggression
- **Unlock:** resolves a real disagreement respectfully (observed or reported)

## L7
- Digital citizenship: online kindness, no pile-ons, think-before-posting
- Public behavior: restaurants, transport, events, places of worship
- Reputation awareness: consistency between private and public behavior
- **Unlock:** clean digital conduct period + praised public behavior at an outing

## L8
- Conflict resolution: de-escalate, listen first, find win-win
- Mediation basics: help two others reach agreement
- Forgiveness and repair after real conflict
- **Unlock:** mediates or de-escalates a real conflict successfully

## L9
- Leadership: lead by example, include the excluded, share credit
- Responsibility: own mistakes, keep commitments, admit limits
- Encouraging others: specific praise, mentoring a younger peer
- **Unlock:** leads a group activity/task with positive peer feedback

## L10 — Mastery
- Consistently gracious under pressure: calm, fair, kind when tested
- Models integrity: does the right thing unobserved
- Mentors peers in etiquette and conflict skills
- **Unlock:** capstone — sustained multi-setting observation + peer mentoring evidence
