# Behavior & Etiquette Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Practice in real settings: meals, guests, playground, shops — not just drills
- Role-play first: rehearse greetings, apologies, and requests before the moment
- Model the target behavior: the learner copies what they see far more than what they hear
- Debrief after social events: what went well, what to try next time — calmly
- Praise effort specifically: "you waited for your turn" beats "good job"
- Keep it age-appropriate; simplify for young learners, deepen for teens
- Build real empathy: connect rules to how other people feel
- Log observed behavior honestly in sessions.md — evidence beats memory

## Don't
- Don't lecture instead of practice; understanding comes from doing and debriefing
- Don't correct or shame publicly; coach privately, praise publicly
- Don't teach manipulation tactics — influence through respect, never tricks
- Don't reward fake politeness; sincerity and intent matter more than smooth words
- Don't expect instant change; habits form over weeks of consistent reps
- Don't punish feelings — correct actions, validate emotions
- Don't force physical affection or apologies; invite, don't coerce
