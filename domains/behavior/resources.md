# Behavior & Etiquette Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — all picks filtered for learner age by human/guardian.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Picture books and story collections about manners and kindness
- Age-appropriate etiquette primers (table, greetings, guests)
- Emotion charts and feelings flashcards
- Role-play scripts and puppet-play prompts for practice
- Simple family manners checklist for the baseline assessment
- Short videos modeling polite behavior (adult-previewed)

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Conversation starter cards and question games
- Children's books on emotional intelligence and self-regulation
- Apology/repair frameworks written for the learner's age
- Social skills group activities: clubs, team games, group classes
- Guided family discussion prompts for debriefing social events
- Simple journaling templates for reflection entries

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Age-appropriate books on communication and conflict resolution
- Biographies/stories of principled leaders and peacemakers
- Volunteering and community-service opportunities (human-approved)
- Public speaking or leadership clubs (school, community)
- Win-win negotiation games and cooperative problem-solving activities
- Etiquette references for formal settings: ceremonies, interviews, hosting
