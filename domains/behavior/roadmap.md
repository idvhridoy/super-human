# Behavior & Etiquette Roadmap

> Maintained by `@roadmap-planner`. Dates adjusted from evidence, not wishes. Pace adapts to learner age.

## Next 30 days
- [ ] Core manners baseline assessed (guardian checklist: greetings, table, space)
- [ ] Daily greeting practice: hello/goodbye/thank you in real settings
- [ ] Table manners routine practiced at shared meals
- [ ] Family feedback loop started: weekly 1–5 politeness score

## 60 days
- [ ] Listening and turn-taking shown consistently at home
- [ ] First sincere unprompted apology observed and logged
- [ ] Accepts "no" gracefully in ≥3 recorded situations
- [ ] 6+ emotion words used correctly in conversation

## 90 days
- [ ] One self-regulation strategy identified and used in a real moment
- [ ] Joins a group activity politely (observed)
- [ ] Calm request under frustration demonstrated
- [ ] Level check: target L3–L4 depending on age and evidence

## This year
- Reach L5–L6: reads social situations, keeps a friendship practice, resolves a real disagreement respectfully. Stretch: first act of helping/including an excluded peer, unprompted.
