# Kung-Fu Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Warm up joints and legs before every session; cold stances strain knees
- Practice stances daily even without a full session — 10 min holds compound
- Train both sides equally; log weak-side gaps
- Film forms occasionally to self-check posture
- Bow in/out every session — discipline is the curriculum

## Don't
- No head contact, ever, while a minor — drills stop below the shoulders
- No full-power striking on partners; power goes to pads only
- Don't stretch cold or bounce-stretch
- Don't skip stances to get to "fun" techniques — stances are the fun techniques
- No solo sparring improvisation; partner work is pre-arranged drills only

## Safety notes (physical)
- CONTACT RULES (minors): no head/neck contact, no joint locks, no throws without mats + supervision. Controlled, pre-arranged drills only; partner wears pads for body targets.
- Stop signals: any pain (not fatigue), dizziness, or partner taps = stop immediately
- Max session load: RPE 7 while building base; hydration break every 20 min
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
