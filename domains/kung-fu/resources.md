# Kung-Fu Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Illustrated stance guides with common-error diagrams
- Beginner Shaolin/kung-fu basics video series (stance + first strikes)
- Flexibility routine card: hips, hamstrings, shoulders
- Intro book on martial arts etiquette and history

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Step-by-step first-form video tutorials (mirrored, slow)
- Home conditioning plan: bodyweight strength + safe forearm conditioning
- Pad/drill equipment list for home practice
- Flexibility progression program toward kicks

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Form-application (bunkai) breakdown videos
- Sparring rules/theory readings + referee etiquette
- Intermediate/advanced forms catalog for style chosen
- Books on martial philosophy, breath, and training discipline
