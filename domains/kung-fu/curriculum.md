# Kung-Fu Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.
>
> MINOR SAFETY RULE (applies at every level while learner is a minor): no head contact, no full-power striking, controlled drills only. See `playbook.md`.

## L0 — Foundations
- History & etiquette: bow-in/out, school rules, respect protocol
- Basic warm-up: joint rotations, dynamic leg swings
- Assessment: explain etiquette, demonstrate safe warm-up

## L1 — Stances
- Horse stance (ma bu), bow stance (gong bu), cat stance (xu bu), rest stance (xie bu)
- Hold standards: horse 30s, bow 30s each side
- Assessment: hold 4 stances to standard, name them unprompted

## L2 — Basic Strikes & Blocks
- Straight punch, palm strike, rising block, inward block, low block
- Chambering, hip rotation, retraction discipline
- Assessment: 10 clean reps each technique both sides

## L3 — Footwork & Kicks (low)
- Step-slide, cross-step, pivoting
- Front snap kick, low side kick — knee height target only
- Assessment: move across floor combining steps + low kicks with balance

## L4 — First Form (Taolu)
- Learn a beginner short form (e.g., 5-step or basic Shaolin sequence)
- Sequence memory + correct stance transitions
- Assessment: perform full form unassisted, ≤2 errors

## L5 — Conditioning — Intermediate milestone
- Knuckle/wrist prep (soft surface), forearm conditioning (light partner taps), leg flexibility work
- Push-up/squat/plank base: 15 push-ups, 30 squats, 60s plank
- Assessment: conditioning circuit + flexibility check

## L6 — Combinations
- 2–4 strike combos: punch-block-punch, kick-punch, evade-counter
- Pad work with partner/coach — body targets only
- Assessment: 3 combos on pads, fluid at moderate pace

## L7 — Second Form + Application Theory
- Longer form; study each movement's martial meaning (bunkai-style)
- Distance, timing, line of attack theory
- Assessment: form + explain 5 applications

## L8 — Controlled Drills & Sparring Theory
- Pre-arranged partner drills, sensitivity (reaction to touch)
- Sparring THEORY: rules, scoring, range management, etiquette
- Contact: light body contact only, full protective gear, NO head contact (minors)
- Assessment: theory quiz + graded partner drill

## L9 — Third Form + Flow
- Advanced form, breath-intent coordination, rhythm changes
- Chain combos under movement
- Assessment: form graded on power, rhythm, expression

## L10 — Mastery
- Demonstrate complete curriculum, lead a warm-up, coach a beginner drill
- Written + practical exam; ongoing path = style specialization
- Assessment: full curriculum demonstration + teaching test
