# Literature Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path from guided reading to independent criticism; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.

## L0 — Foundations
- Establish reading habit: time, place, age-appropriate material
- Guided reading with mentor/guardian: read along, pause, talk
- Recall basics: who, what, where in a short story
- **Unlock:** retell a short story accurately with 3+ details

## L1
- Short stories and poems independently
- Identify character, setting, plot (beginning/middle/end)
- Reading log started: title, pages, one-line reaction
- **Unlock:** finish 3 short works; log entries for each

## L2
- Genre tour: fiction, poetry, drama, nonfiction, folk/myth
- Summarize a chapter in own words
- Distinguish fact vs opinion in text
- **Unlock:** one finished work in 3 different genres + summaries

## L3
- Longer works: novellas, chapter books
- Theme and motif: spot the recurring idea
- First written responses: 3–5 sentence reactions with one quote
- **Unlock:** finish a novella; written response quoting evidence

## L4
- Full novels at increasing complexity
- Compare characters: motives, growth, relationships
- Support every claim with a quoted passage
- **Unlock:** finish a full novel; character comparison using quotes

## L5 — Intermediate milestone
- Literary analysis essay: thesis + evidence + conclusion
- Identify devices: metaphor, simile, irony, foreshadowing, point of view
- Discuss a book for 15 minutes with reasons, not just likes
- **Unlock:** pass a structured analysis essay on a finished novel

## L6
- Classic literature across eras (age-appropriate selections)
- Critical lenses intro: historical context, author's life, social setting
- Longer essays comparing two works
- **Unlock:** comparative essay on two works from different genres/eras

## L7
- Deeper analysis: symbolism, structure, unreliable narrators
- Read one work of drama and one poetry collection closely
- Annotate actively: margin notes, questions, predictions
- **Unlock:** annotated text submitted; analysis of symbolism defended orally

## L8
- Literary criticism: read short scholarly/popular commentary
- Form independent theses that can disagree with critics
- Trace a theme across a whole body of work (one author or movement)
- **Unlock:** original critical essay citing both text and commentary

## L9
- Advanced writing: polished literary essays near publishable quality
- Original creative work: short story or poem cycle applying studied techniques
- World literature: works in translation, non-Western canon
- **Unlock:** essay + original creative piece reviewed by mentor/human

## L10 — Mastery
- Broad canon familiarity: eras, movements, major voices
- Independent critical voice: reviews, essays, recommendations for others
- Mentor capability: lead a reading discussion for a peer
- **Unlock:** capstone — essay collection or led book discussion series
