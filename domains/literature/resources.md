# Literature Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — specific titles chosen with human/guardian for age-appropriateness.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Illustrated short story collections and graded classics
- Beginner poetry anthologies (rhyme, humor, short forms)
- Folk tales, fables, and myths collections
- Audiobooks for read-along support
- Reading log journal (paper or simple file)
- Age-appropriate award-winner lists for picking titles

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Novellas and novels matched to reading level
- Genre samplers: mystery, sci-fi, historical fiction, biography, drama
- Literary devices glossary/handbook (metaphor, irony, POV, foreshadowing)
- Guided discussion question sets per book
- Book club, reading circle, or family discussion slot
- Essay structure templates for first analysis pieces

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Canonical classics across eras, including works in translation
- Literary criticism primers and accessible scholarly commentary
- Author biographies, interviews, and recorded lectures
- Essay collections and criticism journals (age-appropriate selections)
- Creative writing guides and prompt books for original work
- World literature reading lists (non-Western voices, in translation)
