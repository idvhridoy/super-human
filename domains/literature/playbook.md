# Literature Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Read-then-discuss: every session ends with talking or writing about it
- Keep a reading log: title, pages, one honest line of reaction
- Annotate: underline, question marks, margin notes — engage the page
- Finish before judging; abandon only after a fair chance (50+ pages or mentor approval)
- Mix genres and voices; let learner interest steer selection within level
- Ask open questions: "why did the character do that?" not quiz drills
- Re-read favorites — re-reading is where depth lives

## Don't
- Don't substitute summaries/adaptations for actually reading the text
- Don't force speed over comprehension; slow reading is real reading
- Don't assign only "important classics" — joylessness kills the habit
- Don't interrogate after every page; discuss at natural breaks
- Don't correct every interpretation — build the reasoning habit first
- Don't skip poetry and drama; genres train different muscles
