# Life Skills Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Anchor everything to checklists — a written reset list beats memory every time
- Short daily reps over weekend marathons; the 10-minute tidy is the keystone habit
- Read product labels before first use; ventilate; store chemicals up and away
- Photograph before/after for evidence — it feeds `achievements/` and motivation
- Sequence cleaning top→down, dry→wet, cleanest→dirtiest

## Don't
- Never mix cleaning chemicals (especially bleach with anything) — toxic gas risk
- No irons, ladders, power tools, or electrical work without an adult — age-gated per `learner/profile.md`
- Don't attempt repairs on live electricity, gas fittings, or plumbing beyond a plunger — report and escalate
- Don't let streak-pressure cause lying about completion — honesty rule applies to checklists

## Safety notes (physical)
- Tools: hand tools only at L5 with supervised demos; anything powered or elevated is adult-only
- Cleaning chemicals: gloves/ventilation; eye contact or fumes → flush with water, get adult, flag `## NEXT: human-consult`
- Iron/heat: supervised until explicitly cleared; unplug and cool-down check every session
- Sharps (needles, pins): accounted for before and after sewing
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
