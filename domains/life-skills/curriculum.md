# Life Skills Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. Tools, irons, and cleaning chemicals are age-gated per `learner/profile.md` and introduced under adult supervision.

## L0 — Foundations
- What life skills are: independence ladder, why routines beat willpower
- Daily reset habit: 10-minute tidy of own space
- Assessment: 7-day tidy streak + explain the routine-first principle

## L1 — Laundry Basics
- Sorting (colors/fabrics), care labels, detergent dosing
- Machine use supervised; drying and folding
- Assessment: run one supervised full cycle, correctly sorted and folded

## L2 — Cleaning & Organization
- Dusting, sweeping, mopping order (top→down, dry→wet)
- Cleaning-product safety: read labels, never mix chemicals, ventilate
- Assessment: full room clean to checklist + product safety Q&A

## L3 — Kitchen Clean-Up & Waste
- Dishwashing sequence, counter resets, fridge basics
- Waste sorting and recycling; compost tie-in with `domains/agriculture`
- Assessment: own the post-dinner clean for a week

## L4 — Personal Space Systems
- Wardrobe organization (fold/hang, seasonal rotation), desk setup
- Weekly room reset routine with checklist
- Assessment: 4-week reset streak + before/after evidence

## L5 — Basic Home Maintenance — Intermediate milestone
- Changing bulbs/batteries, plunging a drain, tightening screws — supervised demos first
- Recognizing hazards to report (frayed cords, leaks, gas smell → get adult)
- Assessment: 3 supervised maintenance tasks logged + hazard-spotting walkthrough

## L6 — Sewing & Repairs
- Threading a needle, running/back stitch, sewing a button, simple hem
- Patching and fixing a seam
- Assessment: button + hem + small patch completed neatly

## L7 — Household Admin
- Reading mail/bills awareness, calendar use, appointment basics
- Simple budgeting awareness: where household money goes (ties to finance topics)
- Assessment: manage the family calendar for a week + explain a household bill

## L8 — Fabric Care & Deep Cleaning
- Stain removal by type, ironing supervised, seasonal storage
- Deep-clean protocol: a full-room deep clean including windows/edges
- Assessment: 3 stain types removed + one supervised ironing session + deep clean passed

## L9 — Household Operations Drill
- Run a full weekly household routine: laundry cycle, cleans, resets, admin check — independently
- Assessment: one self-managed week meeting every checklist item

## L10 — Mastery
- Sustain independent personal-space and household contribution long-term
- Teach a full routine to a younger learner/sibling
- Assessment: month-long independent record + taught session
