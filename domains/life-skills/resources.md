# Life Skills Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Printable daily-tidy and room-reset checklists
- Laundry care-label decoder chart
- Beginner cleaning-routine videos (order, technique, timing)
- Cleaning-product safety one-pager (labels, mixing rules, storage)

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Dishwashing and kitchen-reset workflow cards
- Waste-sorting/recycling guide for the local area
- Home-maintenance basics guides (bulbs, plungers, screws, batteries)
- Household hazard-spotting checklist

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Hand-sewing tutorial sets (stitches, buttons, hems, patches)
- Stain-removal reference chart by stain type
- Ironing/fabric-care video guides (supervised practice)
- Household-admin templates: calendar, budget overview, maintenance log
- Deep-clean and seasonal-organization protocols
