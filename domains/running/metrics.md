## Domain-specific KPIs
- Weekly distance (km): total logged volume per week — increase ≤10%/week.
- Longest continuous run (km or min): tracked per week and all-time.
- Easy pace (min/km): rolling average of easy-run pace at talk-test effort.
- Fastest 5k time (min:s): re-tested at level assessments.
- Sessions per week: target 2 (L0–L2) → 3 (L3+).
- Resting heart rate (optional, if device available): monthly trend.
- Assessment scores: RPE at benchmark distance, consistency streaks.
