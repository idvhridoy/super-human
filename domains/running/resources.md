# Running Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Beginner walk-to-run plans: "couch to 5k" style 8–9 week programmes (free apps, printable schedules).
- Video guides: dynamic warm-up routines for runners; proper walking/running posture.
- Explainers: RPE scale and the talk test; how to breathe while running; choosing a first pair of running shoes.
- Exercise library: leg swings, ankle circles, high knees, walking lunges.

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- 5k improvement plans: beginner-to-faster-5k schedules (8 weeks, 3 sessions/week).
- Running-form video series: cadence, foot strike, arm swing, posture drills and strides.
- Guides: the 10% rule and polarised (easy/hard) training; fuelling and hydration for runs under an hour.
- Tools: free GPS run-tracking apps; printable pace charts.

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- 10k and half-marathon training plans with tempo/interval structure.
- Workout libraries: VO2 interval sessions, fartlek formats, hill-repeat protocols, tapering guides.
- Books/channels on endurance physiology: lactate threshold, VO2max, periodization for distance running.
- Strength-for-runners routines (links to `domains/strength/` for execution).
- Trail-running safety and technique primers.
