# Running Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Warm up 5–10 min every session: brisk walk + dynamic moves (leg swings, high knees, ankle circles). Never run hard cold.
- Keep easy runs truly easy — talk test must pass (full sentences without gasping).
- Increase weekly volume by ≤10% and add only one stressor at a time (distance OR speed, not both).
- Take at least 1–2 full rest days per week; hard days never back-to-back at L0–L5.
- Log every session: duration, distance, RPE, how the body felt.
- Hydrate before/after; in heat shift sessions to morning or evening.
- Rotate shoes' surface variety gradually — soft surfaces (grass/track) for early levels.

## Don't
- Don't sprint from a standing start or race every session — most running is slow.
- Don't run through sharp, stabbing, or worsening pain — soreness is fine, injury is not.
- Don't add volume and speed in the same week.
- Don't skip cool-down: 5 min walking + gentle calf/hamstring stretch after every run.
- Don't chase pace targets set by other people — benchmarks are age- and baseline-adjusted.

## Safety notes (if physical)
- Stop signals (end session immediately): chest pain or pressure, dizziness/faintness, sharp joint or bone pain, limping/gait change, unusual breathlessness at easy effort.
- Lingering pain >48h or recurring pain in the same spot → flag `@safety-guardian`, next session becomes rest or walk-only.
- Age-appropriate limits: if `learner/profile.md` age is under ~14, cap continuous distance at ~5k and weekly volume at ~15 km; prioritize play, variety, and form over distance. Long-distance (>10k) and high-volume training wait for growth-plate maturity — human approval required per AGENTS.md guardrails.
- Weather rules: no sessions in thunderstorms, extreme heat, or unsafe air quality; high-visibility clothing in low light.
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
