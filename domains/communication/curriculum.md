# Communication Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path from clear speech to persuasive mastery; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. Scope: speaking, presenting, debating, negotiating, and writing. Politeness, empathy, and emotional-social skills live in `domains/behavior/` — this domain assumes them and builds on top. All content filtered by learner age (`learner/profile.md`).

## L0 — Foundations
- Clear speech: volume, pacing, and pronunciation in everyday talk
- Sentence completeness: finish thoughts, avoid trailing off
- Filler awareness: notice "um/like" without shame yet
- **Unlock:** reads a short passage aloud clearly; guardian can follow a 1-minute story

## L1
- Speaking under attention: self-introduction, answer in full sentences
- Eye contact and facing the listener (culturally appropriate)
- Voice control drills: louder/softer, faster/slower on command
- **Unlock:** delivers a 1-minute self-introduction to a familiar listener, audible and complete

## L2
- Storytelling basics: beginning, middle, end; character and setting
- Retelling: recount a real day or a story just heard, in order
- Detail selection: enough detail to be vivid, not so much it drags
- **Unlock:** tells a 2-minute story with a clear arc that a listener can retell back

## L3
- Presentation structure: hook, point, supporting detail, closing
- Signposting: "first / then / finally" and other transition phrases
- Simple visuals: one prop, drawing, or slide that supports the talk
- **Unlock:** delivers a structured 3-minute talk with a clear opening and closing

## L4
- Public speaking: prepared talk to a small audience (family, class)
- Body basics: posture, gestures, where to look
- Managing nerves: breath, pause, first-line rehearsal
- **Unlock:** delivers a 4–5 minute prepared talk to a real audience; debrief logged

## L5 — Intermediate milestone
- Impromptu speaking: organize 60 seconds of thought on a surprise topic
- Argument basics: claim, reason, evidence — state a position clearly
- Rebuttal fundamentals: hear the other side, respond to the actual point
- **Unlock:** takes a side in a friendly debate; restates the opponent's point fairly before rebutting (steelman)

## L6
- Structured debate: opening, points, rebuttal, closing within time limits
- Evidence quality: fact vs opinion, source awareness, admitting uncertainty
- Listening to respond vs listening to understand
- **Unlock:** completes a timed two-sided debate; judged on structure and fairness, not winning

## L7
- Negotiation basics: positions vs interests — what they want vs why
- Preparation: know your goal, your walk-away, and their likely interests
- Trade language: "if you ... then I ..." — concessions as exchanges, not gifts
- **Unlock:** completes a scripted, then a real low-stakes negotiation (chores, plans, purchases) with a win-win attempt

## L8
- Writing fundamentals: clear paragraphs, one idea each; strong openers and closers
- Written persuasion: thesis, reasons, evidence, counterpoint, call to action
- Revision practice: cut filler, sharpen verbs, read aloud to test flow
- **Unlock:** produces a persuasive piece that a peer or mentor can summarize accurately; revised once on feedback

## L9
- Rhetoric: ethos, pathos, logos — and when each fits the audience
- Audience adaptation: same message tuned for child, peer, adult, authority
- Advanced negotiation and debate: handling pressure, deadlocks, unfair tactics calmly (fairness norms per `domains/behavior/`)
- **Unlock:** adapts one message into three audience versions; wins or draws a judged debate on merit

## L10 — Mastery
- Integrates speaking, debating, negotiating, and writing fluently across settings
- Persuades with integrity: honest evidence, no manipulation (see `domains/behavior/`)
- Coaches a peer or younger learner in a speaking or writing skill
- **Unlock:** capstone — a prepared public talk to a real audience + a published/shared persuasive piece + one real negotiation, all with evidence logged
