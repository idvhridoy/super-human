# Communication Roadmap

> Maintained by `@roadmap-planner`. Dates adjusted from evidence, not wishes. Pace adapts to learner age.

## Next 30 days
- [ ] Baseline recorded: 2-minute audio/video of the learner telling a story
- [ ] Daily clear-speech reps: read-aloud or story-retell, 5 min/day
- [ ] First 1-minute self-introduction delivered to a familiar listener
- [ ] Recording habit started: every speech practice captured for review

## 60 days
- [ ] First structured 3-minute talk (hook/point/detail/close) delivered and debriefed
- [ ] Storytelling rep: 2-minute story with a clear arc, listener retells it back
- [ ] Filler-word count trending down across recordings
- [ ] First impromptu round: 60 seconds on a surprise topic, weekly

## 90 days
- [ ] Prepared 4–5 minute talk delivered to a real small audience
- [ ] First friendly structured debate completed with steelman rebuttal
- [ ] One scripted negotiation role-play + one real low-stakes negotiation attempted
- [ ] Level check: target L4–L5 depending on age and evidence

## This year
- Reach L6–L7: timed two-sided debate, win-win real negotiation, and a revised persuasive written piece. Stretch: a talk to an unfamiliar audience or entry in a school/club speaking event.
