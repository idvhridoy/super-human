# Communication Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — all picks filtered for learner age by human/guardian.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Read-aloud passages and picture books for clear-speech practice
- Simple storytelling prompts and story-cube games
- Short videos modeling confident speaking (adult-previewed)
- Phone/tablet recorder for the recording habit
- Mirror or camera drills for eye contact and facing practice

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Speech-outline templates (hook / point / support / close)
- Slide or poster basics for simple visuals
- Impromptu topic card decks and table-topic lists
- Age-appropriate books on public speaking and storytelling
- Speaking clubs: school presentations, drama, scout/group talks

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Debate-format guides and judged-debate rubrics
- Age-appropriate negotiation books and role-play scenarios
- Persuasive-writing anthologies and essay frameworks
- Rhetoric primers: ethos/pathos/logos for the learner's age
- Speech clubs and competitions (debate team, public speaking contests)
- Recorded great speeches for analysis — structure first, delivery second
