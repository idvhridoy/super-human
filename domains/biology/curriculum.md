# Biology Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.

## L0 — Foundations
- Concepts: characteristics of living things (MRS GREN); living vs non-living; intro to classification; what biologists study
- Skills: apply life-process criteria; sort organisms into broad groups; describe the scientific method
- Unlock: life-criteria worksheet 90%+ and explain how a biologist tests a question

## L1 — Cells
- Concepts: cell theory; plant vs animal cells; organelles (nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplasts, membrane); diffusion and osmosis intro; cell→tissue→organ→system
- Skills: draw and label both cell types; match organelles to functions
- Unlock: labeled cell diagrams from memory + organelle-functions quiz 80%+

## L2 — Cell Division & Reproduction
- Concepts: mitosis stages; growth and repair; asexual vs sexual reproduction; fertilization intro
- Skills: sequence mitosis stages; compare reproduction strategies with examples
- Unlock: mitosis sequencing exercise + reproduction-comparison table at 80%+

## L3 — Genetics & Heredity
- Concepts: DNA, genes, chromosomes; dominant vs recessive; genotype vs phenotype; Punnett squares; variation and mutation intro
- Skills: build and interpret Punnett squares; trace traits through simple family trees
- Unlock: Punnett-square problem set at 80%+

## L4 — Human Body I: Support & Movement
- Concepts: skeletal system (bones, joints); muscular system; how muscles and bones work together; skin intro
- Skills: label major bones and muscle groups; explain antagonistic muscle pairs
- Unlock: body-support systems test at 80%+ with labeled diagrams

## L5 — Human Body II: Life Processes (intermediate milestone)
- Concepts: digestion; circulatory system and heart; respiratory system and gas exchange; excretion; nervous system and senses intro
- Skills: trace a molecule of food and a breath of air through the body; label organ diagrams
- Unlock: integrated body-systems assessment at 80%+

## L6 — Plants
- Concepts: photosynthesis equation and purpose; leaf structure; transport in plants (xylem/phloem); plant reproduction and pollination; tropisms intro
- Skills: write the photosynthesis word equation; label leaf and flower diagrams
- Unlock: plant-biology assessment at 80%+

## L7 — Ecology
- Concepts: ecosystems and habitats; food chains and webs; energy flow and pyramids; nutrient cycles (carbon, water, nitrogen intro); biomes
- Skills: construct a food web from species cards; explain energy loss between trophic levels
- Unlock: food-web construction + nutrient-cycle explanation at 80%+

## L8 — Evolution & Classification
- Concepts: natural selection; adaptation; evidence for evolution (fossils, homologous structures); speciation intro; taxonomy and cladistics intro
- Skills: explain selection with worked examples (peppered moth, antibiotic resistance); use a dichotomous key
- Unlock: evolution-explanation test + classification-key exercise at 80%+

## L9 — Health, Disease & Biotechnology
- Concepts: pathogens (bacteria, viruses, fungi); immune system and antibodies; vaccines; antibiotics and resistance; biotech intro (GM, cloning concepts)
- Skills: explain how vaccines work; reason about antibiotic stewardship; argue both sides of a biotech question
- Unlock: immunity/disease assessment 80%+ and a short biotech-ethics essay

## L10 — Mastery
- Concepts: integrated systems thinking (cell → organism → population → ecosystem); experimental design and data interpretation
- Skills: interpret experimental graphs and tables; design a fair test on paper; teach-back of one advanced topic
- Unlock: comprehensive exam at 85%+ and capstone presentation linking three levels of biological organization
