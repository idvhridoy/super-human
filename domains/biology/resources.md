# Biology Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Intro biology textbook chapters on life processes and cells
- Free video course series covering cell structure and division (animated)
- Interactive cell-viewer and virtual-microscope simulations
- Printable labeling worksheets: plant/animal cells, mitosis stages
- Nature-observation sketchbook + field notebook prompts

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- General biology textbook (genetics and human-body chapters)
- Punnett-square and inheritance problem generators with solutions
- Interactive body-system simulators (heart, digestion, lungs)
- Vocabulary flashcard deck with spaced repetition
- Documentary series on human biology and natural history (curated episodes)

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Comprehensive biology textbook (plants, ecology, evolution, disease, biotech chapters)
- Ecology simulations: food-web builders, population dynamics models
- Past-exam question banks emphasizing data/graph interpretation
- Classification-key and field-guide practice sets
- Teach-back rubric + capstone presentation template for L10
