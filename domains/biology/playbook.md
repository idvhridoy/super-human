# Biology Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Draw every structure from memory, then check against a reference — diagrams are the core skill
- Think in levels: always link cell → tissue → organ → system → organism
- Keep a weekly nature-observation entry (plants, insects, birds — sketch + one question)
- Learn vocabulary in spaced bursts (20 terms/week, review old terms first)
- Trace processes as journeys: a bite of food through digestion, a breath through gas exchange
- Use one worked example per abstract idea (peppered moths for selection, vaccine for immunity)
- Quiz-first sessions: recall before re-reading

## Don't
- Don't memorize definitions without a labeled diagram or example attached
- Don't learn body systems in isolation — always connect inputs/outputs between systems
- Don't skip genetics math — Punnett squares need reps like any skill
- Don't cram taxonomy lists; learn organisms through observation and keys
- Don't binge-study before assessments; biology sticks through spaced recall

## Safety notes
- Outdoor observation only — look and sketch, never touch unknown organisms
- Wash hands after handling plants, soil, or outdoor surfaces
- No dissection, culturing, or biological sampling at home — simulations and videos instead
- Never eat wild plants/fungi as part of any activity
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes
