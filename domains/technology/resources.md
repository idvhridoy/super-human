# Technology Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- "How everyday things work" illustrated books/videos (chip, battery, screen explainers)
- Interactive binary/computer-basics visualizations and unplugged activities
- Simple device teardown videos (battery-powered only) for guided "inside the box" tours
- Input→process→output worksheets and tech-scavenger-hunt printables

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Animated "how the internet works" series: packets, routers, servers, undersea cables
- Cloud-computing explainers aimed at beginners (what a data center is, where files live)
- Diagram templates for mapping a message's full journey end-to-end
- Guided smart-home / IoT case studies (thermostat, wearable, voice assistant)

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- AI/ML concept explainers without math: what training data is, why models make mistakes
- Hype-cycle and tech-adoption explainers; "evaluating gadgets" review-channel exemplars with critical-viewing notes
- Product-teardown and iFixit-style repairability resources
- Personal tech-radar templates and example radars from engineering orgs
- Privacy/data-trade-off explainers: what "free" services actually cost
