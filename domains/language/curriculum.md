# Language Mastery Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. Target-language-agnostic framework — the learner picks the target language at first run; all levels below apply to any language. Levels list skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.

## L0 — Foundations
- Pick target language (with human/guardian approval); record in progress.md
- Learn the writing system: alphabet/script characters, stroke order if applicable
- Sound inventory: phonemes, tones/accents, mouth positions
- 20 survival phrases: greetings, yes/no, please/thank you
- **Unlock:** read/write the full script; pronounce the sound inventory; greet a partner

## L1
- Core vocabulary: 500 most frequent words (people, food, home, time, actions)
- Numbers, days, pronouns, demonstratives
- Sentence frames: "I am ___", "I want ___", "Where is ___"
- **Unlock:** 500-word check ≥80%; use 10 sentence frames correctly

## L2
- Present tense conjugation/structure; word order rules
- Question formation; negation
- Describe self, family, daily routine in simple sentences
- **Unlock:** write 10 correct original sentences; answer 5 spoken questions

## L3
- Past and future tenses; common irregular forms
- ~1500 active words; connectors (and, but, because, then)
- Slow-listening comprehension of short dialogues
- **Unlock:** narrate yesterday/tomorrow in speech; understand a slow 2-min dialogue

## L4
- Everyday conversation: shopping, directions, requests, opinions
- ~2500 words; politeness registers
- Write short paragraphs (postcard, message, diary entry)
- **Unlock:** hold a guided 5-min conversation; write a coherent 8-sentence paragraph

## L5 — Intermediate milestone
- Hold a 10-min unscripted conversation on familiar topics
- Read graded texts and summarize them
- ~3500 words; common idioms introduced
- **Unlock:** graded-reader summary ≥80% accurate; 10-min conversation assessment passed

## L6
- Complex grammar: conditionals, hypotheticals, subjunctive-equivalent structures
- ~4500 words; podcasts/lessons at reduced speed
- Formal vs informal register switching
- **Unlock:** follow a learner podcast at ~80% speed; produce correct conditional sentences

## L7
- Fluency in familiar topics; film/TV with target-language subtitles
- Essay writing: opinion, comparison, narration
- ~5500 words; phrasal/collocation patterns
- **Unlock:** summarize a film episode accurately; write a structured 200-word essay

## L8
- Abstract discussion: values, plans, hypotheticals, culture
- ~6500+ words; native-speed media (news, podcasts)
- Recognize and use idioms, humor, register shifts
- **Unlock:** comprehend native news segment; debate a familiar topic for 5 min

## L9
- Near-native comprehension; read real literature/newspapers unaided
- Persuade, negotiate, tell stories with nuance
- Correct self in real time; rare vocabulary gaps only
- **Unlock:** read a short story and analyze it; hold a 20-min free conversation

## L10 — Mastery
- C1/C2-equivalent: effortless conversation on any topic
- Nuanced writing: tone, style, audience control
- Cultural fluency: humor, references, etiquette in-language
- **Unlock:** pass an external-equivalent proficiency assessment; mentor a beginner
