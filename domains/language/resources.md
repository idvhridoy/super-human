# Language Mastery Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — specific picks filled after target language is chosen.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Beginner structured course or app for the target language
- Alphabet/script charts and pronunciation/phonetic guides
- Frequency word list (top 500–1000 words) for the language
- Spaced-repetition flashcard tool for daily vocab review
- Simple phrasebook with audio
- Slow beginner-level listening material (learner podcasts, slow news)

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Graded readers matched to vocabulary size
- Compact grammar reference book for the language
- Learner podcasts and subtitled beginner videos
- Language-exchange partner or tutor (scheduled, human-approved)
- Learner's dictionary with example sentences
- Writing prompts: diary entries, postcards, short descriptions

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Native podcasts, radio, and audiobooks
- Films/series with target-language subtitles, then none
- Newspapers, magazines, and blogs in the language
- Native literature: short stories → novels
- Advanced grammar and style/usage guides
- Immersion options: communities, events, travel (human-approved)
