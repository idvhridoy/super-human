# Language Mastery Roadmap

> Maintained by `@roadmap-planner`. Dates adjusted from evidence, not wishes.

## Next 30 days
- [ ] Pick target language (human/guardian approval) and record in progress.md
- [ ] Learn the writing system: full alphabet/script + sound inventory
- [ ] First 100 core words via spaced repetition, 15 min/day minimum
- [ ] Master 20 survival phrases; first greeting exchange logged

## 60 days
- [ ] 400 cumulative words; present-tense sentence frames in use
- [ ] Complete first simple guided dialogue (self-introduction)
- [ ] Numbers, days, and time expressions fluent
- [ ] 30 sessions logged; streak ≥14 days at least once

## 90 days
- [ ] 800 cumulative words; past + future tense basics
- [ ] Hold a 5-min guided conversation on daily routine
- [ ] Write a 6–8 sentence paragraph unaided
- [ ] Level check: target L2 complete, approaching L3

## This year
- Reach L4–L5: unscripted 10-min conversation, first graded reader finished, ~3500 words. Stretch: first real-world exchange (pen-pal, partner, or community event).
