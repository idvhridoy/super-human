## Domain-specific KPIs
- Map recall: countries correctly labeled on blank map (target 30 → full world).
- Source diversity: distinct outlets/perspectives used per story (target ≥3, spanning viewpoints).
- Country briefs written: count + mentor review score (1–5).
- Regional profiles completed: of 7 target regions.
- Frameworks applied: realism / liberalism / constructivism analyses written.
- Forecast log: count, stated probabilities, calibration result at review (how many % predictions came true vs claimed).
- Weekly news-review streak: consecutive weeks of the 3-source habit.
