# Geopolitics Roadmap

> Maintained by `@roadmap-planner`. Dates adjusted from evidence, not wishes.

## Next 30 days
- [ ] Complete L0: label a blank world map with 30+ correct answers.
- [ ] Learn the core vocabulary: state, nation, government, border, sovereignty.
- [ ] Establish the source-diversity habit: every story read from at least 2 outlets with different perspectives.
- [ ] Finish L1 power-factor study; write the two-country comparison table.
- [ ] Reach L2 by explaining one live international story from each side's stated interests.

## 60 days
- [ ] Complete the L3 region survey; 3 regional profiles written and reviewed.
- [ ] Diagram one institution (UN Security Council recommended) and one real decision it made.
- [ ] Maintain a weekly "3-source news review" habit for 6+ consecutive weeks.
- [ ] Sit the L5 mock: draft a first full country brief (mentor-reviewed).

## 90 days
- [ ] Complete L4 and L5: 3 country briefs published to standard; unlock "Country Analyst".
- [ ] Start L6: analyze one event through two competing frameworks.
- [ ] Log 5 dated forecasts with probabilities to seed the calibration record.
- [ ] Milestone check with `@orchestrator`: adjust pace for sessions/week actually achieved.

## This year
- Reach L7–L8: economics of power + scenario forecasting with a working forecast log.
- Pick one home-region deep-dive topic (e.g., South Asia / Bay of Bengal) as the L9 candidate.
