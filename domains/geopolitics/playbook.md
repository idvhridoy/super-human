# Geopolitics Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Core framing (hard rule)
- Always age-appropriate, non-partisan, multiple-perspective. We study *how* power works — we do not pick teams.
- Every contested topic must present at least two credible perspectives, steel-manned, before any conclusion.
- Distinguish "what a state says it wants" from "what analysts think it wants" — log both.

## Do
- Use a minimum of 3 sources per story, spanning different countries/outlooks; note each source's likely bias.
- Keep a dated forecast log with probabilities — the skill is calibration, not being right once.
- Anchor every abstract concept to a map: always know *where* the story happens and who borders whom.
- Prefer primary sources (official statements, treaty text, data) over commentary when possible.
- Ask "what would the other side say?" before finishing any analysis.
- Revisit old analyses after events unfold; write a one-line postmortem.

## Don't
- Don't adopt a single outlet's framing — no source monoculture, including "our side" media.
- Don't treat any framework (realism, liberalism, etc.) as truth; they are lenses.
- Don't use atrocity footage or graphic content — strategy, not spectacle. Escalate disturbing topics to guardian/mentor framing.
- Don't forecast without writing down assumptions and a probability.
- Don't present opinion as fact; label confidence levels (high / medium / low).
- Don't debate to win — analyze to understand.

## Safety notes
- Content filter: geopolitics involves war, conflict, and propaganda themes. Keep materials age-appropriate per `learner/profile.md`; route sensitive content questions to the human guardian.
