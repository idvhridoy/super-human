# Geopolitics Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — specific titles/URLs added after librarian review (never invent links).

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Blank outline world maps and printable map quizzes.
- Beginner atlas / illustrated geography reference for young readers.
- Intro explainer video series on how countries, borders, and governments work.
- A short "what is geopolitics" primer written for students.
- Weekly news habit kit: one children's/youth news service + one international wire-style outlet + one explainer outlet.

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Region-by-region survey books or course series covering each world region's geography and politics.
- Official websites/orientation pages of the UN, regional blocs, and major treaty bodies.
- Country profile databases (economy, military, demographics) for building country briefs.
- Interactive thematic maps: trade flows, energy, population density, military spending.
- Age-appropriate documentary series on modern history and international relations.

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- An accessible textbook or lecture series on international relations theory (realism, liberalism, constructivism).
- Think-tank analysis feeds from multiple countries — used to compare framings of the same event.
- Data portals: trade, sanctions, energy, defense spending statistics.
- Scenario-planning and forecasting guides; a structured forecasting journal template.
- Primary document collections: treaty texts, official speeches, summit communiqués.
- Media-literacy and propaganda-analysis references for the bias/source-critical strand.
