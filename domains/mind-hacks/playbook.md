# Mind Hacks Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Practice tools in calm moments first — a protocol that only exists on paper won't fire under pressure
- Name the state before fixing it — noticing is half the intervention
- Keep a decision journal: call, reasoning, confidence — review monthly for honest calibration
- When a persuasion attempt is spotted, pause and name the lever — the label breaks the spell
- Match tool to arousal: too wired → down-regulate; too flat → up-regulate; don't calm yourself into sleepiness before a sprint
- Debrief every real high-stakes event: what fired, what froze, what to change
- Escalate when overwhelmed — telling a human IS a mind hack, not a failure
- Keep the ethics frame visible: these tools are for *your* mind — using them to pressure others is out of bounds

## Don't
- NEVER use these techniques to manipulate other people — no pressure levers, no manufactured urgency, no exploiting someone's biases; detection and defense only
- Don't use breathwork while swimming, driving, bathing, or anywhere fainting would be dangerous — seated/standing, safe place only
- Don't push through breath-holds or rapid breathing if dizzy or tingling — stop, breathe normally, sit down
- Don't treat suppression as mastery — the goal is working *with* the state, not pretending it away
- Don't stack tools infinitely — if 2–3 calm attempts fail, the answer is pause/escalate, not a fourth technique
- Don't use decision shortcuts on irreversible or high-stakes calls — speed is for the reversible
- Don't weaponize "bias-spotting" in arguments — these tools audit *your* thinking, not score points against others
- Distress, panic, or mood flags produce `## NEXT: human-consult` — this domain is performance tactics, never therapy

## Safety notes (if physical)
- Breathing drills: seated or standing only; never before/during water activities or vehicle operation; stop on dizziness, tingling, or chest discomfort
- Pressure simulations must stay designed and mild — real stakes should come from real life, not engineered distress
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes
