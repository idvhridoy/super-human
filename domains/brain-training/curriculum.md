# Brain Training Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.
> Method: spaced, playful, benchmarked. We train real cognitive skills — memory, attention, speed, logic, metacognition — not app scores.

## L0 — Foundations
- Skills: baseline battery — digit span, word-list recall, symbol-search speed, simple attention task.
- Theory: how memory works — encode → store → retrieve; why attention is the gatekeeper of memory.
- Assessment: complete the baseline battery; results logged in `metrics.md` as the reference point.

## L1 — Memory Palace (Method of Loci)
- Skills: build a first memory palace from a familiar building; place and retrieve 10 items.
- Theory: why spatial/visual encoding beats rote repetition; vivid-imagery rules (exaggerate, animate, interact).
- Assessment: memorize and recall a shuffled 10-item list in order, twice, 24h apart, ≥90% both times.

## L2 — Pegs & Chunking
- Skills: learn a 10–20 item peg system; apply chunking to numbers and lists.
- Theory: working-memory limits (~4 chunks); grouping, acronyms, and story-linking as compression.
- Assessment: digit span improves +2 vs baseline; recall a 15-item list using pegs at ≥85%.

## L3 — Attention Training
- Skills: single-task focus blocks (10–15 min), distraction logging, guided breathing before study.
- Theory: selective vs sustained attention; context-switching costs; attention as a trainable muscle.
- Assessment: hold a 15-minute focus block with ≤2 logged distractions, 5 days in a row.

## L4 — Processing Speed
- Skills: timed symbol/number tasks, rapid naming, mental math sprints — trained in short bursts.
- Theory: speed vs accuracy trade-off; automaticity; why sleep and practice both drive speed gains.
- Assessment: 15%+ improvement vs baseline on two different speed tasks without accuracy loss.

## L5 — Intermediate milestone: Benchmark Suite
- Skills: run the full self-test battery unaided; chart progress vs baseline.
- Theory: transfer — training must show up outside the drill; how to spot real gains vs practice effects.
- Assessment: full benchmark re-run; ≥3 of 5 metrics improved vs baseline; learner explains why.

## L6 — Logic & Puzzles
- Skills: solve graded logic puzzles — pattern sequences, syllogisms, grid logic, lateral puzzles.
- Theory: deduction vs induction; common reasoning traps (confirmation bias, jumping to the obvious answer).
- Assessment: solve 8/10 graded puzzles within time limits, with written reasoning (not just answers).

## L7 — Working Memory
- Skills: n-back-style tasks, mental arithmetic chains, dual-task drills — short, spaced sessions.
- Theory: working memory as mental workspace; limits of brain-game hype — what transfers and what doesn't.
- Assessment: +1 level sustained on chosen working-memory task; apply WM strategy to one real task (e.g., mental math, multi-step instructions).

## L8 — Metacognition
- Skills: prediction journals for own performance; error logs; identify which strategies work for *this* learner.
- Theory: thinking about thinking — calibration, illusion-of-competence, when to re-study vs move on.
- Assessment: predicted vs actual scores tracked for 2 weeks; calibration gap narrows measurably.

## L9 — Integrated Protocol
- Skills: design a personal weekly cognitive-training plan mixing memory/attention/speed/logic, spaced correctly.
- Theory: spaced repetition vs massed practice; interleaving; deliberate practice principles applied to the mind.
- Assessment: run a self-designed 4-week protocol; present adherence + benchmark delta.

## L10 — Mastery
- Skills: maintain an autonomous, evidence-driven training practice; teach one technique to someone else.
- Theory: synthesis — the learner knows their own cognitive profile, tools, and limits.
- Assessment: capstone — teach a memory technique to a peer successfully + full battery shows durable gains vs L0 baseline + written personal training playbook.
