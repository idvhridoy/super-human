# Brain Training Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Core method
- Spaced practice beats massed practice: short sessions most days > one long cram. Default dose: 15–20 min, 4–5 days/week.
- Everything is benchmarked: no claim of improvement without a number vs baseline.

## Do
- Re-run the full baseline battery every ~6–8 weeks; transfer is the real test, not drill scores.
- Space recall tests — same list at 10 min and 24h is more informative than immediate recall.
- Keep imagery vivid: memory-palace placements should be exaggerated, moving, multisensory.
- Protect accuracy while chasing speed — speed gains with error spikes don't count.
- Log distractions during focus blocks; the log itself is part of the training.
- Coordinate with `@sleep-guardian` — memory consolidates in sleep; training on sleep debt wastes effort.
- End sessions while it is still fun; leave the learner wanting the next one.

## Don't
- Don't train through fatigue, illness, or frustration — one bad session teaches the brain the wrong lesson.
- Don't chase app/drill scores as the goal; measure transfer to real tasks (schoolwork, instructions, mental math).
- Don't stack more than ~25 min/day of deliberate drill — diminishing returns and burnout risk.
- Don't skip the 24-hour recall — same-day scores flatter the technique.
- Don't introduce a new technique before the current one has a passing assessment.
- Don't promise medical/cognitive outcomes — this is skill training, not therapy. Health concerns → `## NEXT: human-consult`.

## Safety notes
- Stop signals: headaches, eye strain, frustration spirals, or dread of sessions → pause, log, and shorten sessions.
- Wellbeing over metrics: a missed streak is never worth a miserable learner — resume light, not punitive.
