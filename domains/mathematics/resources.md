# Mathematics Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Free video course playlists covering arithmetic through fractions (Khan-Academy-style)
- Printable worksheet packs: place value, four operations, times-table drills
- Math games: number-line races, fraction puzzles, mental-math card games
- Concrete manipulatives: base-ten blocks, fraction tiles/circles, abacus
- Flashcard deck for times tables (spaced repetition)

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Pre-algebra and algebra-1 textbook series with worked examples
- Interactive graphing tools (Desmos-style) for lines, systems, transformations
- Geometry construction guides: compass/straightedge exercises
- Word-problem collections graded by difficulty
- Puzzle books: Sudoku, KenKen, logic grids for reasoning stamina

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Algebra-II/precalculus textbook (functions, logs, trig, limits)
- Statistics workbook + real datasets for analysis practice (sports, weather, finance)
- Calculus course: video lectures + graded problem sets
- AoPS-style problem books and past competition papers for L10 stretch problems
- Proof-writing guide with worked examples (induction, contradiction)
