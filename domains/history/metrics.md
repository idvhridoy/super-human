## Domain-specific KPIs
- Sources analyzed: count of primary/secondary sources worked through the full analysis checklist.
- Timelines built: count + correctness rate on ordered-event exercises.
- Essays/reports written: count + mentor review score (1–5).
- Dual/multi-perspective accounts: events analyzed from 2+ viewpoints (target: every contested topic).
- Eras covered: checklist of L1–L8 blocks completed.
- Cause diagrams: multi-cause maps completed with long/short/trigger categories.
- Journal streak: consecutive weeks with ≥1 timeline + ≥1 source note.
