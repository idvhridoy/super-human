# History Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — specific titles/URLs added after librarian review (never invent links).

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Illustrated world-history atlas or timeline wall chart for young readers.
- Beginner "what is history / how do we know" explainer books or videos.
- Printable blank timelines and outline maps (ancient world focus).
- Child-friendly documentary series on ancient civilizations.
- Hands-on archaeology-style activities: artifact guessing games, mock dig exercises.

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Narrative world-history survey written for students (multi-region, not Europe-only).
- Primary-source readers: age-edited letters, inscriptions, explorer accounts, photographs.
- Historical atlas covering medieval and early modern trade/empire maps.
- Source-analysis worksheet packs (origin/purpose/reliability structure).
- Museum virtual-tour collections and artifact databases.
- Age-appropriate biographies of figures from multiple regions and roles.

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Document collections for the world wars and modern era (speeches, treaties, diaries).
- Introductory historiography essays — "how historians disagree" overviews.
- Reference works: historical dictionaries, chronology companions, academic survey texts.
- Oral-history project guides for interviewing family/community members.
- Archive and library search guides; digital collections of national archives.
- Documentary series with companion source material, used critically against written sources.
