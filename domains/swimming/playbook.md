# Swimming Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- ALWAYS swim with a lifeguard on duty; while `learner/profile.md` marks the learner as a ward/minor, a responsible adult must also be poolside — never unsupervised, ever.
- Warm up on deck (arm circles, shoulder rolls, light stretch) then 2–4 easy lengths before any hard work.
- Learn breathing and body position before chasing speed or distance — technique first, always.
- Rest between sets; exit the water to rest if breathing feels uncontrolled.
- Hydrate — you sweat in the pool even though you can't feel it.
- Log distance, stroke count, and drill results after every session.
- Practice safe entries; depth beyond standing level only after the corresponding level is unlocked.
- Shower and rinse goggles/suit after each session; check pool schedule and lane rules before going.

## Don't
- Never swim alone — no solo sessions at any level, no exceptions for minors.
- No breath-holding games or underwater-distance contests (shallow-water blackout risk).
- No running on deck, no diving in shallow water, no pushing/dunking others.
- Don't push through panic, gulping water, or exhaustion — get to the wall and exit.
- No open-water (river/sea) practice without explicit human approval and certified supervision — open water is theory-only at L0–L8.
- Don't skip the cool-down lengths after a hard set.

## Safety notes (if physical)
- Stop signals (exit water immediately): panic or loss of breathing control, choking/water inhalation, cramp, dizziness, blue lips or shivering that doesn't stop, ear pain, coughing that persists.
- Cold-water signs (shivering, clumsy hands, slowed movement) end the session even if distance targets aren't met.
- Ear infections, skin irritation, or asthma-like breathing issues → flag `@safety-guardian` + human consult.
- Flip turns and deep-water skills only after mentor clearance at the right level; never practice them unsupervised.
- Age-appropriate limits: minors cap sessions at ~45–60 min in water; no hypoxic/breath-hold training at any age.
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
