# Swimming Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.

## L0 — Foundations
- Skills: safe pool entry/exit; walking in chest-deep water; face in water + blowing bubbles; supported front/back float.
- Theory: pool rules, why we never swim alone, recognizing the lifeguard, shallow vs deep end.
- Unlock assessment: relaxed face submersion with bubble-blowing + 10 s supported float on front and back.

## L1
- Skills: unassisted front and back float 10 s; stand up safely from a float; push-glide 3–5 m from wall.
- Theory: buoyancy — why lungs full = floating; streamlined body position.
- Unlock assessment: 5 m push-glide + safe return to standing, 3× in a row.

## L2
- Skills: flutter kick with kickboard 10 m; basic freestyle arm action standing then moving; kick on back 10 m.
- Theory: propulsion basics — kick vs pull; exhaling underwater, inhaling to the side.
- Unlock assessment: 10 m propulsion (kickboard-assisted ok) without touching bottom.

## L3
- Skills: freestyle arms + kick combined with side breathing; 15–25 m with brief rest mid-way if needed.
- Theory: breathing rhythm — every 3 strokes intro; body roll concept.
- Unlock assessment: 15 m recognizable freestyle with at least one proper side breath.

## L4
- Skills: 25 m continuous freestyle; backstroke basics — straight-leg kick, alternating arms; safe deep-water entry (age/ability appropriate).
- Theory: pacing vs sprinting; lane etiquette.
- Unlock assessment: 25 m continuous, any stroke, without wall or floor contact.

## L5 — Intermediate milestone
- Skills: 50 m continuous freestyle with side breathing both sides; tread water 1 min; backstroke 25 m.
- Theory: bilateral breathing; sets and rest intervals; what "continuous" really means.
- Unlock assessment: 50 m non-stop + 60 s treading water.

## L6
- Skills: technique refinement — catch-up drill, fingertip drag, streamlining off the wall; open turn intro; breaststroke kick basics.
- Theory: catch/pull phases; drag reduction; why drills isolate skills.
- Unlock assessment: 100 m continuous freestyle + 25 m breaststroke kick.

## L7
- Skills: endurance sets (4×50 m, 8×25 m); flip turn intro (age-appropriate, shallow only after clearance); deep-water comfort.
- Theory: interval rest management; negative-split pacing in the pool.
- Unlock assessment: 400 m continuous swim.

## L8
- Skills: full breaststroke (legal kick + timing); elementary backstroke; butterfly intro (dolphin kick + arm recovery); 200 m mixed sets.
- Theory: stroke order in individual medley; efficient turns; counting strokes per length.
- Unlock assessment: 4×100 m set completed or 600 m continuous.

## L9
- Skills: 1 km continuous; pace-clock use; sighting and open-water theory (safety-first, pool simulation only unless approved).
- Theory: aerobic pacing over distance; open-water safety rules; drafting concept.
- Unlock assessment: 1000 m continuous swim.

## L10 — Mastery
- Skills: 1.5–2 km continuous; recognizable versions of all four strokes; writes own swim workouts.
- Theory: water-safety and basic rescue awareness; training structure (warm-up/drills/main set/cool-down).
- Unlock assessment: 2000 m continuous + water-safety knowledge check + coached/demo'd one drill to a learner.
