## Domain-specific KPIs
- Distance per session (m): total metres swum, logged per session.
- Longest continuous swim (m): tracked per week and all-time.
- 25 m / 50 m time (s): re-tested at level assessments.
- Stroke count per 25 m: lower at same speed = better efficiency.
- Treading-water duration (s): safety benchmark.
- Technique drill list: drills completed vs curriculum list per level (floats, glides, catch-up, fingertip drag, breaststroke kick, dolphin kick, turns).
- Strokes known: count of recognizable strokes (target 4 by L10).
- Sessions per week: target 1–2.
- Supervision confirmation: every session logged with supervising adult/lifeguard while learner is a ward/minor.
