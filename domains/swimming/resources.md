# Swimming Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Water-confidence guides: parent/guardian-friendly introductions to bubbles, floats, and glides.
- Video series: learn-to-swim basics — submersion, floating, standing up from a float, push-glide.
- Equipment guides: choosing goggles, kickboards, swim caps.
- Safety reading: pool rules and why supervision matters (guardian reads too).

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Freestyle technique video courses: body position, side breathing, arm recovery, timing drills.
- Drill libraries: catch-up drill, fingertip drag, kickboard sets, 6-kick switch.
- Beginner workout plans: 4–8 week "first 50 m" and "first 100 m" programmes.
- Backstroke intro tutorials; treading-water technique guides.

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Stroke clinics on video: breaststroke timing, dolphin kick, turn technique (open + flip).
- Endurance plans: building to 400 m, 1 km, and 2 km continuous; interval-set structures with pace clock.
- Books/guides on swim training: aerobic sets, drill progressions, stroke-count efficiency.
- Open-water safety primers (theory): sighting, currents, buddy rules — practice only with explicit approval.
- Water-safety and basic rescue-awareness courses (nationally recognised programmes preferred).
