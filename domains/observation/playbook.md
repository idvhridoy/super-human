# Observation Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Run the entrance scan every time: exits, people, layout, mood, hazards — 5 seconds, relaxed
- Learn the baseline first — you can't spot the anomaly in a scene you never learned
- Label inferences as inferences — "the chair is moved" is observation; "someone was here" is a guess
- Recall-test yourself after leaving — the attempt is the training, not the score
- Read people in clusters (posture + orientation + pace + mood), never from one cue
- Monitor your own state — tired, stressed, or distracted = degraded observer
- Log everything — noticing without recording is entertainment; the log is the skill
- Keep the habit invisible — awareness that looks like calm, not scanning-for-danger theater
- Debrief anomalies: what was off, how you verified, what the outcome was

## Don't
- Never surveil people — no following, staring, photographing strangers, or keeping tabs on private individuals; that is creepy and may be illegal
- Don't observe private spaces or private matters — rooms and shared scenes, yes; people's personal business, no
- Don't narrate your reads aloud about real people — "I can tell she's lying" is overclaiming and rude
- Don't treat body language as mind-reading — cues suggest state, never prove intent
- Don't let anomaly-hunting become alarmism — most anomalies are benign; verify calmly
- Don't run observation reps while compromised — phone-in-hand walking sessions don't count
- Don't practice recall on distressing scenes — flag and disengage; `## NEXT: human-consult` if needed
- Don't perform awareness to impress — quiet noticing, smooth behavior

## Safety notes
- People-observation red lines: consented/family settings and public groups only; never follow, record, or collect on individuals; any safeguarding concern observed → tell a trusted adult, never investigate alone
- Physical: moving-observation drills only in safe areas; awareness never replaces looking both ways — traffic and safety rules stay primary
