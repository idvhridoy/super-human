# Observation Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Inattentional-blindness explainers and classic selective-attention experiments
- Kim's game / memory-tray drill guides and printable image sets
- Spot-the-difference and detail-hunt puzzle collections
- Intro material on situational baselines — exits, norms, hazards, "what normal looks like"

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Deduction-style observation exercises: scene photos with guided "what can you tell?" prompts
- Body-language primers focused on cue clusters and their limits (baseline, context, no mind-reading)
- Scene-recall protocol templates and blind-recall scoring sheets
- Nature/urban observation field guides — tracks, wear patterns, change detection

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Moving-awareness training material: scanning rhythm, layered attention, position tracking
- Anomaly-detection exercises and baseline-vs-anomaly case studies
- Vigilance and attention-fatigue research summaries for self-learners
- Multi-layer observation frameworks from civilian fieldcraft/security-awareness training (ethical material only)
- Capstone field-test kits: staged-change scenarios for live accuracy testing
