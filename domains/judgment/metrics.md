## Domain-specific KPIs
- Decision journal entries (count) — with options, evidence, confidence, review date
- Calibration score (%) — stated confidence vs actual hit rate over rolling predictions
- Decision files completed (count) — facts / claims / unknowns separated and weighted
- Mental-model passes (count) — inversion / second-order / opportunity-cost analyses logged
- Adjudications (count) — contested questions resolved with written verdicts
- Verdicts updated (count) — decisions revised on new evidence (a good sign, not a failure)
- Pre-mortems (count) — high-stakes decisions with failure-mode analysis
- Capstone audit completeness (%) — process checklist score on the L10 audit
