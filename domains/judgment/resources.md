# Judgment Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Intro texts and explainer videos on decision basics: choice, stakes, uncertainty
- Evidence-sorting worksheets — fact vs claim vs unknown practice sets
- "What would change your mind?" prompt cards and discussion exercises
- Simple decision-journal templates with options / evidence / confidence fields

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Mental-model primers: inversion, second-order thinking, opportunity cost — with worked examples
- Probability-literacy intros: expected value, base rates, thinking in ranges (pairs with `mathematics/`)
- Decision-journal guides and worked examples from judgment-focused books
- Case-study collections: real decisions dissected (business, history, medicine) with known outcomes

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Calibration-training tools and prediction-scoring methods (Brier-style scoring)
- Forecasting practice platforms and scored prediction journals
- Pre-mortem and risk-analysis frameworks with templates
- Adjudication case libraries: contested questions with documented evidence both ways
- Classic decision-science and judgment texts for self-learners, plus bias-correction research summaries
- Capstone audit templates: evidence table, model-pass log, confidence record, outcome review
