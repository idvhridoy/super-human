# Body Engineering Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The flagship physical domain — the engineering layer that audits the human machine, then applies proven training levers so the body exceeds its normal operating limits. Frame: a normal body has limits; a superhuman body is *engineered* to exceed them — "commandos run 40–50 km where a regular soldier runs 12."
>
> Method: **ASSESS (L0–L1) → ENGINEER (L2–L6) → EXCEED (L7–L10)**. Every level is gated by re-testing — you cannot engineer what you haven't measured, and you cannot exceed what you haven't rebuilt.
>
> This domain sets the *physiology plan*; movement skills are executed in `domains/running`, `domains/swimming`, `domains/strength`, `domains/mobility`, `domains/kung-fu`, `domains/team-sports`; fuel/rest/repair live in `pillars/nutrition`, `pillars/training`, `pillars/sleep`. Cross-reference, never duplicate.
>
> All content is training guidance — never medical advice. Abnormal vitals or lab readings → `## NEXT: human-consult`. Pain/dizziness stop-signals → `## NEXT: @safety-guardian`. Age-gating per `learner/profile.md` — minors get scaled versions of every load.

## PHASE I — ASSESS (L0–L1): the human machine audit

The body obeys basic laws: continuous oxygen supply, a heartbeat that never stops, blood circulation to every cell. Before engineering anything, we measure the machine's current operating parameters — the same numbers a doctor or elite coach would want on file.

## L0 — Machine audit, part 1: the daily vitals
- Skills: measure resting heart rate correctly (morning, before rising, 60-s count or device); record a 7-day step/activity baseline; measure body weight and waist circumference; understand BMI — compute it, then learn its limits (doesn't separate muscle from fat).
- Theory: the body's basic laws as *measurable parameters* — oxygen supply (cardio fitness), heartbeat (resting HR as engine-idle speed), circulation (BP, perfusion); what sedentary-risk flags look like — overweight BMI/waist, low daily steps (<~5,000), zero structured sessions/week.
- Unlock assessment: 7-day baseline table complete — avg resting HR, avg steps/day, weight, waist, session count — logged to `learner/assessments.md` and `metrics.md`. Named which sedentary flags apply (or confirmed none).

## L1 — Machine audit, part 2: engine capacity + the lab report
- Skills: estimate VO2max with a submaximal test — Rockport 1-mile walk (age-appropriate) or scaled Cooper 12-min run/walk; take a resting blood-pressure reading if guardian provides a device; educationally read a guardian-provided lab report — locate LDL, HDL, triglycerides, fasting glucose, and note which are in/out of reference range.
- Theory: what VO2max is and why it's the single best predictor of endurance ceiling; lipid panel basics — LDL ("delivery trucks," higher = more plaque risk), HDL ("recycling trucks"), triglycerides; blood pressure = pipe pressure; *educational reading only — never diagnosis*: any out-of-range value → `## NEXT: human-consult`, recorded in `learner/health.md` flags by the human, never interpreted by the agent.
- Unlock assessment: full "machine spec sheet" filed — RHR, est. VO2max, BMI + waist, activity baseline, BP, lab-report fields noted — plus a written line naming every flag found and its owner (self / guardian / doctor).

## PHASE II — ENGINEER (L2–L6): proven levers, gated by re-test

Each lever is scientifically established. Each level ends with a re-test against the audit numbers — the machine must prove it changed before the next dial turns. Rules of order: **assessment before intensity, engine before speed, form before load**.

## L2 — Zone 2: build the aerobic engine
- Skills: sustain Zone 2 — just below first lactate/ventilatory threshold, ~72–82% HRmax or talk-test (full sentences possible, slightly strained) — for 30–40 min via running (`domains/running`), swimming (`domains/swimming`), or brisk walking; monitor cardiac drift (HR rise at constant pace); hold 3 Zone 2 sessions/week.
- Theory: mitochondrial biogenesis — slow, high-volume work multiplies the cell's oxygen-burning machinery; polarized/pyramidal distribution — elite endurance athletes do ~80% of volume at LOW intensity and only ~20% hard; why "always moderate" is the worst training.
- Unlock assessment: 4 consecutive weeks of ≥3 Zone 2 sessions/week logged + re-test shows <~10% HR drift over 30 min at constant pace (engine holding steady).

## L3 — Progressive overload: the engineer's dial
- Skills: run a 6-week progression in one domain — raise total volume ~5–10%/week, never more; change ONE variable at a time; follow the progression order: session **time** first → **intensity** second → **frequency** last; log shows the dial turning week over week.
- Theory: overload → adaptation → supercompensation cycle; why >10% jumps spike injury risk without faster fitness; why adding frequency before capacity = junk volume.
- Unlock assessment: compliant 6-week block logged end-to-end + re-test shows improvement or stability (RHR flat/down, Zone 2 pace same-or-faster at same HR) with zero injury flags.

## L4 — Combined training: two systems, one body
- Skills: run a weekly split combining aerobic + resistance — e.g., 3 Zone 2 sessions + 2 strength sessions (per `domains/strength` level); apply sequencing rules — separate hard leg days from hard run days by ≥24 h where possible; same-session order: strength before cardio if both.
- Theory: combined aerobic+resistance training beats either alone for lipid profile (LDL↓, HDL↑, triglycerides↓) and body composition; **the honest math:** exercise-only weight loss averages ~2–3 kg — training builds the engine, `pillars/nutrition` is the fat-loss lever; concurrent-training "interference" is small and managed by spacing, not avoided.
- Unlock assessment: 8 weeks of combined split logged + re-audit trend reviewed (waist, steps, RHR) + explain in own words why nutrition — not extra training — drives fat loss.

## L5 — Intermediate milestone: VO2max development
- Skills: interval work at 90–100% VO2max intensity — e.g., scaled Norwegian 4×4 (4 min hard / 3 min easy) or short HIIT (30–60 s reps); a model week: 3 Zone 2 + 1–2 interval + 2 strength; hard days never back-to-back (coordinates with `pillars/training`).
- Theory: VO2max vs lactate threshold vs running economy — three separate dials; HIIT's acute benefits — the fastest known lever for VO2max and stroke volume; why intervals sit ON TOP of the Zone 2 base, never instead of it (pyramid, not steeple).
- Unlock assessment: re-test — est. VO2max improved ≥~5% vs L1 baseline + completed 4-week interval block with no missed weeks and no safety flags.

## L6 — Load carriage & durability: the commando bridge
- Skills: rucking progression — begin ~10% bodyweight (less for minors), 30–45 min, flat ground; build **distance before load**; pack fit (high, tight, hip-belted), posture under load, foot care (hot-spot protocol, sock system); hill walking with load.
- Theory: loaded-march physiology — eccentric leg stress, energy cost scaling with load (~each kg costs measurable extra oxygen); **aspirational far targets** (adult-elite, years away — direction, not deadline): UKSF "Fan Dance" ≈ 24–26 km loaded march over Pen y Fan; "Long Drag" ≈ 64 km; US SFAS ≈ 45 lb (~20 kg) ruck cross-country. These are the destination on a multi-year horizon — for minors everything scales down (load, distance, and adult benchmark weight caps apply only at maturity).
- Unlock assessment: 2-h ruck at light load completed with zero hot-spots/injury + written long-horizon load progression plan approved by `@safety-guardian` (human approval for load increases per age rules).

## PHASE III — EXCEED (L7–L10): beyond normal limits

The engine is built. Now we test durability — what the body does on day three, hour four, decision twenty when everything is tired.

## L7 — Multi-day durability
- Skills: back-to-back training days — 3-consecutive-day blocks (hard/moderate/easy pattern); morning-after readiness check (resting HR elevated >5–10 bpm? unusual soreness? motivation drop? → downgrade the day); manage cumulative fatigue across a week, not just per session.
- Theory: fatigue accumulation vs recovery debt — fitness hides tiredness until it doesn't; glycogen + sleep interaction (under-fueled + under-slept multiplies fatigue); militaries build durability with repeated-days loading — and pay for it in injuries; we take the adaptation, skip the abuse.
- Unlock assessment: one 3-consecutive-day block logged with all sessions hitting planned intensity + documented readiness checks each morning.

## L8 — Performance under fatigue
- Skills: decision quality while exhausted — end hard sessions with a structured cognitive/technical task (route planning, arithmetic drill, `domains/kung-fu` form under fatigue, `domains/brain-training` recall test); single long efforts 2–4 h with planned fueling per `pillars/nutrition`.
- Theory: central fatigue — an exhausted brain makes worse decisions than a fresh one, and that's trainable within limits; the "fatigue buffer" concept; pacing under stress vs pacing fresh.
- Unlock assessment: completed a 3h+ effort including a logged decision/technique task, scored against own fresh-state baseline — plus after-action notes on where judgment slipped.

## L9 — Personal records & environmental adaptation
- Skills: PR testing protocol — planned, tapered test events across domains (5k time, longest ruck, strength marks per `domains/strength`); heat-acclimation basics — progressive exposure, hydration/sodium per `pillars/nutrition`, timing sessions to cooler hours first; duration adaptation — extend the longest sustainable effort month over month.
- Theory: acclimation timelines — heat adaptation in ~1–2 weeks of progressive exposure, altitude concepts (sleep-low logic, iron status relevance — educational only); why PRs are *planned events*, not accidents; tapering into tests.
- Unlock assessment: ≥2 PRs set vs personal baselines + written taper/test protocol followed and filed in `sessions.md`.

## L10 — Mastery: the commando standard
- Skills: design and execute a personal multi-stage capstone — the learner's own "selection day" (e.g., ruck + run + strength circuit + skills task, scaled to age/baseline, human-approved); periodize a full 12-week engineering block solo; mentor a beginner through a session.
- Theory: the whole-machine view — cardiovascular, musculoskeletal, metabolic, and psychological resilience as ONE engineered system; lifetime maintenance — how to hold the standard for decades, not weeks.
- Unlock assessment: capstone completed safely with after-action report + self-designed block evidence + teach-back documented. This is the standing definition of "superhuman body" in this OS — engineered, measured, proven.
