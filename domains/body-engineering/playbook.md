# Body Engineering Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence. The domain's constitution: **assessment before intensity · engine before speed · form before load**.

## Do
- Audit before engineering — no interval work, no load, no volume jumps until the L0–L1 machine spec sheet exists.
- Keep Zone 2 truly easy — talk test must pass (full sentences, slightly strained). If you can't talk, it's not Zone 2.
- Progress ~5–10%/week, one variable at a time, in order: session time → intensity → frequency.
- Combine aerobic + resistance across the week (per `domains/strength` level) — it's the optimal pattern for lipids and body composition.
- Re-test at every level gate: RHR, est. VO2max, drift check. The machine must prove it changed before the next dial turns.
- Ruck light and long before heavy and short — distance before load, always; foot-check and hot-spot stop every 30–45 min.
- Treat morning resting HR as a readiness gauge: elevated >5–10 bpm vs baseline → downgrade the day, log it, check `pillars/sleep`.
- Plan PRs and long efforts as *events* — taper in, fuel per `pillars/nutrition`, log the result.
- Log every session AND every audit with the formats in `sessions.md` — the engineering record IS the curriculum.
- Coordinate hard days with `pillars/training/periodization.md`; hard sessions never stack back-to-back before L7.

## Don't
- Don't chase the commando benchmarks now — Fan Dance (~24–26 km loaded), Long Drag (~64 km), SFAS 45 lb ruck are multi-year adult-elite targets. Direction, not deadline. Minors are scaled down by age; load increases need human approval.
- Don't let HIIT replace the base — ~80% of volume stays easy even at high levels (pyramid, not steeple).
- Don't expect training to fix body composition alone — exercise-only loss averages ~2–3 kg; the fat-loss lever is `pillars/nutrition`. Training builds the engine.
- Don't read a lab report clinically — locate values, note in/out of range, escalate. Educational only, never diagnosis.
- Don't add ruck load and distance in the same week; don't ruck on consecutive days below L7.
- Don't train through elevated resting HR + poor sleep — that's recovery debt compounding, not toughness.
- Don't test PRs untapered or unplanned — fatigue hides fitness.
- Don't compare numbers to anyone else's benchmarks — all targets are personal and age-adjusted.

## Red-line rules (non-negotiable)
- **Stop signals (end session immediately):** chest pain/pressure, dizziness/faintness, sharp joint or bone pain, unusual breathlessness at easy effort, gait change under load, hot-spot ignored into blister → `## NEXT: @safety-guardian`.
- **Abnormal vitals or lab values** (out-of-range LDL/BP/glucose etc.) → `## NEXT: human-consult`; flags recorded in `learner/health.md` by the human only.
- **Age-gating:** minors — bodyweight-scaled rucking (≤~10% bodyweight to start), no multi-hour efforts without guardian approval, no maximal tests; all elite benchmarks wait for maturity per `learner/profile.md`.
- **Recovery is enforced, not suggested:** ≥2 rest days/week; sleep debt >2h downgrades intensity (auto-rule with `pillars/training`); `@safety-guardian` holds veto over all plans.
- Everything here is training guidance — never medical advice.
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
