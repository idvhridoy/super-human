# Body Engineering Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations — audit + base)
- Exercise-physiology explainers for non-scientists: how the heart/lungs/blood deliver oxygen; what resting HR and VO2max actually measure.
- Zone 2 explainers: "what is Zone 2 cardio" videos/articles — first lactate threshold, the talk test, cardiac drift, mitochondrial biogenesis in plain language.
- Measurement guides: how to take a resting pulse correctly; Rockport 1-mile walk test protocol; how to read a standard lipid panel (LDL/HDL/triglycerides/glucose) — educational primers from public-health sources.
- Activity trackers/apps: step counting, HR tracking (device optional — manual counting works).
- Polarized/pyramidal training explainers: the "80% easy" rule and why elites train slow.

## L3–L5 (intermediate — overload, combined training, VO2max)
- Progressive-overload guides: the ~10% rule, supercompensation cycle, one-variable-at-a-time programming.
- Concurrent-training explainers: combining aerobic + resistance work, sequencing and spacing, the interference effect honestly assessed.
- VO2max interval protocols: Norwegian 4×4 and scaled HIIT formats; pyramid vs steeple intensity distribution.
- Body-composition evidence summaries: what exercise alone vs nutrition does for fat loss (the ~2–3 kg reality).
- Introductory HRV/wearable guides: what heart-rate variability is, using morning resting HR as a readiness gauge, consumer-wearable accuracy caveats.

## L6–L10 (advanced — durability, load, exceed)
- Rucking/loaded-march progressions: pack fit, foot care, load-carriage physiology, distance-before-load programming.
- SOF preparation manuals — publicly available selection-prep guides in the style of MARSOC/SFAS preparation (generic military-fitness prep booklets and published training pipelines); use for horizon-planning ONLY — benchmarks scaled to age and approved by `@safety-guardian`, never copied as immediate targets.
- Sports-science textbooks: endurance-training physiology, fatigue/central-fatigue research summaries, acclimation (heat/altitude) timelines, periodization references.
- Recovery science: deload design, sleep-performance links (executed via `pillars/sleep`), fatigue-accumulation models.
- Decision-under-fatigue material: cognitive performance under physical load — pairs with `domains/brain-training` and `domains/judgment`.
- Long-form case studies: multi-day endurance events, military selection accounts — for structure and pacing lessons, not imitation.
