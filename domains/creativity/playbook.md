# Creativity Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Quantity before quality — the first 10 ideas are supposed to be obvious; the good ones hide behind them
- Capture everything — notebook within reach always; ideas evaporate faster than they arrive
- Defer judgment while generating — edit later, produce now; separate the two modes deliberately
- Remix relentlessly — combine two unrelated things and see what happens; name your sources, make the transform yours
- Use constraints as fuel — limits force invention; invent the restriction first if none exists
- Finish small things often — a done artifact beats a perfect plan every time
- Take breaks on purpose — incubation is part of the process; walk away, then return
- Share work and ask specific questions — "does the middle drag?" beats "is it good?"
- Protect a weekly making session on the schedule — practice creates inspiration
- Review the notebook weekly — pick one spark and build it; a pipeline, not a graveyard

## Don't
- Don't judge ideas during generation — it kills fluency instantly
- Don't wait for inspiration — the session produces the mood, not the other way around
- Don't copy and call it remix — transform and add your angle; name sources honestly
- Don't chase originality for its own sake — useful and strange beats different-for-different
- Don't abandon at first critique — revision IS the creative act; extract the useful, keep your spine
- Don't compare your draft to someone's finished work — compare your draft to your last draft
- Don't grind a blank page for hours — a deliberate break is a technique, not a failure
- Don't let mediums become the point — craft depth lives in `domains/drawing/`, `domains/music/`, `domains/engineering/`; this domain feeds them ideas
- Don't publish or share work publicly without guardian approval
