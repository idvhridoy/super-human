# Creativity Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. Scope: the thinking layer above the craft domains — divergent thinking, idea fluency/flexibility, remix thinking, constraints, original artifacts, and a sustained creative practice. Craft skills live in `domains/drawing/`, `domains/music/`, `domains/engineering/`, `domains/coding/`, `domains/literature/` — this domain supplies the idea engine they all feed on. All content filtered by learner age (`learner/profile.md`).

## L0 — Foundations
- What creativity is: new combinations of existing things — a skill to train, not a gift to wait for
- Permission to be bad: first drafts are supposed to be rough; quantity comes before quality
- Capture habit: idea notebook always in reach — write/draw every idea before it evaporates
- **Unlock:** idea notebook started with 10+ captured sparks; explains why "bad first drafts" are part of the method

## L1 — Divergent Thinking Drills
- Alternate uses: how many ways to use a brick, a paperclip, a sock — everyday objects, wild answers
- What-if questions: what if gravity reversed, pets could vote, doors disappeared
- Listing games: 10 things that are round, 10 ways to cross a room without walking
- **Unlock:** completes 5 daily divergent drills; answers get stranger and more original across the week

## L2 — Idea Fluency
- Speed reps: 30 ideas in 10 minutes on one prompt — no pausing to judge
- Judgment delay: generate now, evaluate later — separate the two modes on purpose
- Idea count tracking: the numbers going up IS the skill growing
- **Unlock:** idea-count trend rising across 4 weekly sprints; can switch judging off and on deliberately

## L3 — Idea Flexibility
- Category hopping: same prompt answered across different types — silly, practical, artistic, scientific
- Medium switching: express one idea as a drawing, a story, a sound, a build
- Perspective shifting: the problem as seen by a child, an ant, an alien, an engineer
- **Unlock:** solves one prompt in 3+ distinct categories AND 2+ mediums — variety, not just volume

## L4 — Remix Thinking
- Combination exercises: fuse two unrelated things (instrument + sport, recipe + board game)
- Transform, don't copy: take an existing idea and change one core element until it's yours
- Cross-domain borrowing: steal a pattern from one field and apply it to another (nature → design, games → learning)
- **Unlock:** produces 3 remix concepts where the sources are named and the transformation is explicit

## L5 — Creative Constraints — Intermediate milestone
- Limits as fuel: one-color drawings, 100-word stories, 3-material builds, one-hand games
- Constraint design: invent the restriction first, then create inside it
- Working within real limits: time-boxed making, fixed-materials challenges, no-buy projects
- **Unlock:** completes 3 constraint challenges; can explain how each limit forced a better idea

## L6 — Original Artifacts
- Make real things: story, drawing, invention, game, song, gadget — a finished thing that didn't exist before
- Draft → feedback → revise: first version shown, notes taken, improved version produced
- Finishing beats perfecting: ship small things often — a done artifact teaches more than a perfect plan
- **Unlock:** one original artifact taken through a full revision cycle; craft skills borrowed from `domains/drawing/`/`engineering/`/`coding/` as needed

## L7 — The Creative Process
- Incubation on purpose: work hard, step away, let it cook — walks, showers, boredom as fuel
- Session rhythm: focused making blocks, then deliberate breaks — never forcing a blank page for hours
- Mood as material: making while bored, frustrated, excited — and noticing what each produces
- **Unlock:** maintains a 2-week making rhythm; logs what incubation/breaks produced vs grinding

## L8 — Sharing & Critique
- Show the work: present artifacts to family, peers, clubs — sharing is part of the skill
- Take feedback without folding: hear criticism, extract the useful, keep your spine
- Give useful critique: specific, kind, about the work not the person — builds your own eye
- **Unlock:** 3 sharing rounds with feedback notes + a revision response; one critique given that a peer found genuinely useful

## L9 — Sustained Creative Practice
- Weekly making session protected on the schedule — practice creates inspiration, not vice versa
- Portfolio habit: every finished artifact documented and filed
- Idea pipeline: notebook → shortlist → build → share → archive — a working system, not a pile
- **Unlock:** 4-week practice streak + portfolio of 5+ finished artifacts + pipeline review showing ideas moved through stages

## L10 — Mastery
- Ships an original project to a real audience: showcased, published, performed, or gifted — with guardian approval
- Runs the full arc unprompted: capture → generate → combine → constrain → make → share → revise
- Teaches a divergent-thinking or remix drill to a peer or younger learner
- **Unlock:** capstone — original project shipped publicly with evidence + portfolio reviewed (10+ artifacts) + one drill taught successfully
