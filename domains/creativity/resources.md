# Creativity Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Pocket idea notebook / capture tools (paper, voice notes — whichever is always reachable)
- Illustrated creativity primers for young learners (ideas-as-combinations, bad-first-draft permission)
- Divergent-thinking drill cards: alternate uses, what-if prompts, listing games
- Fluency sprint timer + idea-count log sheet

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Category/medium/perspective prompt decks for flexibility reps
- Remix prompt collections: combine-these-two-things exercises, transform-one-element games
- Cross-domain analogy guides (nature → design, games → learning)
- Constraint-challenge lists: one-color, 100-word, 3-material, time-boxed formats
- Medium sampler kits: drawing, writing, building, sound — borrow craft from `domains/drawing/`/`music/`/`engineering/`

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Draft-feedback-revise workflow templates
- Critique guides: receiving notes without folding, giving useful critique
- Portfolio templates: artifact documentation, before/after revision records
- Creative-process books for young learners: incubation, practice rhythm, making through moods
- Idea-pipeline board template: sparks → shortlist → build → share → archive
- Showcase venues directory: school fairs, family galleries, community events, clubs (guardian-approved)
- Behind-the-scenes process interviews/making-ofs from real creators
