# Strength & Plyometrics Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Beginner bodyweight guides: the 5 fundamental patterns (squat, hinge, push, pull, brace) with progressions.
- Video tutorials: proper push-up progressions (wall → incline → knee → full), squat form, plank variations.
- Printable beginner plans: 2–3 day/week bodyweight routines, 15–25 min.
- Explainers: sets/reps/rest notation, progressive overload for bodyweight, warm-up routines.

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Tempo-training tutorials: negatives, pause reps, time under tension.
- Plyometric safety primers: landing mechanics, contact counting, age-appropriate jump progressions.
- Exercise libraries: squat/push-up/plank variation trees; core anti-rotation moves (side plank, dead bug, bird dog).
- Pull-up progression plans: dead hang → band-assisted → negative → strict.

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Advanced bodyweight skill progressions: pull-ups, dips, pistol squat, archer push-ups.
- Plyometric program guides: box jumps, broad jumps, contrast/complex training (strength + power pairing).
- Loaded-training introductions (L7+, age-gated): goblet squat, dumbbell pressing, loaded carries — technique-first video series.
- Program-design reading: periodization basics, deloads, upper/lower splits, PR-testing protocols.
- Movement-screen references for self-assessment (links with `domains/mobility/` benchmarks).
