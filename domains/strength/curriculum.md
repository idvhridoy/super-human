# Strength & Plyometrics Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.

## L0 — Foundations
- Skills: learn the 5 fundamental patterns — squat, hinge, push, pull, brace. Bodyweight squat to chair, incline push-up (wall/counter), plank 20 s, glute bridge.
- Theory: what strength is; sets × reps notation; rest between sets; posture and bracing.
- Unlock assessment: form check — perform all 5 fundamentals with correct mechanics (mentor-scored).

## L1
- Skills: volume on fundamentals — 3×8 bodyweight squats, 3×5 incline/knee push-ups, plank 30 s, glute bridge 3×10.
- Theory: progressive overload — reps before difficulty; 48 h recovery per muscle group.
- Unlock assessment: full range-of-motion sets of all L1 targets without form breakdown.

## L2
- Skills: standard bodyweight — push-up progression to floor, split squat, plank 45 s, superman hold, bear crawl.
- Theory: eccentric vs concentric; unilateral (single-side) training value.
- Unlock assessment: 5 full push-ups (or 10 strict knee push-ups for younger/smaller learners) + 10 split squats each leg.

## L3
- Skills: control & tempo — 3-second negatives on squats and push-ups, pause reps, side plank 20 s/side, single-leg balance 30 s.
- Theory: time under tension; why slow reps build control; core anti-rotation.
- Unlock assessment: 3×8 tempo set (3 s negative) on squat and push-up with perfect form.

## L4
- Skills: plyometric intro — pogo hops, low squat jumps, snap-downs; landing mechanics (quiet feet, knees tracking, soft hips).
- Theory: what plyometrics are; stretch-shortening cycle; landing quality before jumping height.
- Unlock assessment: 3×5 jump-and-stick with quiet, aligned landings (mentor-scored).

## L5 — Intermediate milestone
- Skills: plyo progression — low box/step jumps, broad jump, skater hops; explosive push-up prep; weekly structure of 2 strength + 1 plyo session.
- Theory: plyo contacts counting (foot strikes per session); ground-contact time.
- Unlock assessment: jump-and-land quality score pass on box jump + broad jump; 8 weeks consistent logging.

## L6
- Skills: advanced bodyweight — pull-up progression (dead hang → band-assisted → negative → strict), dips, pistol-squat prep, arch push-ups.
- Theory: relative strength (strength vs bodyweight); grip training.
- Unlock assessment: first strict pull-up OR 5 controlled negatives + 5 parallel-bar/bench dips.

## L7
- Skills: loaded intro (age-gated — requires appropriate age in `learner/profile.md` + human approval) — goblet squat, dumbbell press, farmer's carry, kettlebell deadlift. Light loads, technique-first.
- Theory: load progression; RPE-based loading; why external load is added last.
- Unlock assessment: 3×8 goblet squat + 3×8 DB press at a technically clean light load.

## L8
- Skills: loaded work — structured upper/lower split, progressive overload tracked in `metrics.md`; loaded carries, split-stance lifts.
- Theory: periodization basics; deload weeks; warm-up ramping sets.
- Unlock assessment: 8-week progressive block logged end-to-end with increasing load/reps.

## L9
- Skills: power & strength blend — contrast sets (strength move → plyo move), med-ball throws, heavier plyo variants; PR testing protocol.
- Theory: strength→power conversion; readiness testing; auto-regulation.
- Unlock assessment: measurable PR improvements — vertical jump, pull-up reps, and/or loaded-lift numbers vs L6–L8 baselines.

## L10 — Mastery
- Skills: self-programs a full training block; coaches the 5 fundamentals to a beginner; maintains a balanced strength/power/mobility profile.
- Theory: full program design, weak-point analysis, age-appropriate lifetime strength practice.
- Unlock assessment: 90-day self-designed program completed with logged evidence + movement screen pass + teach-back documented.
