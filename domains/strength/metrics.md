## Domain-specific KPIs
- Exercise PRs (reps/time at standard form):
  - Push-up max reps (full + knee variant noted separately)
  - Pull-up max reps (progression stage noted: hang/band/negative/strict)
  - Plank hold (s); side plank (s/side)
  - Bodyweight squat reps in 60 s; split squat reps/leg
  - Dips max reps
- Loaded lifts (L7+, age-gated): goblet squat load, DB press load, carry weight — best sets logged.
- Vertical jump / broad jump (cm): re-tested at level assessments.
- Plyo contacts per session: foot strikes counted; caps enforced by level (see playbook).
- Landing-quality score (1–5, mentor-scored): quiet, aligned, controlled.
- Sessions per week: target 2–3; ≥48 h between same muscle groups.
- Form-check pass rate: % of sets meeting technique standard.
