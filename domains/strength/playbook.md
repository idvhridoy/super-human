# Strength & Plyometrics Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Warm up every session: 5 min light cardio + dynamic moves (arm circles, leg swings, hip openers) + 1 easy practice set of each exercise.
- Technique before reps before difficulty — a rep only counts if form is clean.
- Progress one variable at a time: reps → tempo → harder variation → load.
- Rest 48 h before training the same muscle group again; alternate muscle groups across days.
- Land softly on every jump — quiet feet, knees tracking over toes, hips absorbing. Landing quality outranks jump height.
- Count plyometric contacts and stay inside level caps (see Safety).
- Log every set: exercise, reps, variation, RPE, form notes.
- Finish sessions with a short cool-down and note anything that hurt.

## Don't
- No loaded/barbell work before L7 — and L7+ requires age-appropriateness per `learner/profile.md` plus human approval and qualified supervision.
- No maximal lifts or grinding reps under fatigue — stop the set when form breaks.
- No plyometrics on tired legs, sore joints, or back-to-back days.
- No breath-holding/bracing competitions; breathe through reps (exhale on effort).
- Don't compare loads or reps to others — benchmarks are personal and age-adjusted.
- No pain reps: sharp or joint pain ends the set immediately.

## Safety notes (if physical)
- Stop signals (end session): sharp joint/bone pain, form collapse that can't be corrected mid-set, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, any pain that changes movement pattern.
- Plyo caps: L4–L5 max ~40–60 contacts/session; L6+ max ~80–100 contacts/session; always on a non-concrete surface with proper footwear.
- Age-appropriate limits: for minors (per `learner/profile.md`), strength work stays bodyweight/tempo/plyo until growth-plate maturity; loaded work is light, high-rep, technique-first, supervised, and never maximal — heavy loads and 1-rep-max attempts are prohibited.
- Spotting/supervision: dips, negatives, and any loaded movement get a spotter or safety setup.
- Soreness vs injury: normal muscle soreness fades in 24–72 h; pain in a joint/tendon or lasting longer → flag `@safety-guardian`, regress to easier variation.
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
