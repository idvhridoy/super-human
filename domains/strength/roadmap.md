# Strength & Plyometrics Roadmap

> Maintained by `@roadmap-planner`. Dates adjusted from evidence, not wishes.

## Next 30 days
- [ ] Baseline lifts/jumps recorded: push-up max reps, plank seconds, squat reps, bodyweight squat form check (L0 unlock)
- [ ] 2–3 sessions/week — fundamentals only, all logged
- [ ] Reach L1 targets: plank 30 s, 3×8 squats, incline/knee push-up sets

## 60 days
- [ ] L2: first full push-ups (or strict knee milestone), split squats both legs
- [ ] L3: tempo/negative training introduced — control before difficulty
- [ ] Landing-mechanics practice prep: snap-downs and pogo hops

## 90 days
- [ ] L4–L5: plyometric intro with quiet landings; low box jumps
- [ ] Consistent 3 sessions/week for 4+ weeks
- [ ] Mid-block PR check: push-up reps + plank time vs baseline

## This year
- Target L6–L7: first strict pull-up (or strong progression), dips, and — if age-appropriate per `learner/profile.md` with human approval — light loaded work (goblet squat, DB press). Plyometrics remain landing-quality-first at every level.
