# Geography Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.

## L0 — Foundations
- Concepts: what geography is (physical + human); globe vs map; cardinal and intercardinal directions; continents and oceans; basic landforms (mountain, river, coast, desert)
- Skills: name all continents and oceans; use directions on a simple map
- Unlock: blank-map labeling of continents/oceans at 100% and a directions exercise at 90%+

## L1 — Map Literacy
- Concepts: map elements (title, scale, legend/key, symbols); grid references; latitude and longitude intro; map types (physical, political, thematic)
- Skills: locate features with 4-figure grid references; estimate distances from scale; read any legend
- Unlock: real-map reading test — find 10 features by grid reference and measure 3 distances correctly

## L2 — Physical Geography I: Earth Structure
- Concepts: layers of the Earth; plate tectonics; plate-boundary types; earthquakes; volcanoes; rock cycle intro
- Skills: draw and label Earth layers and boundary diagrams; link events to boundaries
- Unlock: plate-tectonics test 80%+ and explain one real earthquake/volcano case briefly

## L3 — Physical Geography II: Water & Landforms
- Concepts: river systems (upper/middle/lower course); erosion, transport, deposition; coastal landforms; mountains and glaciers intro; weathering
- Skills: draw annotated landform diagrams (meander, waterfall, delta, sea arch); sequence formation processes
- Unlock: landform-diagram test at 80%+ with correct process vocabulary

## L4 — Weather & Climate
- Concepts: weather elements and instruments; climate vs weather; world climate zones; latitude's effect on climate; seasons; reading climate graphs
- Skills: interpret climographs (temperature + rainfall); match graphs to climate zones
- Unlock: climograph-interpretation test at 80%+ and explain why climates differ by latitude

## L5 — Countries & Capitals (intermediate milestone)
- Concepts: political world map; ~195 countries organized by region; major capitals; flag basics
- Skills: locate any country on a blank map; recall capitals per region
- Unlock: region-by-region blank-map quizzes at 80%+ for every world region

## L6 — Population & Settlement
- Concepts: population distribution and density; population growth and pyramids; migration (push/pull); urbanization; settlement patterns and hierarchy
- Skills: interpret population pyramids and density maps; explain why people live where they do
- Unlock: population-data interpretation test at 80%+ and a settlement case study write-up

## L7 — Human Geography: Economy & Development
- Concepts: economic sectors (primary/secondary/tertiary); development indicators (GDP per capita, literacy, HDI); trade basics; global inequality; megacities
- Skills: compare countries using development data; classify jobs by sector
- Unlock: country-development comparison report at 80%+ standard

## L8 — Resources & Environment
- Concepts: energy resources (renewable/non-renewable); global agriculture and food supply; water scarcity; deforestation; climate-change impacts
- Skills: map resource distributions; argue a resource-management trade-off with evidence
- Unlock: issue brief linking one resource problem to its causes and possible solutions

## L9 — Global Systems
- Concepts: globalization; trade and shipping networks; geopolitical regions intro; oceans as commons; international organizations; megatrends (urbanization, aging, migration)
- Skills: draw a systems map linking trade, population, and environment for one region
- Unlock: global-systems essay + systems map assessed at 80%+

## L10 — Mastery
- Concepts: synthesis of physical and human geography; spatial data literacy
- Skills: produce an original regional study (physical setting + human systems + data); intro to a digital mapping/GIS tool; present findings
- Unlock: comprehensive map + data exam at 85%+ and capstone regional-study presentation
