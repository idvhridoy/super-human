# Geography Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Open every session with a 5-minute blank-map drill — draw, label, check, correct
- Learn countries region-by-region, always locating them on the map (never list-only)
- Draw landform and plate diagrams by hand with annotations — drawing IS learning here
- Follow one news story per week geographically: locate it, explain the physical/human context
- Interpret every graph (climograph, population pyramid, density map) with a fixed checklist
- Link physical to human geography: how landforms/climate shape settlement and economy
- Spaced review: re-quiz finished regions monthly so map knowledge doesn't decay

## Don't
- Don't memorize country lists without placing each one on a blank map
- Don't rush to human geography — physical foundations (L2–L4) carry everything later
- Don't copy diagrams — draw from memory, then compare
- Don't treat capitals/flags as trivia separate from regional context
- Don't skip data skills; reading maps/graphs matters as much as recall

## Safety notes (if field observation)
- Outdoor sketching/observation in safe, known areas only; weather and daylight awareness
- No solo trips for minors — check `learner/profile.md` age/mode; guardian-approved outings
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes
