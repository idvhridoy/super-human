# Geography Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Children's/intro atlas and a wall world map for daily reference
- Intro geography textbook chapters on maps, Earth structure, tectonics
- Free video course series on physical geography basics
- Printable blank maps (world, continental) + grid-reference worksheet packs
- Map-quiz apps/sites for continents, oceans, and landmarks

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- General geography textbook (landforms, rivers, coasts, climate chapters)
- Interactive plate-tectonics and river/coast simulations
- Climograph and climate-zone worksheet sets with answer keys
- Country/capital flashcard deck organized by region; spaced-repetition map quiz app
- Weekly news-geography prompt template (locate + explain)

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Human geography textbook (population, development, resources, globalization chapters)
- Data portals for country comparisons (population pyramids, development indicators)
- Digital mapping/GIS tool tutorials (browser-based mapping platforms)
- Past-exam question banks for map-skills and data-response questions
- Regional-study capstone template + presentation rubric for L10
