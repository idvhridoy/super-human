# Mobility & Recovery Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Daily joint-routine videos: 10-min follow-along routines covering neck → ankles.
- Beginner stretching guides: hold times, breathing, stretch-vs-pain sensation.
- Benchmarking how-tos: toe touch, shoulder reach, ankle wall test, deep-squat sit — measurement methods.
- Habit resources: streak tracking, habit-stacking the routine onto daily anchors.

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Hip-mobility routines: 90/90 series, deep-squat progression, world's greatest stretch.
- Thoracic/shoulder mobility drills: rotations, wall slides, doorway stretches.
- Follow-along flow videos: sun-salutation-style sequences, 10–15 min full-body flows.
- Movement-screen explainers: overhead squat assessment, what compensations look like.
- Recovery guides: how mobility fits rest days for running and strength training.

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- End-range flexibility programs: hamstring fold progressions, bridge/backbend safety progressions.
- Loaded-mobility tutorials (L7+): Cossack squat, Jefferson curl — strict light-load progressions.
- PNF/contract-relax stretching guides; active-flexibility drills.
- Ground-movement and flow libraries: crawling patterns, transitions, dynamic warm-up design.
- References on mobility assessment and self-programming; when to seek professional help for pain.
