# Mobility & Recovery Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.

## L0 — Foundations
- Skills: learn the daily 10-min joint routine — neck circles, shoulder rolls, arm circles, hip circles, ankle circles, cat-cow, easy forward fold.
- Theory: mobility vs flexibility; why daily short beats occasional long; breathing through stretches.
- Unlock assessment: complete the routine 5 days in one week (logged).

## L1
- Skills: routine consistency — perform all 8 core positions unguided; hold each stretch 20–30 s; nasal breathing throughout.
- Theory: how range of motion is earned; stretch sensation vs pain.
- Unlock assessment: 14-day streak + unguided routine demo.

## L2
- Skills: baseline flexibility benchmarks — toe-touch distance, shoulder reach test (both sides), ankle wall dorsiflexion test, deep-squat sit test.
- Theory: what each benchmark measures; left/right asymmetry.
- Unlock assessment: all baseline benchmarks measured and recorded in `metrics.md`.

## L3
- Skills: hips & spine focus — deep squat hold 30 s, 90/90 hip switches, thoracic rotations, world's-greatest-stretch flow.
- Theory: hip anatomy basics; why desk/school posture shortens hip flexors and rounds the upper back.
- Unlock assessment: 60 s deep-squat hold + 10 smooth 90/90 switches per side.

## L4
- Skills: movement quality — full-depth squat with heels down, overhead reach without rib flare, single-leg balance 30 s, controlled forward fold.
- Theory: stability + mobility together; compensation patterns (what the body cheats with).
- Unlock assessment: movement screen pass — squat, overhead reach, balance all within standards.

## L5 — Intermediate milestone
- Skills: full-body flow — sun-salutation-style sequence (5 rounds), couch stretch, pigeon pose, thread-the-needle.
- Theory: linking breath to movement; flow as active recovery.
- Unlock assessment: 10-min unguided flow with smooth transitions + 4-week adherence ≥5 days/week.

## L6
- Skills: targeted flexibility — hamstring fold progression, bridge hold 20 s, wrist/forearm prep, doorway chest stretch.
- Theory: end-range vs mid-range flexibility; safe spine extension.
- Unlock assessment: palms-to-floor (or benchmark improvement ≥5 cm) + 20 s bridge.

## L7
- Skills: loaded mobility (light, age-appropriate) — Cossack squat, Jefferson curl with very light load or bodyweight, scapular wall slides.
- Theory: strength at end range; controlled articular rotations concept.
- Unlock assessment: 5 Cossack squats per side with control.

## L8
- Skills: end-range strength — contract/relax (PNF-style) stretching on hamstrings/hips, active straddle work, dead hang 30 s.
- Theory: PNF mechanics; active vs passive range — closing the gap.
- Unlock assessment: measurable improvement on ≥3 benchmark tests + 30 s dead hang.

## L9
- Skills: integrated movement — ground-based flow (crawling patterns, transitions), designing dynamic warm-ups for other domains.
- Theory: mobility as movement skill; programming warm-ups by activity demand.
- Unlock assessment: design + demonstrate a personal daily routine and a sport-specific warm-up (used by `domains/running` or `domains/strength`).

## L10 — Mastery
- Skills: self-assessment and reprogramming; teaches the joint routine to someone else; maintains all benchmarks green.
- Theory: lifetime mobility practice; reading the body's signals; when to refer out (pain → human consult).
- Unlock assessment: full re-benchmark with all scores at/above L6+ standards + documented teach-back.
