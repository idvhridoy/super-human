## Domain-specific KPIs
- Routine adherence: days routine completed per week (target ≥5); streak in days.
- Benchmarks (record at L2, re-test each level):
  - Toe touch / forward fold distance (cm to floor, or ±cm past/before toes)
  - Shoulder reach test — distance between hands behind back (cm, each side)
  - Ankle wall dorsiflexion (cm from wall, knee touching wall)
  - Deep-squat sit hold (s)
  - Single-leg balance (s)
- Range benchmarks at higher levels: palms-to-floor (y/n + cm), bridge hold (s), dead hang (s), Cossack squat reps/side.
- Symmetry flag: left/right differences >10% on any paired test.
- Tightness/pain notes: recurring tight areas logged to guide focus blocks.
- Movement screen results: squat / overhead reach / balance — pass/fail per L4.
