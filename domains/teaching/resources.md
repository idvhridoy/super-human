# Teaching Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — all picks filtered for learner age by human/guardian.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Short articles and videos explaining the Feynman technique (adult-previewed)
- Everyday explain-it-back prompts: "what did you learn today — teach me"
- Jargon-to-plain-word translation exercises and word-substitution games
- Analogy practice decks: familiar-object cards mapped to abstract ideas
- Recorder (phone/tablet) to capture and review explanations

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Teach-back and check-question scripts for young tutors
- Example/counterexample generation exercises
- Mini-lesson plan templates (objective / explanation / practice / check)
- Step-by-step demonstration guides for simple skills to practice teaching
- Age-appropriate books on how learning works (memory, misconceptions, practice)

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Question-design guides: open vs closed questions, probing follow-ups
- Tutoring frameworks and one-on-one session structures
- Feedback models for learners (specific, kind, actionable)
- Curriculum-sequencing and lesson-planning references
- Peer-tutoring and mentoring program opportunities (school, clubs, family)
- Recorded great teachers/explainers for analysis — structure first, charisma second
