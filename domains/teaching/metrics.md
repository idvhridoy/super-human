## Domain-specific KPIs
- Things taught: distinct concepts/skills successfully taught to a real learner (count)
- Teach-back success rate: % of sessions where the learner restated or performed correctly (%)
- Explain-it-back reps completed (self and peer)
- Original examples + counterexamples produced (count)
- Mini-lessons designed and delivered (count + pass rate of end-checks)
- Misconceptions diagnosed and fixed (count)
- Peer/tutee improvement evidence (before vs after on the taught skill)
