# Teaching Roadmap

> Maintained by `@roadmap-planner`. Dates adjusted from evidence, not wishes. Pace adapts to learner age.

## Next 30 days
- [ ] Baseline recorded: audio/video of the learner explain-it-back on a recently learned concept
- [ ] Daily Feynman reps: explain-it-back on anything studied that day, 5 min/day
- [ ] First simplification rep: re-explain a textbook paragraph in half the words
- [ ] First teach-back held: learner explains, listener restates the main point — success logged

## 60 days
- [ ] Simplification + analogy drill weekly: 3 jargon-heavy ideas made plain
- [ ] Original example + counterexample produced for 3 different concepts
- [ ] First real skill taught to a peer: demonstrate → guided try → independent try, peer performs alone
- [ ] Check-question habit started: every explanation ends with "tell me what you got"

## 90 days
- [ ] First designed mini-lesson (objective/explanation/practice/check) delivered to a real learner
- [ ] One mid-lesson check failed and successfully re-taught a different way — logged
- [ ] Teach-back success rate across sessions trending at or above 70%
- [ ] Level check: target L4–L5 depending on age and evidence

## This year
- Reach L6–L7: regular tutoring of a peer on a real subject with measurable improvement, plus a second designed mini-lesson. Stretch: begin mentoring a younger learner toward a multi-week goal with a shared progress log.
