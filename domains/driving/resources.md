# Driving Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. THEORY materials only.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Local driver handbook/manual (official licensing authority publication)
- Road-sign flashcard decks and sign-quiz apps
- Illustrated vehicle-controls diagrams and dashboard-light guides
- Beginner "rules of the road" explainer videos

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Basic car-mechanics explainers for owners (fluids, tires, warning lights)
- Hazard-perception practice video/clip sets
- Official-style mock theory exam banks
- Following-distance and braking-distance visualizations

## L6–L10 (advanced / locked)
- Defensive-driving course material (theory modules)
- Accident-analysis case-study writeups for judgment training
- Instructor-recommended pre-license study list (when gate nears)
- Post-license: advanced driving/roadcraft reading
