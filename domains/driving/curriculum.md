# Driving Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.
>
> LEVEL GATE — READ FIRST: this curriculum is THEORY ONLY. All practical/behind-the-wheel levels (L7+) are LOCKED until the learner reaches legal driving age AND practices under a licensed human instructor. Agents may never simulate, encourage, or arrange real driving. Human approval gate per `AGENTS.md`.

## L0 — Foundations
- Why rules exist: crash physics basics, human reaction time
- Vehicle anatomy tour: controls, pedals, mirrors, lights, indicators
- Assessment: label a car's controls and instruments on a diagram

## L1 — Road Signs & Markings
- Regulatory signs (stop, yield, speed), warning signs, guide signs
- Lane markings: solid/broken, arrows, pedestrian crossings
- Assessment: 90% on a 40-sign recognition quiz

## L2 — Rules of the Road
- Right-of-way at intersections, roundabouts, 4-way stops
- Speed limits, following distance (2–3s rule), signaling rules
- Assessment: scenario quiz — who goes first, 20 cases

## L3 — Vehicle Mechanics Basics
- Engine basics, fuel/charging, oil/coolant, tire pressure & tread
- Dashboard warning lights; what to do on a breakdown
- Assessment: walk through a pre-drive inspection checklist

## L4 — Hazard Perception
- Scanning technique: near/far/mirrors cycle
- Vulnerable road users: pedestrians, cyclists, animals
- Weather/night hazards; distraction and fatigue theory
- Assessment: hazard-spot in 10 video/image scenarios

## L5 — Intermediate milestone: Mock Theory Exam
- Combined review: signs + rules + hazards
- Situational judgment: rain, fog, emergency vehicles, school zones
- Assessment: pass full-length mock theory exam ≥ 90%

## L6 — Advanced Theory & Defensive Driving
- Defensive driving principles: space cushion, escape routes, SIPDE
- Skid/hydroplaning theory, braking distances math
- Sharing road with trucks/motorcycles; eco-driving basics
- Assessment: written defensive-driving exam + braking-distance problems

## L7 — LOCKED: Pre-Drive Practical Prep
- LOCKED until legal age + licensed instructor. Seat/mirror setup theory, cockpit drill (DSSSM-style), hand positions
- Assessment (theory only): verbal walkthrough of pre-drive routine

## L8 — LOCKED: Supervised Basics
- LOCKED. Instructor-led: moving off, stopping, steering control — logged by licensed instructor only
- Agents track instructor-entered evidence; never instruct

## L9 — LOCKED: Supervised Road Skills
- LOCKED. Junctions, parking, highway theory-to-practice under instructor

## L10 — Mastery (post-license)
- LOCKED. License obtained; defensive driving refinement, first-solo-year safety plan
