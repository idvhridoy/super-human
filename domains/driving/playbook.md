# Driving Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Keep every session theoretical: quizzes, sign flashcards, scenario discussion, diagrams
- Use the local driving manual as source of truth (Bangladesh BRTA or learner's jurisdiction)
- Retake weak quiz sections until ≥90% before advancing
- Practice hazard-spotting passively: call out observations as a passenger (no driver distraction)

## Don't
- NEVER arrange, simulate as "real", or encourage actual driving — theory only until legal age + licensed instructor
- Don't teach jurisdiction-wrong rules; check the local manual, not generic web advice
- Don't treat simulator/game play as driving progress — log it as interest, not skill
- Don't unlock L7+ content for assessment until the human gate opens

## Safety notes
- THEORY-ONLY DOMAIN: no physical driving activity is authorized at any level below legal age with a licensed human instructor. This is a hard `AGENTS.md` gate.
- If the learner asks to practice in a real vehicle: log the request, refuse instruction, produce `## NEXT: human-consult`.
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
