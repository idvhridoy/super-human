# Physics Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.

## L0 — Foundations
- Concepts: what physics studies; SI units (m, kg, s, N, J, W); scientific notation; measurement and uncertainty basics
- Skills: unit conversion, reading simple graphs, algebra rearrangement (solve for x)
- Unlock: unit-conversion worksheet at 90%+ and describe one everyday motion in words + diagram

## L1 — Motion (Kinematics)
- Concepts: distance vs displacement, speed vs velocity, acceleration, free fall intro
- Skills: distance–time and velocity–time graphs; v = d/t and a = Δv/t problems
- Unlock: kinematics problem set at 80%+ and interpret 3 motion graphs correctly

## L2 — Forces & Newton's Laws
- Concepts: balanced/unbalanced forces, friction, weight vs mass, Newton's 1st/2nd/3rd laws
- Skills: free-body diagrams; F = ma calculations; net-force reasoning
- Unlock: draw correct FBDs for 5 scenarios + solve F = ma problems at 80%+

## L3 — Energy & Work
- Concepts: kinetic and potential energy, conservation of energy, work, power, efficiency, simple machines
- Skills: energy-transfer bookkeeping; W = Fd and P = W/t calculations
- Unlock: energy problem set at 80%+ including one roller-coaster/pendulum analysis

## L4 — Momentum & Pressure
- Concepts: momentum, impulse, elastic vs inelastic collisions; pressure, fluid pressure, buoyancy intro
- Skills: p = mv, conservation-of-momentum collision problems, P = F/A
- Unlock: collision problem set + written explanation of why objects float/sink

## L5 — Waves & Sound (intermediate milestone)
- Concepts: amplitude, frequency, wavelength, period; transverse vs longitudinal; v = fλ; sound properties; reflection/refraction of waves
- Skills: label wave diagrams; calculate λ, f, v given two of three
- Unlock: waves problem set at 80%+ and explain echo/Doppler qualitatively

## L6 — Light & Optics
- Concepts: reflection, refraction, Snell's law qualitatively, lenses and mirrors, the eye, color, electromagnetic spectrum
- Skills: accurate ray diagrams for mirrors and lenses; image description (real/virtual, size, orientation)
- Unlock: optics problem set + 5 correct ray diagrams

## L7 — Electricity
- Concepts: charge, current, voltage, resistance; Ohm's law; series and parallel circuits; electrical power
- Skills: circuit diagrams; V = IR and P = VI problems; series/parallel analysis
- Unlock: analyze mixed series+parallel circuits at 80%+

## L8 — Magnetism & Electromagnetism
- Concepts: magnetic fields, electromagnets, motors, generators, electromagnetic induction
- Skills: field-line diagrams; explain how motors and generators work; predict induced current direction
- Unlock: EM concept test at 80%+ and compare motor vs generator in writing

## L9 — Thermal & Modern Physics Intro
- Concepts: heat transfer (conduction/convection/radiation), specific heat, gas behavior intro; atomic models, radioactivity basics, half-life
- Skills: Q = mcΔT problems; identify alpha/beta/gamma decay; half-life calculations
- Unlock: combined thermal + atomic assessment at 80%+

## L10 — Mastery
- Concepts: synthesis of mechanics, energy, waves, electricity; qualitative relativity and quantum ideas; experimental data analysis
- Skills: multi-step problems spanning 2+ topics; design a simple measurement experiment and analyze its data
- Unlock: capstone project + comprehensive exam at 85%+ and teach-back of one advanced topic
