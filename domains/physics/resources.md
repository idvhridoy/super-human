# Physics Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Intro/conceptual physics textbook chapters on measurement and motion
- Free video course playlists covering kinematics and Newton's laws (Khan-Academy-style)
- Interactive motion simulations (PhET-style: moving-man, forces, ramps)
- Printable worksheet packs: unit conversion, graph reading, speed/acceleration
- Everyday observation prompts: estimate speeds on walks, time falling objects (safely)

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Conceptual physics textbook (energy, momentum, waves chapters)
- Simulation labs: energy-skate-park, collision lab, wave-on-a-string
- Problem sets from past exam papers or worksheet generators (graded difficulty)
- Short explainer videos on sound/Doppler/echo with real demos
- Flashcard deck for formulas + units (spaced repetition)

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Algebra-based physics textbook (optics, circuits, EM, thermal, modern chapters)
- Circuit-builder and optics simulations; virtual lab benches
- Olympiad-style or honors-level problem books for stretch problems
- Home-safe experiment guides (pendulum period, lens focal length, simple circuits)
- Comprehensive review book / exam-prep question bank for the L10 capstone
