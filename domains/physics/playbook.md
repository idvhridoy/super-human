# Physics Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Write down known/unknown quantities and units BEFORE touching a formula
- Draw a diagram for every problem (sketch, free-body, ray, or circuit)
- Estimate the answer's order of magnitude first; sanity-check after solving
- Rearrange formulas algebraically before plugging numbers in
- Re-do every missed problem 48h later, then again after 1 week (spaced review)
- Keep a formula sheet the learner builds by hand — not a printed one
- Connect each topic to one real phenomenon (braking distance, why ships float)

## Don't
- Don't memorize formulas without knowing what each symbol means and its units
- Don't drop units mid-calculation or report answers without them
- Don't start a new level with the previous level's quiz still below 80%
- Don't skip graph interpretation — physics is read in graphs as much as numbers
- Don't grind 50 same-type problems; mix problem types (interleaving)

## Safety notes (if physical)
- Household-safe demonstrations only: rolling objects, pendulums, ramps, magnets, low-voltage batteries (≤ 9 V), water/ice observations
- NEVER mains electricity, open wiring, lasers aimed at eyes, or projectiles indoors
- Heat experiments (warm water only, never boiling unattended) need adult supervision if learner is a minor — check `learner/profile.md` age/mode first
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes
