# Engineering & Making Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Kid-safe hand-tool introduction guides and workshop-safety posters
- Simple-machines explainer books/videos with build-along demos
- Measurement practice worksheets and marking-out tutorials
- Scrap-wood starter project plans (straight cuts, butt joints)

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Card/paper bridge and tower challenge guides with load-testing method
- Beginner woodwork plans: box, birdhouse, bookshelf (measured drawings)
- Household teardown guides for learning fasteners and assembly
- Structures/truss explainers: compression, tension, load paths

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Breadboard electronics starter kits and low-voltage project books
- Schematic-symbols reference and circuit-diagram tutorials
- Mechanism libraries: gears, cams, cranks, linkage animations + build plans
- Design-thinking workbook for brief-to-prototype projects
- Supervised soldering/solder-free joining technique guides
