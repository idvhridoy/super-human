# Mindfulness Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path from breath awareness to an integrated daily practice; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. Durations are age-scaled — roughly one minute per year of age for young learners; adults may double them. Pairs with `pillars/sleep/` (`@sleep-coach`) for evening wind-down sits. All content filtered by learner age (`learner/profile.md`).

## L0 — Foundations
- What mindfulness is: paying attention on purpose, without judging
- Breath awareness: find the breath — nostrils, chest, or belly
- First sits: 1–2 minutes, eyes open or closed, comfortable posture
- **Unlock:** completes a 2-minute sit noticing the breath without distress

## L1
- Belly breathing: hand-on-belly drill, slow inhale/exhale
- Counting breaths: 1 to 10 and back — restart kindly when lost
- Posture basics: sit tall, relaxed shoulders, still hands
- **Unlock:** 3–5 minute guided sit, daily for one week

## L2
- Short guided sits: 5–8 minutes following a recorded guide
- The wandering mind: noticing drift and returning is the practice, not failure
- Anchor choice: breath, sounds, or body as the return point
- **Unlock:** 10 guided sits logged; names what pulled attention and returned

## L3
- Body scan: move attention slowly from feet to head
- Noticing sensations: warm, cool, tense, tingly — without fixing them
- Relaxation vs awareness: it's fine to relax, but the goal is noticing
- **Unlock:** completes a full 8–10 minute body scan; reports 3+ distinct sensations

## L4
- Emotions in the body: where anger, worry, and joy physically show up
- Name it to tame it: label the feeling ("anger is here") during a sit
- The pause: one breath before reacting — applied in a real moment
- **Unlock:** uses the pause/label technique in a real emotional moment (observed or reported); regulation support per `domains/behavior/` where needed

## L5 — Intermediate milestone
- Focus training: 10–15 minute sits, sustained attention on one anchor
- Noting practice: softly tag distractions — "thinking", "hearing", "feeling"
- Working with impatience and boredom on purpose
- **Unlock:** 15-minute sit with consistent returning; streak of 10+ days

## L6
- Mindfulness of thoughts: thoughts as passing events, not facts or orders
- Mindful listening: full attention on another person for 2 minutes
- Open awareness: rest attention on whatever arises, no fixed anchor
- **Unlock:** sits without anchor for 10 minutes; mindful listening rep logged

## L7
- Mindfulness in motion: mindful walking — feel each step
- Mindful eating: one meal or snack with full attention, no screens
- Informal moments: brushing teeth, waiting in line as practice
- **Unlock:** one week with a daily informal practice logged alongside sits

## L8
- Longer sits: 15–20 minutes, unguided preferred
- Sitting with difficulty: stay present with mild discomfort, boredom, restlessness
- Self-compassion basics: kind inner voice when practice is hard
- **Unlock:** 20-minute unguided sit; worked through a difficult sit without quitting early

## L9
- Daily integration: practice woven into the day, not just the cushion
- Wind-down pairing: evening breath/body-scan routine coordinated with `@sleep-coach`
- Habit resilience: how to restart after missed days without self-blame
- **Unlock:** 30-day streak of at least one formal or informal practice per day

## L10 — Mastery
- Self-guided practice: designs own sits — length, anchor, focus
- Applies mindfulness under real load: tests, conflict, competition, stress
- Leads a short guided sit for a peer, sibling, or family member
- **Unlock:** capstone — sustained independent practice + one led sit + integration evidence across settings
