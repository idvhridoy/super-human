# Mindfulness Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — all picks filtered for learner age by human/guardian.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Guided breath-awareness audio for young learners (1–5 min)
- Breathing-buddy drill: a stuffed animal riding the belly
- Simple mindfulness picture books (adult-previewed)
- Timer with a gentle bell — no jarring alarms
- Quiet practice corner: cushion or chair, low distraction

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Guided body-scan recordings (5–10 min, age-appropriate)
- Emotion-body maps: drawings of where feelings live in the body
- Noting-practice guided sits ("thinking", "hearing", "feeling")
- Mindfulness journal: one line after each sit
- Kid/family meditation apps or audio libraries (guardian-approved)

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Unguided-sit timers with interval bells
- Mindful-walking guides and mindful-eating scripts
- Age-appropriate books on meditation, attention, and self-compassion
- Sit-with-difficulty guided practices (boredom, restlessness)
- Group sits or school mindfulness programs
- Beginner-friendly retreat-style audio for longer sits (20+ min, teens/adults)
