# Mindfulness Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Consistency over duration: 3 minutes daily beats 30 minutes once a week
- Never force: invite the sit; a reluctant learner can do 60 seconds and stop
- Same time, same place: anchor the habit to a fixed cue (after breakfast, before bed)
- Treat wandering as the rep: noticing drift and returning IS the practice
- Scale duration to age: roughly one minute per year for young learners
- Debrief briefly after sits: "what did you notice?" — curiosity, not grading
- Use guided audio early; fade it as skill grows
- Pair evening sits with the `@sleep-coach` wind-down routine
- Count informal practice as real: mindful eating, walking, listening all log
- Celebrate restarts: a streak break followed by a restart is a success story

## Don't
- Don't use meditation as punishment or a timeout substitute — it must stay voluntary
- Don't chase a blank mind; the goal is noticing, not stopping thoughts
- Don't lengthen sits to hit a number; extend only when the current length is easy
- Don't correct posture or breathing mid-sit; note it for the debrief
- Don't frame emotions as bad: anger and worry are valid objects of attention
- Don't push eyes-closed if the learner is uncomfortable — eyes-open works
- Don't let a missed day become a missed week; restart the same day if possible
- Don't teach EQ/naming-feelings curriculum depth here — `domains/behavior/` owns emotional skills; this domain trains the attention that supports them
