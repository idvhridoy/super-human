# Mindfulness Roadmap

> Maintained by `@roadmap-planner`. Dates adjusted from evidence, not wishes. Durations scale to learner age (~1 min per year for young learners).

## Next 30 days
- [ ] Baseline: learner completes a 2-minute breath-awareness sit; comfort level noted
- [ ] Daily sit habit started: 3–5 min guided, same time each day
- [ ] Anchor chosen: breath, sounds, or body — logged in sessions.md
- [ ] Practice spot and cue set (chair/corner, after breakfast or before bed)

## 60 days
- [ ] Full body scan (8–10 min) completed and sensations reported
- [ ] Emotion-labeling during a sit practiced; "name it to tame it" working
- [ ] First real-world pause: one breath before reacting, observed or reported
- [ ] 14+ day streak on the daily sit

## 90 days
- [ ] 10–15 minute sits with noting practice, mostly guided
- [ ] One informal daily practice running (mindful eating, walking, or teeth-brushing)
- [ ] Evening wind-down sit paired with `@sleep-coach` routine if wanted
- [ ] Level check: target L4–L5 depending on age and evidence

## This year
- Reach L7–L8: unguided 15–20 minute sits, mindfulness in motion and at meals, sitting with mild difficulty without quitting. Stretch: 30-day streak and leading one short sit for someone else.
