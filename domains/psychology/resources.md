# Psychology Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — specific titles/URLs added after librarian review (never invent links). All materials educational, never therapeutic/self-diagnosis oriented.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Illustrated "naming feelings" books/emotion charts with a wide vocabulary.
- Age-appropriate books on how the brain and senses work.
- Optical-illusion and memory-trick collections with simple explanations.
- Emotion-journal template (name / trigger / body signal / what helped).
- Simple attention experiments and focus-game ideas for home use.

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Beginner-friendly books on habits and motivation science.
- Habit-tracker templates (cue / routine / reward design sheet).
- Illustrated bias reference cards for the spotting log.
- Growth-m mindset materials — effort, strategy, and "yet" framing.
- Self-experiment design worksheets (hypothesis → measure → conclude).
- Documentary/explainer content on classic social-psychology findings, age-vetted.

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Accessible introductions to social psychology and personality science.
- Simplified study summaries and "how to read a psychology finding" guides.
- Media-vs-science comparison exercises on distorted pop-psych claims.
- Wellbeing-literacy materials: stress response, sleep, connection, help-seeking — reviewed with guardian.
- Critical-thinking checklists for evaluating mental-health content online.
- Project portfolio templates for the L10 capstone self-studies.
