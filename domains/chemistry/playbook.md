# Chemistry Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Open every session with a 10-equation balancing warm-up once L4 is reached
- Use the mole-map (mass ↔ moles ↔ particles) as THE stoichiometry scaffold
- Build flashcards for element symbols, common ions, and functional groups
- Connect topics to kitchen/bathroom chemistry (acids in food, pH of soap — observation only)
- Draw dot-cross and energy-profile diagrams from memory, then check
- Space review: revisit each finished level after 1 week and 1 month
- Use simulations for anything visual (orbitals, bonding, reaction rates)

## Don't
- Don't memorize the periodic table top-to-bottom — learn groups and trends first
- Don't balance equations by guessing; use the atom-inventory method
- Don't skip safety refreshers — the safety quiz must stay at 100%
- Don't start stoichiometry until balancing is automatic (90%+)
- Don't treat chemistry as pure math — always ask "what are the particles doing?"

## Safety notes
- Household-safe ONLY: observing dissolving, evaporation, melting ice, pH of foods via packaged indicators purchased for the purpose
- NEVER mix household chemicals — especially bleach with anything (ammonia, acids) — this is a hard rule
- No fume-producing, heat-producing, or combustion experiments at home — simulations instead
- No tasting, sniffing directly, or handling unknown substances; wash hands after any observation activity
- Any hands-on activity requires adult supervision if the learner is a minor — check `learner/profile.md` age/mode
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes
