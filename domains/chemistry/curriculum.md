# Chemistry Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.

## L0 — Foundations
- Concepts: what chemistry studies; states of matter and changes of state; pure substances vs mixtures; measurement and units
- Skills: classify substances (element/compound/mixture); read a thermometer/scale; safety symbol recognition
- Unlock: substance-classification worksheet 90%+ and lab-safety quiz at 100%

## L1 — Atoms & Elements
- Concepts: atomic structure (protons, neutrons, electrons); atomic number and mass number; elements and chemical symbols; isotopes intro
- Skills: label atom diagrams; determine particle counts from symbols; recall first 20 elements
- Unlock: atomic-structure quiz 80%+ and recall first 20 element names/symbols

## L2 — The Periodic Table
- Concepts: groups and periods; metals, nonmetals, metalloids; qualitative trends (reactivity down groups 1/7/0, size)
- Skills: navigate the table fluently; predict properties from position; group families (alkali, halogens, noble gases)
- Unlock: periodic-trends quiz 80%+ and blank-table labeling of key regions

## L3 — Bonding & Molecules
- Concepts: ionic vs covalent bonding; simple ions; Lewis/dot-cross diagrams; chemical formulas; naming binary compounds
- Skills: write formulas from names and vice versa; draw dot-cross diagrams for simple compounds
- Unlock: formula-writing and naming test at 85%+

## L4 — Chemical Reactions
- Concepts: word and symbol equations; conservation of mass; balancing; reaction types (synthesis, decomposition, displacement, combustion); observable signs of reaction
- Skills: balance equations; identify reaction type; write equations from descriptions
- Unlock: balancing drill at 90%+ and reaction-type quiz at 80%+

## L5 — Stoichiometry & the Mole (intermediate milestone)
- Concepts: the mole; molar mass; mole↔mass↔particle conversions; reacting masses; concentration intro
- Skills: mole-map method; multi-step stoichiometry from balanced equations
- Unlock: stoichiometry problem set at 80%+ including limiting-type reasoning

## L6 — Acids, Bases & Salts
- Concepts: pH scale; indicators; neutralization; everyday acids and bases; salt formation
- Skills: interpret pH/indicators; write neutralization equations; classify household substances by pH
- Unlock: acids/bases assessment 80%+ and a safe household-pH write-up (observation only)

## L7 — Energy & Reaction Rates
- Concepts: exothermic vs endothermic; collision theory; factors affecting rate (concentration, temperature, surface area, catalysts); energy profile diagrams
- Skills: draw/interpret energy profiles; predict rate changes from conditions
- Unlock: rates-and-energy test at 80%+

## L8 — Organic Chemistry Intro
- Concepts: hydrocarbons; alkanes and alkenes; functional groups intro; naming simple organics; polymers intro
- Skills: name and draw C1–C4 alkanes/alkenes; identify functional groups; describe polymerization conceptually
- Unlock: organic naming/structure test at 80%+

## L9 — Water, Air & Materials
- Concepts: water chemistry and treatment; atmospheric gases; corrosion and rust prevention; metals and extraction intro; sustainability of materials
- Skills: connect chemistry to real systems (water cycle, rusting, alloys); evaluate material lifecycles qualitatively
- Unlock: applied-chemistry assessment at 80%+

## L10 — Mastery
- Concepts: synthesis across inorganic, physical, and organic topics; qualitative analysis logic; data interpretation
- Skills: multi-topic problems; read real product labels/datasheets critically; plan a safe investigation on paper
- Unlock: comprehensive exam at 85%+ and capstone explain-it-to-a-friend presentation
