# Digital Literacy Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level. Generic categories — no invented URLs.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Free touch-typing tutor sites/games with progress tracking (kid-friendly options preferred)
- File-management tutorials and printable folder-organization exercises
- "How the internet works" explainer videos (browser, URL, server, Wi-Fi)
- Guided scavenger-hunt worksheets for browser skills

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Search-skills lesson packs: query operators, keyword strategy, source-evaluation checklists (CRAAP-style)
- Email-writing guides with etiquette examples for different audiences
- Password/passphrase strength explainers + phishing-quiz sites (interactive red-flag spotting)
- Digital-footprint audit worksheets

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Fact-checking primers and lateral-reading curricula (media-literacy-org style)
- Reverse-image-search and metadata tutorials; deepfake-spotting guides
- Productivity-tool official tutorials: docs, sheets, slides, sharing permissions
- Creative-commons and copyright explainer resources for kids/teens
- AI-literacy guides: how LLMs work, hallucination demos, prompt-practice exercises
- Personal-digital-hygiene checklists: backups, updates, 2FA, footprint audits
