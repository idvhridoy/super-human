# Digital Literacy Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level.
> Foundational practical domain: typing (L1) supports coding; research skills (L3) and safety (L4) gate all unsupervised internet use.

## L0 — Device & File Basics
- Concepts: what a computer/phone is, files vs folders vs apps, storage locations, saving/renaming/moving, recycle bin, charging and shutdown hygiene
- Skills: create/rename/move/delete files, find a saved file, take a screenshot
- Unlock: organize a messy folder correctly (sort 20 files by type) + retrieve 5 items on demand

## L1 — Typing & Input
- Concepts: home row, touch typing posture, punctuation/symbols, common shortcuts (copy/paste/undo/save/find, tab navigation)
- Skills: touch-type without looking; use 10 core shortcuts
- Unlock: sustained 25+ WPM at 95%+ accuracy on a standard typing test (higher target if age/aptitude supports)

## L2 — Browser & Internet Basics
- Concepts: URLs and domains, tabs/bookmarks, downloads and where they go, history, what a search engine is, Wi-Fi basics
- Skills: navigate by URL vs search, manage bookmarks, find a downloaded file, judge a site by its domain
- Unlock: complete a 5-task scavenger hunt (find X, save it, revisit it via bookmark) with no wrong turns

## L3 — Research Skills
- Concepts: query phrasing, keywords vs questions, operators (quotes, minus, site:), evaluating sources (author, date, evidence), citing a source
- Skills: form 3 different queries for one question; pick the 2 most credible results and justify why
- Unlock: mini-research task answered from 2+ credible sources, cited, with explanation of source choice

## L4 — Online Safety & Privacy
- Concepts: strong passwords and passphrases, 2FA concept, phishing red flags, personal information rules (never share full name+address+school+photos publicly), stranger-contact protocol, scam patterns
- Skills: craft/memorize a strong passphrase, spot phishing emails/messages, apply the "ask a human first" rule reflexively
- Unlock: pass a 10-scenario safety test (phishing, stranger DM, prize scam, password reuse) at 100% — this level gates independent browsing

## L5 — Communication & Digital Footprint (intermediate milestone)
- Concepts: email anatomy (to/cc/subject/body), tone online, permanence of posts, digital footprint, audience collapse
- Skills: write a proper email, reply vs reply-all correctly, audit own footprint
- Unlock: draft 3 correct emails for different audiences + a written map of own digital footprint

## L6 — Media Literacy: Spotting Misinformation
- Concepts: clickbait, edited/fake images and video, AI-generated content, sponsored content vs news, emotional manipulation, lateral reading
- Skills: reverse-search an image, check a claim against 2 independent sources, identify paid/affiliate content
- Unlock: score 8/10 on a mixed real-vs-fake content test + write a debunk of one fake claim with evidence

## L7 — Productivity Tools
- Concepts: documents (formatting, headings), spreadsheets (cells, formulas, sorting), presentations (structure over decoration), cloud storage and sharing permissions
- Skills: produce a formatted doc, a working spreadsheet with formulas, a 5-slide deck
- Unlock: one polished artifact in each of doc/sheet/slides meeting a quality rubric

## L8 — Digital Citizenship & Rules of the Road
- Concepts: copyright and fair use basics, licensing (Creative Commons), plagiarism, age restrictions on platforms and why they exist, netiquette in shared spaces
- Skills: correctly attribute reused content, identify whether content is licensed for reuse, explain platform age rules
- Unlock: correctly licensed/attributed media project + written summary of platform age-restriction rules

## L9 — AI-Tool Fluency
- Concepts: what LLMs/chatbots are and aren't, prompting basics (context, constraints, examples), hallucination and verification, privacy with AI (never input personal data), AI vs human work honesty
- Skills: write effective prompts for research/learning, verify AI claims against sources, know when NOT to use AI
- Unlock: AI-assisted research task completed with every AI claim verified and flagged where wrong + written list of 5 "never use AI for" situations

## L10 — Mastery
- Concepts: evaluating new tools independently, personal digital-hygiene system (passwords, backups, updates, footprint), threat awareness (account takeover, data brokers)
- Skills: build and document a personal digital-hygiene routine; teach a peer/family member one safety lesson
- Unlock: capstone — documented personal digital-safety system + teach-back session + final mixed assessment at 90%+
