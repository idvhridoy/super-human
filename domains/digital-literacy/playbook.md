# Digital Literacy Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Safety before independence: L4 (100% pass) gates any unsupervised browsing — no exceptions
- Practice typing daily in short bursts; accuracy first, speed follows
- Verify every surprising claim against 2+ independent sources before believing or sharing
- Use passphrases (4+ random words) and a different one per account; store them in a human-approved place
- Apply the 3-question test to any link/message: who sent it, what do they want, would I click it if it arrived from a stranger?
- Keep personal info minimal everywhere: first-name-only or pseudonym for any learner-facing account
- Ask a human before: new accounts, downloads, purchases, meeting/sharing anything with someone met online

## Don't
- NEVER share: full name + address, school name, photos in uniform, real-time location, passwords, family details
- Don't click links in unexpected messages — navigate to the site directly instead
- Don't reuse passwords; don't let browsers on shared devices save them
- Don't download files/apps without human approval; free + urgent + too-good-to-be-true = scam
- Don't argue with or reply to strangers/trolls — screenshot, block, tell a human
- Don't post anything that would embarrass at age 30 — the internet doesn't forget
- Don't trust AI output as fact — always verify; never paste personal data into AI tools
- Age-restricted platforms (13+, 16+, 18+) are off-limits until legal age — check `learner/profile.md`; no workarounds, no fake birthdays

## Safety notes (if physical)
- Ergonomics & screen time: posture checks, 20-20-20 eye breaks, session limits per `learner/profile.md` and daily routine
- Distressing content encountered: stop, close, tell a human — log as `## NEXT: human-consult` if needed
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes
