# Agriculture Curriculum

> Maintained by `@domain-mentor`. The full L0→L10 path; each level lists skills, theory, and the assessment that unlocks the next level. Tools and any chemicals always used under adult supervision.

## L0 — Foundations
- What plants need: sun, water, air, nutrients, space
- Local seasons & climate calendar (Bangladesh: pre-monsoon, monsoon, dry)
- Assessment: name the 5 plant needs + describe the local growing seasons

## L1 — Soil Basics
- Soil types: sandy, clay, loam — feel/jar test
- Compost vs. fertilizer, soil pH concept
- Assessment: identify soil type on-site by feel test

## L2 — Seeds & Germination
- Seed anatomy, germination conditions, depth/spacing rules
- Sprout 3 fast seeds (mung bean, mustard, coriander) in pots
- Assessment: 2/3 seeds germinated and cared for 2 weeks

## L3 — Planting a First Bed
- Bed prep: clearing, loosening, amending with compost
- Sow a small bed or container set of easy crops (radish, spinach, chili)
- Assessment: bed planted to plan; thinning done correctly

## L4 — Garden Care
- Watering discipline (morning, root-zone), mulching
- Weeding, pest scouting (identify before acting — no chemicals without human approval)
- Assessment: 4-week care log, plants alive and mulched

## L5 — Compost & Soil Health — Intermediate milestone
- Build a compost pile/bin: greens/browns ratio, turning schedule
- Vermicompost or leaf-mold option
- Assessment: active compost pile maintained 6 weeks + finished compost used

## L6 — Irrigation & Water Management
- Hand watering vs. drip/bottle irrigation; drainage fixes
- Rainwater collection basics
- Assessment: build and use a simple bottle-drip system on 3 plants

## L7 — Crop Cycles & Planning
- Succession planting, rotation basics, companion planting (e.g., marigold borders)
- Seasonal planting calendar for the home plot
- Assessment: write a 2-season planting plan

## L8 — First Real Harvest
- Grow one crop seed-to-harvest; weigh/log yield
- Seed saving from open-pollinated crop
- Assessment: harvest logged with yield; seeds saved and labeled

## L9 — Small-Scale Yield Management
- Multi-crop bed design, continuous harvest planning
- Basic food preservation: drying, simple storage
- Assessment: 3-month continuous-harvest record

## L10 — Mastery
- Manage a full-season home garden plan independently
- Teach one full grow cycle to a beginner
- Assessment: season review report + taught session
