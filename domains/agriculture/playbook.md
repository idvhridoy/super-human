# Agriculture Playbook

> Do/don't list maintained by `@domain-mentor` from session evidence.

## Do
- Water in the morning at the root zone, not on leaves
- Keep a garden journal: sowing dates, weather, observations — it becomes the best teacher
- Scout pests before acting; hand-pick or water-spray first, escalate only with human approval
- Mulch every bed — moisture, weeds, and soil life all improve
- Label everything sown: crop, variety, date

## Don't
- No chemical pesticides/fertilizers without explicit human approval — ever
- Don't sow out of season; check the local calendar first
- Don't overwater — soggy soil kills more seedlings than drought
- Don't let the learner use tools alone at early levels — see safety

## Safety notes (physical)
- Tool safety: hand trowel/fork only at L1–L4 under supervision; hoe/spade introduced with a supervised demo; no machetes, powered equipment, or ladders — adult-only
- Wash hands after soil work; cover cuts; tetanus awareness on file with guardian
- Sun/heat: hat, water bottle, sessions ≤ 60–90 min in heat; stop on dizziness
- Allergies/stings: check `learner/health.md` before outdoor sessions
- `@safety-guardian` may append vetoes.
