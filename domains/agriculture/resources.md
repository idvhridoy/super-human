# Agriculture Resources

> Curated by `@knowledge-librarian`, ordered by level.

## L0–L2 (foundations)
- Beginner gardening books for young growers (soil, seeds, seasons)
- Local agricultural extension leaflets on seasonal crops
- Seed-germination experiment guides (jar/bag methods)
- Illustrated soil-type and compost intro charts

## L3–L5 (intermediate)
- Container and small-bed planting guides for the local climate
- Home composting manuals (pile, bin, vermicompost)
- Organic pest-identification charts and hand-control methods
- Mulching and watering technique videos

## L6–L10 (advanced)
- Crop rotation and companion-planting reference tables
- Bottle-drip and rainwater-harvest DIY plans
- Seed-saving guides per crop
- Local-market crop calendars and small-plot yield records templates
